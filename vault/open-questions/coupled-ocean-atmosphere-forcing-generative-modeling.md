title: Coupled Ocean-Atmosphere Forcing in Generative Modeling
---

**Background:** Machine learning models for weather and climate prediction rely heavily on simplified external forcing representations or lack fully coupled ocean-atmosphere interactions.

**Question / Future Work:** Expanding generative assimilation and prediction frameworks to incorporate fully coupled ocean dynamics, as well as dynamic greenhouse gas and aerosol forcings beyond prescribed sea surface temperatures, remains an important direction for future research.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for extending data-driven weather and climate frameworks to accurately model long-term feedback loops and anthropogenic climate change scenarios.

**Evidence:** Future work will aim to directly represent forcing-response relationships in a coupled modeling framework.

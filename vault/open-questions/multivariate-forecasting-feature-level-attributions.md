title: Multivariate and Feature-Level Forecasting Explanations
---

**Background:** Post-hoc explainability methods for time series forecasting must account for multivariate interactions and feature-level dependencies alongside temporal lag attributions.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend time series explanation frameworks from univariate lag-level attribution to comprehensive multivariate forecasting scenarios by combining lag-level attribution with fine-grained feature-level explanations.

**Why It Matters:** Real-world industrial and financial forecasting tasks are predominantly multivariate, requiring explanations that jointly resolve both when past events matter (lags) and which variables matter (features).

**Evidence:** Future work will extend the approach to multivariate forecasting, combine lag-level attribution with feature-level explanations, and evaluate interpretability through user-centered studies.

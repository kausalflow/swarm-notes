title: Frequency-Dependent Anisotropic PTA Extensions
---

**Background:** Pulsar timing array analyses conventionally model the stochastic gravitational wave background as isotropic, despite the physical inevitability of anisotropy from finite supermassive black hole binary populations.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending directional anisotropic sensitivity frameworks to accommodate general frequency-dependent angular power spectra, relaxing weak-signal and Earth-term limitations, and systematically handling basis truncation artifacts across real pulsar timing array datasets remain important directions for future work.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for robustly interpreting real PTA data without biases from truncation or unmodeled propagation effects.

**Evidence:** Several directions follow naturally. Because the background’s anisotropy is expected to grow with frequency and a hotspot may be produced by a single loud binary or by several quieter binaries close together on the sky, directional sensitivity curves provide a bridge between stochastic-background and continuous-wave searches... We have worked throughout in the weak-signal, Earth-term limit... relaxing these assumptions is left to future work.

title: Binary Versus Rotational Variability
---

**Background:** Photometric searches for compact binaries via blue colors and short-period variability admit an intrinsically heterogeneous population of objects, including both binaries and single variable stars.

**Question / Future Work:** Determining whether short-period photometric variability in compact blue stars arises from orbital motion (such as ellipsoidal modulation or eclipses) or from single-star phenomena (such as rotation of magnetic or chemically inhomogeneous white dwarfs and stellar pulsations) remains ambiguous without time-resolved spectroscopy and multiwavelength follow-up.

**Why It Matters:** Critical for correctly interpreting photometric variability signatures, avoiding contamination in gravitational-wave source catalogs, and accurately identifying true compact binaries.

title: Connections Between Outcome-Conditional and Auto-Calibration
---

**Background:** Outcome-conditional recalibration ensures that probabilistic predictions are calibrated when restricted to user-defined regions of the outcome space, but existing theoretical connections between outcome-conditional calibration and auto-calibration (distribution calibration) remain insufficiently explored.

**Question / Future Work:** Further research is needed to formally clarify and establish the theoretical connections between outcome-conditional calibration and auto-calibration (distribution calibration), particularly examining whether strong conditional calibration notions theoretically subsume or align with outcome-specific recalibration properties.

**Why It Matters:** Clarifying these theoretical relationships bridges disparate hierarchies of calibration concepts in regression and helps determine whether general conditional calibration frameworks can automatically guarantee reliable tail and event-of-interest forecasting without specialized localized recalibration.

**Evidence:** Further work is therefore needed to clarify the connections between outcome-conditional calibration and auto-calibration.

title: Weaker Regularity Conditions for Local Approximation
---

**Background:** Establishing rigorous theoretical guarantees for divide-and-conquer and approximation methods in time-series models often requires imposing strict regularity and stability conditions.

**Question / Future Work:** Establish the local approximation results under less restrictive regularity conditions, particularly by deriving rather than imposing the normalising-constant and consensus-stability conditions.

**Why It Matters:** Removing strong assumptions enhances the general applicability and mathematical rigor of approximation error bounds in spectral and distributed MCMC methods.

**Evidence:** an important direction is to establish the local approximation results under less restrictive regularity conditions, particularly by deriving rather than imposing the normalising-constant and consensus-stability conditions.

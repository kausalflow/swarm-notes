title: Multimodal Clinical Time-Series Forecasting
---

**Background:** Longitudinal clinical time-series cohorts are frequently pervasively incomplete and irregularly sampled across heterogeneous medical modalities.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigating the extension of coupled oscillatory state-space architectures to handle continuous-time, irregularly sampled multi-rate observations without relying on heuristic modality imputation or zero-filling.

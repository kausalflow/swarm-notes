title: Production-Scale Data Mixtures Evaluation
---

**Background:** Time series foundation models are pre-trained on diverse domains, but the persistence of synthetic data compute savings in production-scale multi-source data mixtures remains unexplored.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work needs to evaluate whether the compute savings achieved via synthetic data distillation persist at the scale and data mixtures of production time series foundation model pre-training runs, where synthetic data forms only a part of the overall corpus.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for establishing the practical utility of data distillation techniques in real-world large-scale foundation model pre-training pipelines that blend real and synthetic data.

**Evidence:** whether that compute saving persists at the scale and data mixtures of production TSFM pre-training runs, where synthetic data is only part of the corpus

title: Disentangling Breadth from Anticipation in Forecasting
---

**Background:** Evaluating automated research idea forecasting requires resolving ambiguity between genuine anticipation of scientific discoveries and the production of broad, underspecified forecasts that easily match subsequent literature.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine the extent to which higher realization scores achieved by certain models are driven by precise anticipation of future innovations versus intrinsic forecast breadth and vagueness, while controlling for matching threshold sensitivities and judge biases.

**Why It Matters:** This question is crucial for advancing scientific forecasting benchmarks, as it addresses the fundamental validity of realization metrics and prevents models from gaming evaluation systems through overly broad or trivial statements.

**Evidence:** Qwen2.5’s high scores leave open whether its forecasts anticipate future work more precisely or state broader ideas that more papers can satisfy. We examine forecast breadth and matching opportunities to assess this ambiguity, while keeping their association separate from causal evidence.

title: Physically Grounded Inverter Adjacency Modeling
---

**Background:** Multimodal machine learning architectures for photovoltaic power generation forecasting integrate various meteorological and telemetry data streams to predict short-term power outputs.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research directions include investigating the integration of external vendor irradiance products to extend forecast accuracy across the full horizon range, utilizing true geographic coordinates of individual inverters to establish a physically grounded adjacency prior rather than relying on a fully data-driven graph structure, and analyzing performance gaps and transition-window calibration asymmetries between morning and evening periods.

**Why It Matters:** Incorporating physical coordinates and external weather products addresses fundamental limitations in data-driven spatial modeling for renewable energy, directly enhancing the interpretability and robustness of graph-based forecasting frameworks.

title: Scalable Counterfactual Time Series Explanation
---

**Background:** Post-hoc explanation methods for multivariate time-series models often incur high computational overhead when performing repeated counterfactual interventions in learned latent causal spaces.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and mitigate the computational bottlenecks associated with repeated counterfactual interventions in latent causal spaces for time series explanation, while extending necessity-aware frameworks to broader and more complex temporal architectures.

**Why It Matters:** Efficiency and scalability of counterfactual intervention-based explanations remain key bottlenecks for real-time and large-scale industrial deployment in domains like healthcare and finance.

**Evidence:** A limitation is the computational cost of repeated counterfactual interventions, which we leave to future work together with extensions to broader temporal architectures.

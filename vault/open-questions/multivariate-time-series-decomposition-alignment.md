title: Multivariate Time Series IMF Alignment
---

**Background:** Counterfactual explanation generation methods for time series data often perturb raw signal features or latent spaces, which can destroy temporal structure and produce physically implausible waveforms.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend decomposition-based counterfactual frameworks to multivariate time series data by developing principled alignment strategies for intrinsic mode functions across multiple channels.

**Why It Matters:** Multivariate time series are ubiquitous in real-world applications such as industrial monitoring and healthcare, making channel alignment in decomposition spaces a critical architectural hurdle.

**Evidence:** The method is also currently limited to univariate series, extending to multivariate data requires a principled way to align IMFs across channels.

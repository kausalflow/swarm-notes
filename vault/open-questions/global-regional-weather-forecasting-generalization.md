title: Global-Regional Weather Forecasting Generalization
---

**Background:** Pretrained global weather models provide efficient large-scale atmospheric forecasts but lack kilometer-scale regional resolution, motivating the development of deep learning architectures that couple global guidance with high-resolution regional prediction.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how diverse pretrained global weather models and regional datasets can be combined under universal architectures to enable cross-region generalization, driver-invariant transferability, and reliable handling of extreme localized phenomena such as abrupt storm transitions.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding how to generalize data-driven weather forecasting models across varying regional domains and upstream global drivers without retraining is critical for practical, operational deployment.

**Evidence:** Evaluation across additional regions and forecasting systems would help establish broader applicability.

title: Hyperbolic Geometry for Time Series Hierarchies
---

**Background:** Multi-scale time series representations exhibit natural parent-child hierarchies that can be modeled using hyperbolic geometry, but existing frameworks rely heavily on heuristics rather than principled non-Euclidean geometric priors.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the theoretical and empirical generalization of hyperbolic multi-scale representation spaces beyond temporal downsampling hierarchies, exploring how alternative geometric structures or learned adaptive hierarchies can further enhance long-term time series forecasting performance across diverse non-stationary regimes.

**Why It Matters:** Bridging non-Euclidean representation learning with multi-scale time series dynamics is an emerging research direction that requires deeper investigation into how geometric inductive biases interact with temporal structures.

**Evidence:** These results suggest that explicitly modeling the abstraction hierarchy serves as a useful inductive bias that improves multi-scale time series forecasting.

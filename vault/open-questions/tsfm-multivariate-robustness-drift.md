title: Multivariate and Robustness Benchmarking for TSFMs
---

**Background:** Time series foundation models (TSFMs) are increasingly deployed for zero-shot forecasting across diverse domains, but their robustness under missing data, irregular sampling, and concept drift remains underexplored.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research will focus on extending current zero-shot time series foundation model benchmarks to multivariate forecasting settings as well as evaluating their robustness under data quality challenges such as missing values, irregularly sampled observations, and distribution shifts (concept drift).

**Why It Matters:** Evaluating robustness under real-world data imperfections and multivariate dependencies is critical for deploying foundation models safely in industrial pipelines.

**Evidence:** Future work includes extending the benchmark to multivariate forecasting and to robustness under missing data, irregular sampling, and concept drift.

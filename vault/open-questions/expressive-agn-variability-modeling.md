title: Expressive AGN Variability Modeling
---

**Background:** Reverberation mapping of active galactic nuclei relies on simple parametric models like damped random walks and uniform priors, which struggle with light curve complexity, aliasing peaks, and high-dimensional parameter spaces.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate more expressive models for continuum and response variability—such as time-varying response noise processes, structured Fe II contamination correction, or non-stationary noise models—to better capture non-call-and-response behaviors and improve lag recovery accuracy for Mg II and C IV emission lines.

**Why It Matters:** Model misspecification in AGN light curves leads to underestimated reverberation fractions and biased lag recoveries, making advanced variability modeling crucial for large-scale surveys.

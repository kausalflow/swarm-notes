title: Operational Forecasting and Station Transfer
---

**Background:** Short-range hindcasting of wave spectra using machine learning models often relies on reanalysis data and historical buoy records, leaving operational forecasting performance and generalization to independent stations largely unexplored.

**Question / Future Work:** Evaluate the proposed data-driven and physics-guided spectral wave hindcasting framework using operational forecasting conditions (with winds available only at issue time and realistic buoy data latency) as well as independent stations not included during training.

**Why It Matters:** Determines whether local data-driven and physics-guided wave spectrum models can successfully transition from retrospective hindcasting to operational forecasting environments.

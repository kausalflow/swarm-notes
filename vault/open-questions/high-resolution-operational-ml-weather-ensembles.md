**Background:** Machine-learning ensemble weather forecasting models currently operate at coarse spatial resolutions, limiting their applicability to higher-resolution operational standards.

**Question / Future Work:** Scaling machine-learning ensemble weather forecasting systems to higher spatial resolutions (such as 0.25 degrees) while maintaining computational efficiency and incorporating operational initial states remains an important direction for future investigation.

**Why It Matters:** Advancing weather forecasting models to higher resolutions is crucial for capturing fine-scale atmospheric phenomena and bridging the gap between experimental AI models and operational meteorology.

**Evidence:** Applying the same recipe at 0.25 degree and with operational initial states is the next step.

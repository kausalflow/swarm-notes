title: Efficient Probabilistic Time Series Forecasting
---

**Background:** Probabilistic time series forecasting requires balancing predictive accuracy, computational efficiency, and interpretability through trend-seasonal decomposition.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how to further improve the computational efficiency and forecasting fidelity of variational autoencoder frameworks for probabilistic time series forecasting, particularly when extending trend-seasonal decomposition techniques to handle extremely high-dimensional or non-stationary domains without heavy memory overhead.

**Why It Matters:** This addresses the persistent quality-computation trade-off in generative probabilistic forecasting models.

**Evidence:** Consequently, developing a computationally efficient framework that accurately captures both temporal dynamics for probabilistic forecasting remains an open challenge.

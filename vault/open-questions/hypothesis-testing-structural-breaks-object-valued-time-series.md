title: Testing Long Memory and Breaks
---

**Background:** Object-valued time series defined on metric spaces lack the linear structure required by traditional autocovariance-based methods, necessitating intrinsic metric-space alternatives for modeling long-range dependence.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop formal hypothesis testing procedures to determine whether an object-valued time series exhibits genuine long-range dependence versus short-memory dynamics or persistence induced by structural breaks.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for statistical inference and model validation in non-Euclidean time series analysis, as failing to distinguish long memory from structural instability leads to severe forecasting and inferential errors.

**Evidence:** 1) Develop a hypothesis test for examining whether an object-valued time series exhibits long memory. 2) Distinguish genuine long memory from persistence induced by structural breaks

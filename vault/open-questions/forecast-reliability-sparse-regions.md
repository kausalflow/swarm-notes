title: Air Quality Forecast Reliability in Data-Sparse Regions
---

**Background:** Operational air pollution forecasts rely on coarse grids that hide local exposure variations and are typically evaluated against sparse monitoring networks.

**Question / Future Work:** Quantifying forecast reliability and evaluating air pollution predictions in regions lacking comparable live monitoring networks remains a key challenge due to the propagation and potential amplification of regional biases and errors in high-resolution particulate matter estimates.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for ensuring global equity and trustworthiness of air-quality warning systems in data-sparse regions, particularly across the Global South.

**Evidence:** Although we quantitatively evaluate forecasts against observations in countries with live monitoring networks, forecast reliability remains uncertain in regions without comparable measurements.

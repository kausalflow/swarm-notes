title: Probabilistic and Multi-Step Direction-Aware Forecasting
---

**Background:** Time series forecasting models are predominantly optimized using point-wise magnitude losses like Mean Squared Error (MSE), which fail to explicitly capture the direction of change.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work should explore masking or confidence-weighting near-flat windows where directional signals are undefined or negligible, and extend the proposed direction-aware loss function to probabilistic, multi-step forecasting frameworks.

**Why It Matters:** Extending direction-aware losses to probabilistic and multi-step forecasting addresses a critical limitation in decision-driven applications where uncertainty estimation and multi-horizon trajectory alignment are essential.

**Evidence:** Future directions include masking or confidence-weighting near-flat windows and extending it to probabilistic, multi-step forecasting.

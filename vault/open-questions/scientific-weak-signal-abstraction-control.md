title: Improving Scientific Weak-Signal Abstraction-Level Control
---

**Background:** Predicting scientific weak signals requires recovering early precursors of mature research topics from temporally constrained literature corpora, a task that currently suffers from poor alignment and high error rates across state-in-the-art language and research agent models.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and develop methods that enhance semantic alignment, abstraction-level control, and accurate precursor identification in scientific foresight models, addressing current limitations where models struggle to distinguish genuine precursors from adjacent look-alikes or broad domain themes.

**Why It Matters:** This problem is central to overcoming the current performance ceiling in machine learning systems applied to automated scientific discovery and foresight, shifting the focus from mere information retrieval to true causal or structural precursor reasoning.

**Evidence:** Scientific foresight therefore requires precise precursor identification beyond simply generating relevant scientific content. Progress on BackTrend will depend less on model scale or evidence budget than on abstraction-level control.

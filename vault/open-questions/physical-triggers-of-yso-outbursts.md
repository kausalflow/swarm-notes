title: Physical Triggers of YSO Outbursts
---

**Background:** The initial physical triggers of eruptive accretion events and outbursts in young stellar objects remain poorly constrained due to a general lack of high-cadence spectroscopic coverage during the early rising phases of outbursts.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine the precise physical triggers responsible for accretion outbursts in young stellar objects by securing high-cadence spectroscopic monitoring during the initial rising phases of future eruptions.

**Why It Matters:** Identifying the physical mechanism responsible for triggering episodic accretion bursts remains a critical open problem in pre-main-sequence stellar evolution.

**Evidence:** Unfortunately, spectroscopic coverage during the first 100 days of the outburst (particularly the rising phase) was lacking, limiting our ability to constrain the physical trigger of the eruption.

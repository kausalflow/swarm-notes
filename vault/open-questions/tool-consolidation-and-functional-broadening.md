title: Tool Consolidation and Functional Broadening
---

**Background:** Industrial fault diagnosis systems under shifting operating conditions often struggle to choose appropriate tool measurements and appropriately weigh conflicting evidence.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research can explore consolidating tools while broadening their functional scope to reduce the context needed to describe them, simplify tool selection, and support richer signal analysis.

**Why It Matters:** Simplifying toolspaces and contexts in agentic diagnostics reduces token costs and improves reliability during multi-turn investigations.

**Evidence:** First, consolidating tools while broadening their functionality could reduce the context needed to describe them, simplify tool selection, and support richer signal analysis.

title: Multi-Domain Neural Operator Flood Forecasting
---

**Background:** Neural operator surrogates for flood inundation forecasting are typically trained and evaluated on single geographical domains or synthetic event libraries, limiting their broader operational deployment.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work involves expanding neural-operator flood models to operate across multiple cities through multi-domain training and domain adaptation techniques, while refining river networks, incorporating more extreme historical scenarios, and coupling the surrogate with real-time operational weather and stage forecasts.

**Why It Matters:** Scaling neural operator surrogates across multiple geographic domains and coupling them directly with operational meteorological forecasts are critical steps for deploying reliable, region-wide early warning systems.

**Evidence:** Future work will extend the approach to several cities through multi-domain training and domain adaptation, refine the mesh near the rivers, enrich the training set with more extreme events, and couple the surrogate with operational stage and rain forecasts.

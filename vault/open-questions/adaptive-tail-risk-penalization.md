title: Adaptive Tail Risk Penalization
---

**Background:** Decentralized finance (DeFi) yield trading protocols feature complex multi-asset dynamics, but existing quantitative forecasting models struggle to accurately handle non-Gaussian data distributions and heavy-tailed volatility spikes.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research needs to explore adaptive risk aversion coefficients and dynamic penalty weighting mechanisms that automatically adjust to changing market regimes across different decentralized yield asset classes.

**Why It Matters:** Dynamic tuning of asymmetric penalty weights is critical for generalizing tail-risk-aware models across diverse and evolving cryptocurrency market conditions.

**Evidence:** The optimal $\alpha$ value varies by model architecture but consistently falls within the $1.0$–$5.0$ range...

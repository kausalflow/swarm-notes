title: Serial Dependence Decay under Curvature
---

**Background:** Exponential smoothing is extended to time series of random objects in Hadamard spaces via geodesic exponential smoothing (GES), yielding a one-parameter online update rule and a generative innovations framework without reliance on stationary or mixing assumptions.

**Question / Future Work:** The current theoretical analysis for the decay rate of the quasi-autocovariance and level autocorrelation is established exclusively in Hilbert spaces using linearity and inner products. Identifying the exact additional geometric structure or weaker conditions under which polynomial decay-in-time rates hold for general nonpositive curvature (Hadamard) spaces remains an open problem.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the precise decay of serial dependence under curvature is crucial for extending inference and testing tools from Euclidean or Hilbert space settings to general non-Euclidean metric spaces.

**Evidence:** Second, the decay-in-time law of Corollary 4.1 is proved only in Hilbert spaces, although the quasi-autocovariance remains nonpositive in every Hadamard space by Theorem 4.2 (ii); which additional structure recovers the rate under curvature remains open.

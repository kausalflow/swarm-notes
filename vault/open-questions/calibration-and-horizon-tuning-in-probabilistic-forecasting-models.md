title: Calibration and Horizon Tuning
---

**Background:** Probabilistic time-series forecasting models must balance central accuracy, sharp uncertainty estimates, and reliable coverage across multiple horizons.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work needs to investigate calibration methods, horizon-specific hyperparameter tuning, and component selection strategies (such as mask relaxation, bottleneck dimensions, and reconstruction regularization) to improve long-horizon forecast coverage and sharpness without degrading short-horizon performance.

**Evidence:** The results identify calibration, horizon-specific tuning, and component selection as the central research priorities.

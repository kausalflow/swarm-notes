title: Real-World Time-Series Interaction Data
---

**Background:** Although large-scale streaming time-series interaction datasets combine synthetic and real-world scenarios, current construction remains heavily reliant on synthetic data generation pipelines.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend time-series interaction frameworks and data collection methodologies to a broader range of real-world operational environments, moving beyond synthetic approximations to capture more naturally occurring interaction scenarios, complex user behaviors, and domain-specific dynamics.

**Why It Matters:** Transitioning from synthetic scenarios to fully natural real-world interaction traces is essential to ensure generalizability, handle long-tail edge cases, and validate interactive time-series intelligence in safety-critical deployment domains.

**Evidence:** Although StreamTSI-34K provides large-scale time-series interaction data spanning both synthetic and real-world time series across multiple domains, the current interaction construction still relies primarily on synthetic scenarios. In practice, real-world settings involve much richer and more diverse interaction scenarios and user behaviors. Future work should therefore extend Time-Series Interaction to a broader range of real-world systems...

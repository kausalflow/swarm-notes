title: Generalizability of Adaptive Ensembles
---

**Background:** Adaptive ensemble methods in epidemic forecasting can improve predictive performance by adjusting model weights according to disease transmission regimes, but their generalizability to different outbreak contexts remains an open challenge.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work needs to evaluate how well rank-based adaptive ensemble forecasting methods generalize to other epidemiological contexts, disease settings, and varying amounts of available training data.

**Why It Matters:** Assessing the robustness and generalizability of adaptive weighting schemes ensures that public health decision support tools remain reliable when applied to emerging pathogens with different transmission characteristics.

**Evidence:** In future work we plan to investigate how well the rank-based ensemble generalises to other context and how the performance depends on the amount of training data.

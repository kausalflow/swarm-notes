title: Longer and Multivariate Time Series Captioning
---

**Background:** Time series captioning models are typically evaluated on short univariate sequences, while practical applications frequently require handling longer or multivariate time series data.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the LineupRL framework and evaluation methodology to handle longer temporal horizons as well as multivariate time series data.

**Why It Matters:** Real-world time series data is frequently multivariate and spans long durations, making scalability to these settings a crucial milestone for time series representation learning and captioning.

**Evidence:** Longer and multivariate series and captioners beyond VLMs are left to future work.

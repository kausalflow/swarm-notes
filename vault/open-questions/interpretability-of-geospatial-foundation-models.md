title: Interpretability of Geospatial Foundation Models
---

**Background:** Planetary geospatial foundation models provide multimodal representations of place that can be incorporated as covariates into epidemiological models across various global health tasks.

**Question / Future Work:** Evaluate how interpretable planetary geospatial foundation models are relative to traditional demographic and socioeconomic covariates, aiming to attribute predictive gains to specific input modalities or correlate latent embedding dimensions with known socioeconomic constructs.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the interpretability of foundation model embeddings is critical for public health adoption, ensuring that black-box representations can be audited and aligned with causal or descriptive epidemiological analyses.

**Evidence:** Another direction for future work is to compare how interpretable PDFM embeddings are relative to traditional covariates.

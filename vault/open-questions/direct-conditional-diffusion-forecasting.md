title: Direct Conditional Diffusion Training
---

**Background:** Generative diffusion models can be adapted for spatiotemporal epidemic forecasting by treating epidemic seasons as structured grids and applying conditioning through inpainting techniques.

**Question / Future Work:** Future extensions of diffusion-based epidemic forecasting frameworks can explore training conditional diffusion models directly for forecasting tasks rather than relying exclusively on unconditional training followed by generation-time conditioning or inpainting.

**Why It Matters:** Transitioning from generation-time inpainting to direct conditional diffusion training is a key architectural frontier for improving task-specific predictive accuracy and reducing potential inefficiencies in spatiotemporal disease forecasting.

**Evidence:** Future extensions could improve forecasting performance, at the expense of flexibility, by training conditional diffusion models directly for forecasting rather than conditioning during generation.

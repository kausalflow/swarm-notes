title: Uncertainty-Aware Object Placement Refinement
---

**Background:** Precise placement of objects during egocentric hand-object interaction forecasting remains challenging due to sensitivity to intent conditions and spatial constraints.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work needs to model uncertainty in predicted intention attributes and jointly refine spatial goals with contact and support constraints to improve placement accuracy and physical consistency.

**Why It Matters:** Placement accuracy is a major bottleneck in interactive AR/VR systems where objects must be set down accurately on supporting surfaces, making robust modeling of uncertainty and joint spatial-contact refinement technically critical.

**Evidence:** Precise placement remains a substantial challenge. Although predicted intentions improve Place-Hit from 18.94% to 44.93%, the 95.59% achieved under oracle conditioning reveals a substantial performance gap. This gap suggests that precise placement is highly sensitive to inaccuracies in the inferred intention conditions. Future work could model uncertainty in predicted intention attributes and jointly refine spatial goals with contact and support constraints to improve placement accuracy and physical consistency.

title: Exogenous-Driven Pattern Modeling
---

**Background:** Exogenous-aware ride-hailing time series forecasting relies heavily on future-known external variables, yet current models struggle to capture disturbance-induced pattern changes under complex external contexts.

**Question / Future Work:** Design advanced exogenous-aware forecasting architectures that can natively process future-known categorical semantics, sparse large-scale events, and heterogeneous spatial-temporal responses to external disturbances.

**Why It Matters:** Essential for robust decision-making under weather anomalies, public holidays, and large-scale cultural or sporting events.

**Evidence:** However, current exogenous-aware models still struggle to fully capture disturbance-induced pattern changes under complex external contexts.

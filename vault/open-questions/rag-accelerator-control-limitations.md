title: RAG Limitations in Accelerator Control
---

**Background:** Retrieval-augmented generation frameworks applied to particle accelerator control systems must cope with inconsistent naming conventions across subsystems, variability in operator logbook documentation quality, and imperfect time synchronization between disparate operational data sources.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and address current operational bottlenecks including inconsistent naming conventions across subsystems, variability in logbook documentation quality, and imperfect time synchronization between heterogeneous data sources, alongside evaluating retrieval performance and latency under realistic operator queries.

**Why It Matters:** Addressing these limitations is crucial for ensuring the reliability, accuracy, and real-time usability of AI-assisted decision support tools in safety-critical accelerator environments.

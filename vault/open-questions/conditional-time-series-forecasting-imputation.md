title: Conditional Forecasting and Imputation
---

**Background:** Multivariate time-series generation is typically approached via unconditional modeling frameworks, whereas many practical applications require conditional generation tasks such as forecasting and imputation.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending the wavelet flow matching framework from unconditional generation to conditional time-series tasks, including forecasting, imputation, and missing data completion, represents a crucial direction for future work.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for broadening the applicability of wavelet-domain flow matching models to standard predictive and operational time-series problems.

**Evidence:** Adapting the decomposition to the scale structure of individual datasets and extending the framework to conditional tasks such as forecasting and imputation are natural directions for future work.

title: Cross-Market Validation of MFMJD
---

**Background:** Validating and extending parameter estimation frameworks across multiple financial markets and diverse asset classes is necessary to establish general applicability and robustness.

**Question / Future Work:** The mixed fractional Merton jump diffusion model and its EM-MH estimation framework have thus far been demonstrated primarily on a single market index (the Helsinki Stock Index). Future work should focus on cross-market validation and testing across various asset classes to ensure the framework's broad applicability and robustness under different macroeconomic and market conditions.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for demonstrating that complex stochastic volatility and long-memory jump-diffusion models are universally applicable rather than overfitted to specific regional market dynamics.

**Evidence:** However, since the empirical analysis in this study is restricted to one market data set \"the Helsinki Stock Index\". The researchers suggest a further research with Validation across multiple financial markets and asset classes to further strengthen the general applicability and robustness of the framework.

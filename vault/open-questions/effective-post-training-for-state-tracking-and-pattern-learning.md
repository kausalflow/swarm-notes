title: Effective Post-Training for Long-Context Capabilities
---

**Background:** Long-context language models undergo post-training techniques designed to improve reasoning, yet their impact on complex long-context capabilities such as state tracking and pattern learning remains unverified.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate effective post-training techniques to improve state tracking and pattern learning in long-context language models, addressing the empirical finding that current reasoning-oriented post-training can compromise these fundamental long-context capabilities.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for guiding the design of future alignment and post-training algorithms so that reasoning enhancements do not degrade native long-context utilization and representation.

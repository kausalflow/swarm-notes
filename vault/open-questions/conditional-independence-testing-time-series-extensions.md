title: Extensions of Time Series Conditional Independence Testing
---

**Background:** Conditional independence testing in time series settings faces theoretical and computational challenges due to temporal dependence, potential time-varying conditional laws, and high-dimensional feature spaces.

**Question / Future Work:** Explore the extension of conditional independence testing frameworks and the Generalised Temporal Covariance Measure (GTCM) to handle more complex scenarios, such as high-dimensional conditioning variables, unmeasured confounding, or fully nonstationary time series where stability assumptions might be relaxed further.

**Why It Matters:** Extending conditional independence testing beyond current stationarity and low-dimensional conditioning constraints is critical for broad applicability in real-world complex systems like climate science and macroeconomics.

**Evidence:** While much of the literature in time series analysis assumes stationarity, which would preclude this latter possibility, in many settings, such a restriction would be implausible.

title: Weight-Space Diagnostics for Unknown Dynamics
---

**Background:** Machine learning models trained across populations of parameters are increasingly studied using weight-space representations, but validating these diagnostics on complex, uncharacterized physical systems remains a key challenge.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research directions involve extending weight-space diagnostic methods and joint-embedding hypernetwork architectures like JEPAWG to complex physical systems whose underlying dynamics are not yet fully understood, in order to identify uncharacterized structural transitions or guide data collection.

**Why It Matters:** Extending weight-space diagnostics from systems with known ground-truth bifurcations to genuinely unknown physical regimes represents a crucial frontier for validating machine learning artifacts as scientific instruments.

**Evidence:** Having validated the weight-space response against known bifurcation structure, we see a promising prospect in applying the method to systems whose dynamics are not yet fully understood, where the weights would point to structures not yet identified, indicating where to concentrate simulation or further training rather than sweeping a parameter range at uniform cost.

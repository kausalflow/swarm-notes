title: Adaptive Balancing and Shift
---

**Background:** Masked pretraining methods for multivariate time series often rely on fixed objective weights and focus on single benchmarks without exploring broader distribution shifts or adaptive balancing strategies.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work must investigate broader types of domain shift, richer multimodal temporal settings, and adaptive balancing strategies for evidential and consistency terms during self-supervised masked pretraining.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding how to adaptively balance evidential objectives and consistency terms across diverse multimodal domains and distribution shifts is critical for scaling reliable self-supervised time-series representation learning.

**Evidence:** Future work should therefore test broader types of shift, richer multimodal settings, and adaptive balancing strategies for evidential and consistency terms.

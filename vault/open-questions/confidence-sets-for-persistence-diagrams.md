title: Confidence Sets for Persistence Diagrams
---

**Background:** Topological data analysis and time-delay embeddings are increasingly used to characterize periodicity and dynamics in irregularly-spaced time series, but statistical inference for persistence diagrams requires rigorous confidence sets.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending significance testing and confidence sets for homology generators to direct filtrations on point-cloud spaces requires bounding the bottleneck distance with the Hausdorff distance, which currently yields excessively wide confidence sets requiring tailored methods.

**Why It Matters:** Developing tight confidence sets and hypothesis testing frameworks for persistence diagrams is crucial for reliable statistical inference in topological data analysis.

**Evidence:** Initial investigations produced wide confidence sets, indicating the need for a more tailored method, which remains a future research direction.

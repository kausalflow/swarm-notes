title: Temporal Information in Causal Scoring
---

**Background:** Time-dependent dynamics in event sequences are currently evaluated primarily through negative log-likelihood scoring without explicit temporal feature incorporation, leaving open how to integrate explicit temporal timestamps and intervals into language model scoring for causal discovery.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work involves incorporating explicit temporal information, such as exact timestamps or inter-event time intervals, directly into the language model scoring mechanism to improve causal direction inference.

**Why It Matters:** Integrating explicit temporal information is technically important because many real-world event sequences depend heavily on exact timing and delays rather than purely discrete sequential ordering.

**Evidence:** Our future work will focus on incorporating temporal information into the scoring mechanism and extending this pairwise framework to recover full causal graphs.

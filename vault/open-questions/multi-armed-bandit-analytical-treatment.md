title: Analytical Treatment of Multi-Armed Bandits
---

**Background:** Multi-armed bandit algorithms driven by multi-threshold or multi-bit stochastic processes currently lack complete analytical treatment.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the stochastic-process model and analytical techniques to time-series-based decision-making frameworks for multi-armed bandit problems involving three or more arms (multi-threshold, multi-bit processes).

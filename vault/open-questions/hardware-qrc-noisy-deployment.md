title: Hardware Quantum Reservoir Forecasting
---

**Background:** Quantum reservoir computing uses simulated or hardware quantum dynamics to forecast chaotic differential equations, but evaluating its performance on physical devices remains challenging due to noise.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigating the scalability and robustness of quantum reservoir computing pipelines when deployed on actual noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) hardware devices rather than pure-state classical simulations.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding how quantum noise and hardware constraints impact chaotic reduced-order forecasting is critical for transitioning quantum machine learning models from simulation to real-world deployment.

**Evidence:** Third, the QRC used here is a pure-state simulated reservoir rather than a hardware execution or noisy quantum device.

title: System-Specific Cost Functions for Differential Embedding
---

**Background:** Numerical differentiation and attractor reconstruction from noisy time-series data require tuning parameters for sliding-mode differentiators and smoothing filters.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing residual-based costs and optimisation strategies that are better suited to particular classes of dynamical systems, and analyzing more systematically how different choices of cost function affect reconstruction quality and derivative accuracy.

**Why It Matters:** Optimizing parameter selection costs for differential embedding and denoising remains an open problem across nonlinear dynamics and signal processing applications.

**Evidence:** One is to design residual-based costs and optimisation strategies that are better suited to particular classes of dynamical systems and to analyse more systematically how different choices of cost function affect reconstruction quality and derivative accuracy.

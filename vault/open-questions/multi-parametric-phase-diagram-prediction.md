title: Multi-Parametric Phase Diagram Prediction
---

**Background:** Parameter-aware reservoir computing architectures are typically restricted to single-parameter variations, leaving multi-parametric phase diagram prediction largely unexplored.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend parameter-aware reservoir computing frameworks to simultaneously handle variations in multiple control parameters, such as both the bifurcation parameter and the coupling strength, to enable the prediction of full multi-parameter phase diagrams.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for modeling complex physical systems where multiple control parameters govern stability and phase transitions simultaneously.

**Evidence:** First, extending the PARC framework to simultaneously handle variations in both the bifurcation parameter μ and the coupling strength ε would enable prediction of full two-parameter phase diagrams [31].

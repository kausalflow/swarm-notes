**Background:** Spatio-temporal urban traffic data forecasting involves complex, non-stationary patterns that require coordinating temporal, spectral, spatial, and higher-order topological dependencies.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research directions include exploring more efficient routing strategies, incorporating multi-source external factors, and developing more interpretable expert specialization for large-scale spatio-temporal data mining.

**Why It Matters:** Highlights concrete methodological directions for advancing mixture-of-experts and LLM-based spatio-temporal forecasting architectures beyond current limitations.

**Evidence:** Future work will explore more efficient routing strategies, the incorporation of multi-source external factors, and more interpretable expert specialization for large-scale spatio-temporal data mining.

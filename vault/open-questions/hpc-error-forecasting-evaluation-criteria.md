title: Standardized Evaluation Criteria for HPC Error Forecasting
---

**Background:** Predictive maintenance and proactive failure intervention in high-performance computing (HPC) rely heavily on accurate error forecasting, yet standardized performance metrics and quantitative usefulness thresholds for error-level forecasting remain undefined.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine appropriate evaluation criteria, standardized performance metrics, and actionable usefulness thresholds for error-level time series forecasting in high-performance computing systems.

**Why It Matters:** Establishing rigorous and standardized performance benchmarks is critical to determine whether forecasting models provide practical utility for real-world HPC fault avoidance and proactive scheduling.

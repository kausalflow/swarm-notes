title: Trade-off in Multivariate Forecasting
---

**Background:** Channel-independent and channel-dependent multivariate time series forecasting methods present a persistent trade-off between modeling granularity, temporal capacity, generalization, efficiency, and scalability.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work must address the unresolved trade-off among modeling granularity, temporal capacity, generalization, efficiency, and scalability in multivariate time series forecasting architectures, specifically by designing cross-variable interaction mechanisms that scale robustly without sacrificing long-range temporal modeling capacity or inducing severe overfitting.

**Why It Matters:** This trade-off is fundamental to multivariate time series forecasting, defining the core architectural limitations that separate channel-independent and channel-dependent paradigms.

**Evidence:** These developments therefore leave an unresolved trade-off among modeling granularity, temporal capacity, generalization, efficiency, and scalability.

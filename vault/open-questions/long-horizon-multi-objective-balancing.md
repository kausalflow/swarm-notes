title: Long-Horizon Multi-Objective Balancing
---

**Background:** Ride-hailing forecasting systems require models that can simultaneously maintain pointwise accuracy, broad trend quality, and near-term reliability across extended prediction horizons.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and develop model architectures capable of simultaneously achieving low pointwise errors, accurate broad trends, and reliable near-term forecasts under long-horizon 8-week-ahead ride-hailing forecasting settings.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for bridging the gap between short-term operational dispatching and long-term strategic planning in urban mobility systems.

**Evidence:** For long-horizon forecasting, existing models cannot simultaneously achieve low pointwise errors, broad trends, and reliable near-term forecasts.

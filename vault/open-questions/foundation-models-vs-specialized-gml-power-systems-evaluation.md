title: Foundation Models vs Specialized GML
---

**Background:** Modern power systems increasingly rely on graph machine learning (GML) architectures to capture topological dependencies, but verifying their generalizability and robustness remains hindered by the scarcity of standardized, large-scale, and publicly available benchmarks.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine whether unified foundation models for electric power grids can comprehensively resolve all graph-based power system tasks across transmission and distribution scales, or if specialized task-specific GML architectures will continue to dominate operational deployments.

**Why It Matters:** Resolving whether a single grid foundation model can replace task-specific GML pipelines is critical for standardizing power system architectures and guiding future benchmark development.

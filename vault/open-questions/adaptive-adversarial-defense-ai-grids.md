title: Adaptive Adversarial Defenses for AI-Driven Grids
---

**Background:** AI models deployed for power system monitoring and intrusion detection are vulnerable to adversarial attacks that perturb inputs while bypassing physical and bad data detection mechanisms.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and design robust adversarial defenses and dynamic defense paradigms, such as moving target defense (MTD) combined with ML-based and physics-based strategies, that continuously evolve to protect AI-enabled power grids against adaptive adversaries without suffering from static model vulnerabilities.

**Why It Matters:** Static defenses are easily bypassed by adaptive adversaries in prolonged power system operations; developing scalable dynamic defenses is critical for securing AI-driven energy infrastructures.

**Evidence:** Consequently, many existing defenses provide robustness primarily against previously observed attack patterns rather than adaptive attacks that explicitly optimize against the deployed detector... Consequently, improving the robustness of AI models through one-time model hardening alone is generally insufficient for providing long-term protection, highlighting the need for defense mechanisms that continuously evolve together with the attacker.

title: Systematic Design Rules for Multimode Recurrent Processors
---

**Background:** Systematic design rules and architectures for optimizing multimode recurrent processors remain underdeveloped.

**Question / Future Work:** Systematic design rules and architecture optimization guidelines for multimode recurrent processors are currently lacking, mirroring established design principles in electronic neural networks. Future work needs to establish robust theoretical frameworks and design guidelines to optimize multimode recurrent processors for diverse spatiotemporal learning tasks.

**Why It Matters:** Establishing rigorous design rules is critical to transition optical recurrent neural networks from proof-of-concept demonstrations to scalable, application-specific photonic hardware accelerators.

**Evidence:** While the present results demonstrate stable operation across multiple tasks, systematic design rules for optimizing multimode recurrent processors—analogous to architecture design in electronic neural networks—are still lacking.

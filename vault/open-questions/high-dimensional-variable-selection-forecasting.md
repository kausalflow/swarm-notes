title: High-Dimensional Variable Selection for Forecasting
---

**Background:** Electricity price forecasting models must handle high-dimensional sets of candidate predictors characterized by strong serial and cross-sectional dependence, leading to over-parameterized models when using standard regularization techniques.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the theoretical and empirical properties of multiple testing and screening-based variable selection procedures, such as Boosting with Multiple Testing (BMT) and generalized OCMT, in high-dimensional forecasting applications involving strongly dependent or non-linear macroeconomic and energy predictors.

**Why It Matters:** Addressing high multicollinearity and pseudo-signal selection in high-dimensional forecasting without incurring severe over-parameterization is a central challenge across econometric and machine learning applications.

**Evidence:** Given the growing complexity and dimensionality of candidate predictor sets in electricity markets, driven by the increasing integration of renewable generation and cross-border interconnections, screening-based procedures of this kind warrant further attention as a practical complement to established regularization approaches in applied forecasting.

title: Multivariate and Temporal Forecast Calibration
---

**Background:** Time series foundation models (TSFMs) are increasingly evaluated for probabilistic forecasting, but standard calibration metrics often rely on marginal or pooled average coverage that can obscure conditional or horizon-specific deficiencies.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending uncertainty calibration and evaluation frameworks for time series foundation models to multivariate probabilistic forecasts, more expressive recalibration techniques conditioning on the forecast trajectory or horizons, and dedicated training-time calibration objectives.

**Why It Matters:** Important for advancing trustworthy forecasting in high-stakes domains by moving beyond marginal marginal-coverage metrics toward multivariate and temporally-coherent calibration.

**Evidence:** Extending the analysis to joint forecast calibration across time, more expressive recalibration methods that condition on the forecast horizon or on the forecast itself, and training-time approaches for improving calibration are promising directions for future work.

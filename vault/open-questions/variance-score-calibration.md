title: Calibrating Forecast Variance Scores
---

**Background:** Stochastic forecasting models generate multiple trajectories for observed biological structures, producing sample variance scores that help identify reliable predictions.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work must calibrate the variance score against observed forecast error and quantify its sensitivity to specific imaging perturbations and biological changes that are absent from the input history.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for establishing confidence intervals and reliability metrics in high-stakes biological forecasting tasks.

**Evidence:** Future work should calibrate the variance score against observed forecast error and quantify its sensitivity to these specific imaging perturbations and biological changes that are absent from the input history.

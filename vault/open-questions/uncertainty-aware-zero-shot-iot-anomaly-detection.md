title: Uncertainty-Aware Zero-Shot IoT Anomaly Detection
---

**Background:** Unsupervised anomaly detection models in heterogeneous Internet of Things (IoT) networks frequently experience severe performance degradation under distribution shift when deployed in unseen target environments without target-side adaptation or fine-tuning.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate advanced uncertainty-aware rejection mechanisms, broader multi-source domain coverage strategies, and lightweight adaptor-only calibration techniques to mitigate false positives and enhance robust generalization in strict zero--shot target domains where aligned source representations lack sufficient latent coverage.

**Why It Matters:** Addresses the fundamental reliability bottleneck of zero-shot anomaly detection under severe cross-domain distribution shifts in critical infrastructure.

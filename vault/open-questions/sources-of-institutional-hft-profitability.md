title: Mechanisms of Institutional Trading Profitability
---

**Background:** Institutional traders exhibit persistent intraday price-forecasting ability across multiple sub-second to minute horizons, which translates into highly profitable trading strategies and positive covariation with proprietary realized P&L.

**Question / Future Work:** While the paper documents significant price-forecasting persistence and higher profitability for traders with better forecasting proxies and passive execution roles, a comprehensive understanding of the precise economic mechanisms—such as separating the contributions of private information, order-flow anticipation, speed advantages, and inventory risk-bearing—remains an open area for high-frequency market microstructure research.

**Why It Matters:** Pinpointing the exact causal sources of institutional high-frequency profitability helps resolve debates regarding market quality, informational rents versus intermediation compensation, and the welfare effects of algorithmic trading.

**Evidence:** An important question about trading in financial markets is where proprietary intraday trading profits come from... understanding their source requires measuring profits and candidate mechanisms at the same empirical unit.

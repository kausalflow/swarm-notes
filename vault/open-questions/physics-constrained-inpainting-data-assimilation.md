title: Physics-Constrained Inpainting for Data Assimilation
---

**Background:** Ensemble score filtering integrates image inpainting to solve data assimilation problems with partial observations, but existing dictionary learning and PDE-based inpainting techniques require further adaptation to physical constraints and complex geophysical models.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing advanced inpainting techniques for ensemble score filters that incorporate physical constraints and conservation laws into the learning process, extend to time-varying/adaptive observation networks, hybridize PDE-based and dictionary-learning methods dynamically based on local observation density, and scale to complex operational geophysical systems such as primitive equation models and coupled atmosphere-ocean systems.

**Why It Matters:** Bridging generative data assimilation filters with physical laws and scaling them to operational weather forecasting systems is critical for real-world geoscience applications.

**Evidence:** First, the current dictionary learning approach could be enhanced by incorporating physical constraints and conservation laws into the learning process... A particularly important direction for future research is the application of this framework to more complex geophysical systems beyond the SQG model, such as primitive equation models and coupled atmosphere-ocean systems.

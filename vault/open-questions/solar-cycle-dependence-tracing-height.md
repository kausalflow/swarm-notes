title: Solar-Cycle Dependence of Tracing Height
---

**Background:** The Wang-Sheeley-Arge (WSA) model uses magnetic field line tracing in the solar corona to determine open-field boundaries and predict solar wind high-speed enhancements at Earth.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate whether the optimal initial field line tracing height exhibits a systematic dependence on the solar-cycle phase across an extensive dataset spanning different phases of the solar cycle, in order to determine whether a universal optimal value can be identified or whether it varies over time.

**Why It Matters:** Essential for establishing robust, universally applicable space weather forecasting models that remain accurate across all phases of solar activity rather than being tuned to specific solar cycle epochs.

title: Scalable Graph Construction Strategies
---

**Background:** Patch-based multivariate time series forecasting methods utilize various partitioning strategies, but often struggle to balance computational efficiency with the exploration of scalable structured semantic modeling and graph construction strategies.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work should investigate more efficient graph construction strategies and scalable structured modeling techniques to broaden the applicability of semantic-structured patch partitioning frameworks for multivariate time series forecasting.

**Why It Matters:** Important for scaling graph-based dependency modeling to massive multivariate time series domains with thousands of variables.

**Evidence:** We believe future work can further explore more efficient graph construction strategies and scalable structured modeling to broaden its applicability.

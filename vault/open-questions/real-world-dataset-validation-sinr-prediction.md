title: Real-World Validation for SINR Prediction
---

**Background:** Predicting downlink signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR) in beam-based 5G New Radio (NR) networks typically relies on synthetic datasets and simulation models.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work needs to extend the proposed data-driven SINR prediction framework from synthetic 3GPP-compliant datasets to real-world measurement datasets, evaluating its robustness in actual operational cellular environments.

**Why It Matters:** Validating data-driven wireless performance predictors on real-world measurements is critical to confirm their operational viability beyond synthetic channel models.

title: Theoretical Scaling Laws for AI Weather Models
---

**Background:** Artificial intelligence weather prediction models are trained on coarse-grained historical data that filter out small-scale atmospheric variability, yet the scaling laws relating training data resolution and size to prediction accuracy remain purely empirical.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop theoretical scaling laws relating training-data size and resolution to AI weather prediction model accuracy beyond current empirical estimates, establishing explicit mathematical formulations for how data constraints affect large-scale skill.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the theoretical scaling limits of AI weather models is crucial for predicting data requirements and optimizing resource allocation for future weather and climate foundation models.

**Evidence:** Developing theoretical scaling laws relating training-data size to AIWP model accuracy, beyond emerging empirical estimates, remains an important open research direction and can build on our findings.

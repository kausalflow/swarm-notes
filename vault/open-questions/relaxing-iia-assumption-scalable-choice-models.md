title: Relaxing IIA in Scalable Choice Models
---

**Background:** Demand transfer estimation methods rely heavily on multinomial logit models that depend on the independence of irrelevant alternatives (IIA) assumption, which can restrict accurate representation of complex substitution behavior across large retail categories.

**Question / Future Work:** Explore more flexible choice models, such as Nested Logit or Mixed Logit formulations, to relax the Independence of Irrelevant Alternatives (IIA) assumption while preserving computational scalability for large item universes.

**Why It Matters:** Relaxing IIA is critical for capturing realistic substitution patterns in retail assortment optimization without sacrificing the computational tractability required for million-item product universes.

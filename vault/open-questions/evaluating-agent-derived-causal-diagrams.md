title: Evaluating Agent-Derived Causal Diagrams
---

**Background:** Autonomous multi-agent systems are increasingly deployed to automate enterprise forecasting workflows by interpreting domain documents and interacting with data pipelines.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing robust mechanisms to evaluate and quantify the quality, reliability, and validity of intermediate artifacts such as Ideal Causal Loop Diagrams and Data-Grounded Causal Loop Diagrams within multi-agent forecasting systems remains an open challenge.

**Why It Matters:** Evaluating intermediate causal graphs and representations is critical to ensuring that agent-driven forecasting modifications are methodologically sound and reliable for enterprise decision-making.

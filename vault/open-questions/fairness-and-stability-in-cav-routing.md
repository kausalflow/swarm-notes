title: Fairness and Stability in CAV Routing
---

**Background:** Decentralized dynamic traffic routing strategies for connected and automated vehicles often optimize individual or aggregate travel efficiency metrics without explicitly guaranteeing system-level fairness among heterogeneous road users.

**Question / Future Work:** Design cooperative multi-vehicle routing strategies and game-theoretic control frameworks that simultaneously mitigate network-wide congestion, enhance route stability, and ensure equity and fairness between connected and automated vehicles and non-CAV traffic.

**Why It Matters:** Ensuring fairness and stability across mixed vehicle fleets prevents bottlenecks and discriminatory delays for human-driven or non-cooperative vehicles in real-world deployments.

**Evidence:** Future work will validate the framework on larger, more diverse networks and develop cooperative multi-vehicle strategies to reduce congestion while preserving fairness and route stability.

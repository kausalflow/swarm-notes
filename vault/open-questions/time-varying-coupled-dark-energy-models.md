title: Time-Varying Coupled Dark Energy
---

**Background:** Coupled dark energy models typically assume a constant interaction strength between dark energy and dark matter throughout cosmic history, but late-time datasets favor models that allow for time-varying couplings.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing and motivating cosmological models that incorporate time-varying dark energy-dark matter couplings remains an important direction, as late-time datasets are only sensitive to detections when the interaction rate evolves from a small past value to a larger present-day value.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for reconciling strict late-time cosmological constraints with ongoing observational searches for interacting dark sectors.

**Evidence:** The possibility of detecting the coupling with late-time data seems therefore to require models with time-varying couplings, so that one can have a small \\beta in the past and a relatively larger one today. Constructing and motivating such models is an interesting challenge for future work.

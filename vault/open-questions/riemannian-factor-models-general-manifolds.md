title: Riemannian Factor Models on General Manifolds
---

**Background:** Factor models on Riemannian manifolds lack comprehensive asymptotic theory and estimation frameworks capable of handling high-dimensional, temporally dependent manifold-valued data under incomplete geometry conditions.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending Riemannian factor models and associated high-dimensional statistical inference guarantees to more general or incomplete manifolds (such as specific configuration spaces or infinite-dimensional generalizations) remains an open challenge for future theoretical development.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for broadening geometry-aware dimension reduction techniques to complex topological spaces where standard completeness assumptions fail.

**Evidence:** Although these properties introduce some complexity to the theoretical analysis, our theoretical results are applicable in this setting; see Section 3 for further discussions.

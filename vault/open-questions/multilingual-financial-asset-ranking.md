title: Multilingual Financial Asset Ranking
---

**Background:** Multilingual evaluation is vital for financial asset ranking models, as current approaches rely exclusively on English corpora and fail to capture regional market signals or local news events.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the FinRankGRPO portfolio optimization and listwise asset ranking framework to multilingual financial corpora to capture regional market signals, cross-border macroeconomic indicators, and local financial events outside the English-speaking news ecosystem.

**Why It Matters:** Important for generalization across international markets where localized news and non-English financial documents drive significant asset volatility.

**Evidence:** Extending FinRankGRPO to multilingual financial corpora is an important direction for future work.

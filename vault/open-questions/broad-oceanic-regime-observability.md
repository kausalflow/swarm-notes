title: Broad Oceanic Regime Observability
---

**Background:** Predicting and detecting the thermal wakes of underwater vehicles in infrared imagery requires understanding how surface thermal anomalies are governed by environmental variability and coupled boundary-layer dynamics.

**Question / Future Work:** Future observations across broader oceanic regimes are required to establish predictive relationships between varying ocean-atmosphere states and thermal wake manifestations, moving beyond regional field datasets toward universal forecasting models.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for transitioning thermal wake surveillance from localized port environments to general oceanic deployment under varying climatic and meteorological regimes.

**Evidence:** Future observations across broader oceanic regimes will be required to establish predictive relationships between ocean-atmosphere states and thermal wake manifestation.

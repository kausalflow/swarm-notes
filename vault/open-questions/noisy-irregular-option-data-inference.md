title: Inference with Noisy Option Data
---

**Background:** Rough volatility models and Bergomi-type specifications are driven by singular forward variance kernels, yet parametric inference theories typically rely on regular kernels or restrictive auxiliary approximations.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigating whether the marginal composite likelihood estimation and asymptotic normality results can be extended to settings with observational noise, market microstructure frictions, or asynchronous and irregularly spaced option data.

**Why It Matters:** Extending high-frequency option-based inference to noisy or irregular observational setups is crucial for bridging theoretical rough volatility models with real-world financial data limitations.

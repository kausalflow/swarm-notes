title: Surrogate Null Models for Search Dynamics
---

**Background:** Online search data and epidemiological time series often exhibit complex predictive relationships that conventional single-metric evaluation protocols fail to fully capture.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop robust validation strategies and surrogate-based null models to distinguish true source-specific predictive contributions from slow common dynamics, seasonal trends, and unmeasured media effects in observational time series.

**Why It Matters:** Important for separating genuine information signals from spurious temporal correlations driven by seasonality or shared external confounding in digital epidemiology and time-series analysis.

**Evidence:** A focused empirical null analysis using temporally constrained surrogate sources would be a useful next step for distinguishing source-specific contribution from slow common dynamics and seasonality, particularly for control-like pairs.

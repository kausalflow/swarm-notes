title: Origin of Turbulent Lyapunov Scaling
---

**Background:** The Lyapunov exponent in fully developed turbulence is governed by small-scale dynamics, but current direct numerical simulations and theoretical models yield conflicting estimates regarding its exact scaling dependence on the Reynolds number.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine the precise origin and correct scaling behavior of the turbulent Lyapunov exponent with respect to the Reynolds number, resolving discrepancies between theoretical predictions (such as multifractal/She-Leveque models), shell models, and recent direct numerical simulations of three-dimensional Navier-Stokes turbulence.

**Why It Matters:** Resolving the scaling of the Lyapunov exponent in high-Reynolds-number turbulence is critical for bridging microscopic chaotic sensitivity with macroscopic predictability limits.

**Evidence:** In 3D turbulence, recent results found a different value [53, 54, 55], the origin of which is still debated and may be linked to effects coming from sweeping of the small scales from the large ones [56], an effect that is absent in the shell model.

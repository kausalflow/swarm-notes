title: Domain Extension and Boundary Conditions
---

**Background:** Observation-driven wind nowcasting frameworks are typically limited to regional spatial domains, restricting their ability to anticipate weather systems entering from outside the modeled area.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and develop domain extension and boundary condition modeling techniques to allow observation-driven wind nowcasting models to account for approaching weather systems originating outside the training and inference domains.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for improving nowcasting accuracy during non-uniform or highly dynamic synoptic events where large-scale weather systems propagate across regional boundaries.

**Evidence:** This current limitation of WindCastNet can be addressed in future versions of the model, motivating further research on domain extension and boundary condition modeling.

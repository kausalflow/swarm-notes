title: Evaluating Quantum Models on NISQ Hardware
---

**Background:** Financial time-series forecasting models based on hybrid quantum-classical architectures typically rely on noiseless state-vector simulations and lack evaluation on actual quantum processing hardware.

**Question / Future Work:** Evaluate and scale hybrid quantum-classical neural network frameworks for financial time-series forecasting on real quantum hardware devices, accounting for quantum noise, decoherence effects, and finite qubit resource constraints.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for understanding the practical deployability and fault-tolerance requirements of quantum machine learning models in real-world noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) financial environments.

**Evidence:** Second, the quantum part was simulated with a state-vector simulator without taking into account the impact of quantum noises, decoherence, and finite qubit resources.

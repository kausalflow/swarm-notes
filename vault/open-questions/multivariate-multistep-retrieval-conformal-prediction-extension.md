title: Extension to Multivariate and Multi-step Forecasting
---

**Background:** Time series forecasting models often struggle to produce reliable prediction intervals under high-dimensional data, multivariate targets, and multi-step forecasting horizons where uncertainty varies across dimensions and time.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and extend retrieval-corrected conformal prediction methods to handle high-dimensional observations, multivariate targets, and multi-step forecasting horizons, addressing scenarios where residuals are structured and uncertainty fluctuates across multiple dimensions and future steps.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for scaling uncertainty quantification and conformal prediction methods to complex multivariate and multi-step industrial time series applications.

**Evidence:** Future work should also study retrieval–corrected calibration for high-dimensional observations, multivariate targets, and multi-step horizons, where residuals are structured and uncertainty can vary across dimensions and horizons.

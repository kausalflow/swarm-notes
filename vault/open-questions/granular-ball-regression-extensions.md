title: Extensions of Granular Ball Regression
---

**Background:** Granular ball computing and twin support vector regression are integrated to build robust region-level regression models for complex and noisy data distributions.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research will focus on extending the proposed granular ball twin support vector regression and quantile regression framework to multivariate and high-dimensional regression tasks, online and streaming data settings, and deep hybrid architectures that combine granular representations with neural networks to further enhance adaptability and scalability.

**Why It Matters:** Extending the granular ball regression framework to high-dimensional, streaming, and deep architectures represents a crucial methodological direction for handling large-scale real-world data streams.

**Evidence:** Future work will extend the framework to multivariate and high-dimensional regression, online and streaming settings, and deep hybrid architectures integrating granular representations with neural networks to improve adaptability and scalability.

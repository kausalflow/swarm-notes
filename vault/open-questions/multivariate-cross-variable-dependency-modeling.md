title: Multivariate Cross-Variable Dependency Modeling
---

**Background:** Time series anomaly detection requires modeling complex temporal dynamics where anomalies depend heavily on contextual operating states and feature dependencies across variables.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the context-anchored pairing and supervision recovery framework to explicitly model and capture cross-variable dependencies in multivariate time series data rather than relying solely on variable-wise independent processing.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for scaling anomaly detection frameworks to complex multi-sensor industrial systems where anomalies frequently manifest as abnormal correlations or interactions across multiple dimensions rather than isolated univariate changes.

**Evidence:** The current framework focuses on temporal anomaly effects; explicit modeling of cross-variable dependencies remains an important direction for future work.

title: Generalizing Scenario-Conditioned Impact Estimation
---

**Background:** Deep limit order book forecasting models capture complex nonlinear market dynamics, but their ability to quantify the effects of counterfactual order book messages requires systematic evaluation across various architectures and trading horizons.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate alternative forecasting architectures, prediction horizons, market regimes, and multi-message strategies to evaluate how model-implied impact generalizes beyond single-message interventions and specific model configurations.

**Why It Matters:** Determining whether scenario-conditioned response estimators generalize across diverse neural network architectures and multi-message counterfactuals is critical for robust execution algorithms and market surveillance systems.

**Evidence:** Future work should examine additional forecasting architectures, horizons, market regimes, and multi-message strategies, and determine how model-implied impact can be calibrated for downstream execution and surveillance applications.

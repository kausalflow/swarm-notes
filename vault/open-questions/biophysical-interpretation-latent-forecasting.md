title: Biophysical Interpretation of Latent Forecasting
---

**Background:** Earth observation foundation models often rely on observation reconstruction or temporal summarization rather than directional predictive supervision, leaving the effectiveness of multi-horizon latent forecasting for generalizable pixel-level representations largely underexplored.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and establish how multi-horizon latent forecasting objectives can be generalized across diverse, irregularly sampled, and missing-modality satellite records without relying on complete-coverage monthly compositing, while directly relating latent trajectory predictions to underlying biophysical and agronomic processes.

**Why It Matters:** Important for bridging the gap between abstract latent forecasting performance and physically interpretable Earth-observation models that can handle real-world irregular sensor availability.

**Evidence:** High latent-forecasting accuracy does not by itself guarantee physical interpretability, and future work should relate individual latent trajectories to measurable biophysical and agronomic processes.

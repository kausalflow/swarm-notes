title: Extensions to Discontinuous Regimes and Higher Resolutions
---

**Background:** Deep generative models and grey-box dynamical systems combining physical priors with data-driven components often rely on continuous-time modeling for forecasting complex earth system dynamics.

**Question / Future Work:** The authors explicitly note that extending the current framework to handle discontinuous regimes and higher spatial or temporal resolutions represents a natural and important next step for future investigation.

**Why It Matters:** Handling discontinuities (such as atmospheric fronts or phase changes) and higher resolutions is critical for scaling data-driven climate models to operational weather forecasting scales without breaking physical constraints.

**Evidence:** Extensions to discontinuous regimes and higher resolutions are natural next steps.

title: Uncertainty Calibration in Filtering
---

**Background:** Deep Bayesian filtering frameworks rely on learned noise covariances and variational formulations that typically capture measurement and state uncertainty while often neglecting the epistemic uncertainties of the underlying neural networks.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending deep Bayesian filtering to provide fully calibrated uncertainty estimates requires quantifying both aleatoric and epistemic uncertainties across the learnable network weights and filter components, ensuring reliable predictive intervals and innovation statistics in operational monitoring.

**Why It Matters:** Uncertainty quantification and calibration are vital for reliable digital twinning and structural health monitoring, where uncalibrated confidence bounds can lead to faulty structural assessment decisions.

title: Explicit Motion Representation in Spatio-Temporal Graph Models
---

**Background:** Spatio-temporal graph models typically use a space-then-time factorization that fails to explicitly represent the velocity or rate of change of propagating patterns, leading to displacement errors over recursive forecasts.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate whether explicit representation of motion and velocity can be generalized and optimized for other continuous or discrete spatio-temporal domains beyond meteorological and standard temporal graph benchmarks, and study the theoretical limits of second-order state representations in higher-dimensional or non-linear graph dynamics.

**Why It Matters:** Important for extending wave-inspired state-space models to a broader range of chaotic and propagating spatio-temporal systems.

**Evidence:** This raises a natural question. Can the activation pattern and its velocity be represented jointly, so that propagation through graph edges and evolution through time are advanced within the same transition?

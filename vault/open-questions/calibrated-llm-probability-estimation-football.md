title: Calibrated Probabilistic LLM Football Forecasting
---

**Background:** Football exact-score forecasting relies on a balance between statistical rate estimation and contextual reasoning, yet existing hybrid architectures struggle with calibrating LLM-generated pathways and handling zero-goal or tail outcomes.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and develop methods to output normalized, properly calibrated probabilities directly from LLM match-state simulation paths, enabling rigorous scoring-rule evaluation rather than mere ranking.

**Why It Matters:** Transitioning from heuristic rankers to calibrated probabilistic forecasters is essential for sound decision-making and proper evaluation in sports forecasting.

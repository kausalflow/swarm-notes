title: Finer-Grained Contextual Agentic Orchestration
---

**Background:** Agentic model orchestration methods combine multiple time series foundation models to improve forecasting accuracy, but evaluating their true contribution independent of candidate strength and pool composition remains challenging.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate alternative orchestration mechanisms such as finer-grained commitments and agentic methods that utilize richer contextual information beyond backtests to better harness the theoretical headroom provided by candidate model pools.

**Why It Matters:** Developing finer-grained commitment strategies and leveraging richer context is crucial to bridging the gap between current deployable orchestrators and optimal window-level oracles in time series forecasting.

**Evidence:** Future work may therefore explore finer-grained commitments, agentic methods that use context beyond backtests, and candidate pools built around complementary error profiles rather than model rank.

title: Metallicity Calibration in Gyrochronology
---

**Background:** Empirical gyrochronology models are predominantly calibrated using solar-metallicity benchmarks, leaving the exact impact of sub-solar metallicities on temperature-period relations and stellar spin-down rates unresolved due to a lack of coeval clusters with diverse chemical compositions.

**Question / Future Work:** Securing a broader sample of benchmark open clusters with diverse chemical compositions is necessary to fully calibrate metallicity trends and properly constrain these effects within gyrochronology models.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for testing whether temperature-period relations universally mitigate metallicity-driven differences in stellar spin-down across diverse stellar populations.

**Evidence:** However, a lack of coeval clusters with diverse chemical composition currently hinders a full calibration of metallicity trends. Securing a broader sample of such benchmark clusters represents an exciting next step for properly constraining these effects within the gyrochronology community.

title: Broader Evaluation of Probabilistic Forecasting
---

**Background:** LLM forecasting systems typically employ holistic (monolithic) prediction stages that combine all collected evidence in a single context window, which can obscure individual evidence contributions and lead to uncertainty collapse.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the evaluation of likelihood elicitation and aggregation forecasting frameworks to a broader range of languages, specialized scientific domains, longer forecast horizons, and substantially different agent designs beyond English forecasting and agentic information-seeking benchmarks.

**Why It Matters:** Important for testing the generalizability and robustness of decoupled probabilistic aggregation approaches in LLM forecasting across diverse multi-lingual and domain-specific challenges.

**Evidence:** Extending the evaluation to other languages, specialised scientific domains, longer forecast horizons, and substantially different agent designs is left to future work.

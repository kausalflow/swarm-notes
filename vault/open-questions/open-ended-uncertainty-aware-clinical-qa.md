**Background:** Question answering over irregular clinical time series requires handling sparse, asynchronous, and multi-scale observations through multimodal large language models.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work will extend the framework to open-ended questions, uncertainty-aware reasoning, and evaluation across broader clinical settings.

**Why It Matters:** Extending time-series reasoning frameworks from multiple-choice benchmarks to open-ended generation and uncertainty quantification is crucial for real-world clinical deployment and reliability.

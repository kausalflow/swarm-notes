title: Interpretability of Shared Temporal Filters
---

**Background:** Multivariate time series forecasting models increasingly explore lightweight architectures that share temporal components across variables while adapting to variable-specific dynamics through learned scalar weights.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigating the interpretability aspects of shared temporal filter architectures, particularly analyzing the exact contributions of individual temporal filters to each variable using explicit feature-specific weights and pruning criteria to determine relevant temporal patterns.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding feature-specific weights and filter importance scores bridges the gap between compact time-series architectures and model explainability, providing insights into which temporal scales matter for specific variables.

**Evidence:** Exploring these interpretability aspects, as well as extending the approach to different temporal structures, represents a promising direction for future work.

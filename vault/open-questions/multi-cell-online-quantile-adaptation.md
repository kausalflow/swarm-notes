title: Multi-Cell and Online Quantile Adaptation
---

**Background:** Goal-oriented probabilistic forecasting frameworks are typically evaluated on isolated single cells or beam configurations without considering broader multi-cell coordination or online adaptation mechanisms.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the goal-oriented probabilistic forecasting framework to multi-cell environments and incorporate online quantile adaptation mechanisms to maintain performance under temporal traffic distribution shifts.

**Why It Matters:** Extending single-cell resource allocation frameworks to multi-cell settings and enabling online adaptation are critical steps for deploying decision-focused learning and goal-oriented forecasting in dynamic, large-scale cellular networks.

**Evidence:** Future work will extend this framework to multi-cell environments and online quantile adaptation.

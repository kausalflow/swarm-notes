title: End-to-End Agentic Trading Integration
---

**Background:** Quantitative trading systems are increasingly built using large language model (LLM) agents that perform specific tasks across the trading pipeline, but existing architectures remain heavily fragmented and primarily concentrated on signal discovery rather than full end-to-end integration.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and construct full-stack agentic trading pipelines that seamlessly integrate signal generation with downstream portfolio construction, order execution, and robust risk management modules, moving beyond isolated signal discovery systems.

**Why It Matters:** Most current literature treats quantitative trading as a pure return-forecasting or signal-generation problem, ignoring the critical downstream bottlenecks of portfolio optimization, execution slippage, and risk control that dictate real-world profitability.

**Evidence:** Our review finds that current systems remain concentrated on signal discovery, while complete integration with portfolio construction, execution, and risk control is still uncommon.

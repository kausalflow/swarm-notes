title: Latent Dynamics for Closed-Loop Assimilation
---

**Background:** Real-time reconstruction of fluid flows from sparse sensor measurements utilizes data-driven reduced-order models optimized for forecasting rather than state estimation.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work needs to systematically investigate how different latent-space dynamics and operator parameterizations directly impact closed-loop data assimilation and state estimation performance under streaming, sparse, and noisy observations.

**Why It Matters:** Bridging the gap between open-loop forecasting accuracy and closed-loop data assimilation performance is critical for reliable real-time sensing and control in fluid dynamics.

**Evidence:** This work addresses a task-driven question motivated by sparse sensing: when the downstream objective is data assimilation, which choice of latent dynamics model yields the most effective state reconstruction?

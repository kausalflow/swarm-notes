title: Real-World Multimodal Evidence Arbitration
---

**Background:** Large language models are increasingly integrated into multi-source decision pipelines involving textual summaries, numerical observations, and external tool outputs, but frequently exhibit failure modes when these heterogeneous sources conflict.

**Question / Future Work:** Characterize real-world source conflicts and complex multi-modal decision environments beyond controlled synthetic trajectories, investigating how large language models arbitrate real-world messy data where ground-truth and provenance are ambiguous.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding whether heuristic arbitration strategies observed in synthetic benchmarks persist or shift when facing unconstrained, noisy, and heterogeneous real-world data is critical for safe deployment in high-stakes domains like healthcare and finance.

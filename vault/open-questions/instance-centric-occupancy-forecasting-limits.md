title: Instance-Centric 4D Occupancy Forecasting Limits
---

**Background:** Camera-only 4D occupancy forecasting in autonomous driving relies on complex spatiotemporal representations that struggle with instance consistency and computational efficiency over long horizons.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigating how instance-centric query-based 4D occupancy forecasting generalizes to more complex, highly crowded driving environments, highly dynamic non-rigid objects, and longer prediction horizons remains an important open challenge for real-time autonomous driving perception.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the scalability of instance-centric queries to longer forecasting horizons and complex scenes is critical for deploying 4D occupancy models in real-world autonomous vehicles.

**Evidence:** These results demonstrate that the proposed instance-centric formulation retains a consistent forecasting advantage across all evaluated horizons.

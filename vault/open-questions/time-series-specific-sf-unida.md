title: Time-Series-Specific SF-UniDA Approaches
---

**Background:** Source-free universal domain adaptation (SF-UniDA) methods developed for computer vision rely on general-purpose backbones and fixed thresholds that fail to perform effectively when applied directly to complex temporal dynamics in time series data.

**Question / Future Work:** The development and optimization of source-free universal domain adaptation methodologies tailored specifically for time series representations remain an open challenge, given that current frameworks are predominantly adapted from computer vision and exhibit hypersensitivity to inference thresholds.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for advancing domain adaptation in temporal data modalities without relying on source data privacy violations or poorly calibrated vision-based heuristics.

**Evidence:** ...backbone selection currently has a larger impact on performance than the choice of SF-UniDA method, suggesting that developing time-series-specific SF-UniDA approaches is a promising direction for future work.

title: Phase Quality and Representation Encoding
---

**Background:** Wi-Fi sensing models must generalize across diverse environments, users, and device placements, yet input representations often remain sensitive to shifts in local propagation conditions.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how phase quality, phase calibration, and alternative representation-specific encodings impact cross-domain generalization and robustness across diverse Wi-Fi sensing environments and device configurations.

**Why It Matters:** Phase information is notoriously noisy and hardware-dependent in Wi-Fi CSI; understanding how to properly represent and utilize phase without degrading cross-domain performance is a critical foundational challenge for robust wireless sensing.

**Evidence:** AP-Patch records the lowest mean accuracy with all four backends, motivating further examination of phase quality and representation-specific encoding [6, 2].

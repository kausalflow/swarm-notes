title: Statistical vs. Economic Forecasting Value
---

**Background:** Electricity price forecasting models often optimize purely for statistical error metrics like root mean squared error, but their alignment with actual economic value and trading profitability remains an open challenge.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the divergence between statistical accuracy and economic performance in electricity price forecasting models, specifically determining why models with superior statistical error metrics often underperform in practical battery storage arbitrage and financial trading strategies.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the mapping between statistical loss functions and real-world economic utility is critical for deploying profitable decision-making models in energy markets.

**Evidence:** demonstrates that in practice, business value of forecasts needs to be analyzed alongside their statistical properties.

title: Joint Multivariate and Multi-Station Forecasting
---

**Background:** Adapting univariate time series foundation models to multi-station forecasting typically focuses on inter-variable correlations while overlooking static spatial relations and dynamic error priors.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work includes extending the current framework—which primarily captures interactions among stations—to simultaneously incorporate dependencies among meteorological variables for joint multivariate and multi-station weather forecasting.

**Why It Matters:** Extending station-level spatio-temporal adaptation to fully joint multivariate-station modeling represents an important architectural scaling step for weather forecasting foundation models.

**Evidence:** While this work focuses on interactions among stations, future work will further incorporate dependencies among meteorological variables for joint multivariate and multi-station forecasting.

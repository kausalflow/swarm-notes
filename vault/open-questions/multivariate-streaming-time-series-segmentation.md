title: Multivariate and Streaming Segmentation
---

**Background:** Time-series segmentation encompasses both change point detection (CPD) and state detection (SD), but existing literature lacks unified benchmarks that jointly evaluate these tasks across diverse domains and dimensionalities.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the expansion of unified time-series segmentation benchmarks to highly multivariate settings, streaming data environments, and gradual or hierarchical state transitions.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for scaling time-series segmentation models to real-world industrial and IoT streaming applications involving high-dimensional sensor arrays and gradual regime shifts.

**Evidence:** It covers univariate and moderately multivariate series, leaving highly multivariate settings largely unexplored... Finally, it focuses on offline segmentation and does not yet address online or streaming settings.

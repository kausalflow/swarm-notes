title: Reliability of Intervention-Aware Forecasting
---

**Background:** Time series foundation models can incorporate external covariates in-context to perform scenario analysis and forecast target responses to future events, but their reliability and generalization capabilities under novel conditions remain largely unverified.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate whether current time series foundation models can reliably perform scenario analysis and forecast target responses to future events when covariates are set to unseen future values (Novel futures), as current models experience sharp performance degradation outside previously observed covariate ranges.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding how to make time series foundation models robust to out-of-distribution covariate shifts is essential for reliable decision-making and planning across science, engineering, and commerce.

**Evidence:** Whether they can do this reliably is an open question.

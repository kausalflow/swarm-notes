title: Task-Object Hallucination and Routing Reliability
---

**Background:** Time-series language models often generate plausible text that fails to instantiate the required numerical tensors, legal decisions, or structured records, a failure mode known as task-object hallucination.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing generalizable compilation and execution frameworks that eliminate task-object hallucination across broader, highly heterogeneous multi-modal time-series domains remains an open challenge.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding and mitigating routing failures and runtime exceptions in heterogeneous task compilation is critical for deploying reliable language-interfaced time-series systems in production.

**Evidence:** Overall, the single checkpoint produces 4,766 valid objects from 4,795 evaluation instances. The remaining cases consist of one runtime failure and 28 waveform-routing failures.

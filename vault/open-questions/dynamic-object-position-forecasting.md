title: Dynamic Object Position Forecasting
---

**Background:** Accurate human motion forecasting in dynamic scenes requires models to account for objects that change position during the prediction interval, yet current approaches typically assume objects remain static at their last observed coordinates.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing human motion forecasting frameworks capable of handling moving objects within the prediction interval, by jointly predicting future object positions alongside human motion rather than relying solely on static last-observed object positions.

**Why It Matters:** Essential for expanding motion forecasting capabilities from controlled environments with static furniture to realistic, dynamic real-world environments where objects are manipulated or relocated by humans.

**Evidence:** Addressing this would require jointly predicting the future positions of the objects alongside human motion, a promising direction for future work.

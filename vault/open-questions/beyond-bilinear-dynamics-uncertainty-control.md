title: Beyond Bilinear Dynamics and Uncertainty-Aware Control
---

**Background:** Time series forecasting often relies on bilinear dynamical formulations within lifted spaces, but these frameworks remain restricted by the structural limitations of bilinear approximations and deterministic control assumptions.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research needs to extend the bilinear dynamical formulation to capture more general, higher-order nonlinear system dynamics beyond the current bilinear constraints, incorporate uncertainty-aware control inference mechanisms, and improve robustness in complex engineered or physical systems.

**Why It Matters:** Extending modeling beyond bilinear dynamics and incorporating uncertainty-aware control are crucial next steps for overcoming the representation capacity limits of current Koopman-based forecasting frameworks.

**Evidence:** Future work will focus on extending NBDM to capture more general nonlinear dynamics beyond the current bilinear formulation, incorporating uncertainty-aware control inference mechanisms, and further improving the modeling of complex dynamical systems.

title: Prediction-Powered Inference Under Non-Stationarity
---

**Background:** Real-world time-series data often exhibit non-stationary behaviors due to changing climates, evolving claims practices, or shifting environments over time.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending prediction-powered inference and simulation-based procedures to maintain validity under weaker forms of temporal stability, such as local stationarity or smoothly varying distributions, remains an important open challenge for future work.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for applying prediction-powered inference methods to real-world environmental and financial time series where long-term stationarity assumptions are violated.

**Evidence:** We consider developing simulation-based procedures which maintain validity under weaker forms of temporal stability (like local stationarity or smoothly varying distributions) an important direction for future work.

title: Distributional and Inferential Theory for Functional Factor Models
---

**Background:** Approximate factor models for functional time series rely on structural identification via autocovariance operators to separate predictable low-dimensional factor components from unpredictable error terms.

**Question / Future Work:** Establishing rigorous distributional theory, confidence bands, and hypothesis tests for the estimated loading functions and model parameters remains an open direction for future research beyond consistency results.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for statistical inference, hypothesis testing, and constructing valid confidence intervals for functional factor loadings and forecasts.

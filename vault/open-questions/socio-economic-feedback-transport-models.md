title: Coupling Socioeconomic Feedback to Transport Models
---

**Background:** Human demographic dynamics are governed by age-structured transport partial differential equations where fertility and mortality act as latent time-varying constitutive relations.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the coupling of latent constitutive laws within Bayesian transport PDEs to external socio-economic variables, such as gross domestic product, housing price indices, and female labor force participation, to create fully closed-loop socio-physics models of demographic feedback.

**Why It Matters:** Connecting demographic transport parameters directly to macroeconomic and socio-economic drivers is critical for understanding feedback loops between societal aging and economic performance.

**Evidence:** Third, the latent constitutive laws can be coupled to other socio-economic variables (such as GDP, housing price index, or female labor participation) by feeding them as inputs to the BNNs, enabling a fully closed-loop socio-physics modeling of demographic feedback.

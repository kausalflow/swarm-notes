title: Feature Sets for Regression and Forecasting
---

**Background:** Time-series feature sets are widely used to extract scalar representations for classification tasks, yet their relative performance and applicability remain underexplored beyond univariate classification settings.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the comparative performance of open-source time-series feature sets on alternative learning tasks such as extrinsic regression and time-series forecasting.

**Why It Matters:** Extends the benchmarking methodology to other foundational time-series tasks, testing whether feature set performance trends translate beyond classification.

**Evidence:** While here we focused on the time-series classification setting, future work may seek to explore comparative performance of the time-series feature sets on other problems like extrinsic regression or forecasting problems

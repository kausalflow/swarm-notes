title: Alternative Weighting and Continuous-Time Receptive Fields
---

**Background:** Irregular time series feature extraction methods often rely on uniform local aggregation and index-based statistics that fail to capture variable temporal supports and non-uniform sampling intervals.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate alternative boundary conventions for the initial time-delta weight, analyze the sensitivity of the time-aware transform to different irregularity types, develop continuous-time receptive fields, and formally evaluate the quantitative quality and stability of model explanations.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding how boundary assumptions and continuous-time adaptations affect symbolic pattern extraction is crucial for generalizing dictionary-based time series classification to diverse real-world irregular domains.

**Evidence:** Investigating the sensitivity to alternative boundary conventions is left for future work. ... Future work will study alternative weighting conventions, the effects of different irregularity types, continuous-time receptive fields, and the quantitative quality and stability of explanations.

title: Robust Granular-Ball Time-Series Extensions
---

**Background:** Time-series classification using dynamic time warping and nearest-neighbor classifiers is vulnerable to training label noise and requires intensive distance computations during inference.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop more reliable strategies for hyperparameter selection under noisy training labels, evaluate the granular-ball computing framework across a broader range of datasets and noise types, and extend the methodology to alternative time-series analysis tasks like clustering and anomaly detection.

**Why It Matters:** Hyperparameter selection under noise remains a key bottleneck in robust granular-ball computing, and extending the framework to other time-series tasks broadens its utility in machine learning applications.

**Evidence:** In future work, in addition to exploring more reliable strategies for hyperparameter selection under noisy labels, we plan to evaluate DTW-GBC on a broader range of time-series classification datasets and investigate its robustness under other types of label noise.

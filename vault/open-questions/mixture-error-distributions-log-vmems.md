title: Mixture Error Distributions for Log-vMEMs
---

**Background:** Heavy-tailed distributions and extreme value peaks in financial time series often cause model residuals to exhibit pronounced upper-tail departures when modeled using standard parametric error structures like the multivariate Gamma distribution.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and develop mixture error distributions within logarithmic vector multiplicative error models (log-vMEM) to simultaneously capture both the heavy tails and the central bulk of high-frequency financial time series, thereby resolving upper-tail departures observed in residual quantile-quantile plots.

**Why It Matters:** Developing flexible mixture error distributions is crucial for accurately modeling extreme financial volatility shocks and tail risks without breaking tractable likelihood estimation.

title: Adaptive Routing for Evolving Time Series
---

**Background:** Time series anomaly detection models often struggle to handle evolving data distributions and concept drift when using static routing thresholds.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate adaptive routing strategies for evolving time series environments, where continuously changing data distributions may require dynamic updates of routing decisions in cascaded inference frameworks.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for deploying efficient cascaded models in streaming or non-stationary environments where static pre-filtering thresholds fail over time.

**Evidence:** As future work, we will investigate adaptive routing strategies for evolving time series environments, where continuously changing data distributions may require dynamic updates of routing decisions.

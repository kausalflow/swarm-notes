title: Diverse Scenarios and Conformal Calibration
---

**Background:** Physics-aware trajectory prediction frameworks integrate kinematic models to ensure physical feasibility but often evaluate uncertainty and conformal calibration primarily under restricted highway settings.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research needs to extend the evaluation of uncertainty-aware physics-based trajectory prediction frameworks and conformal calibration methods to more diverse highway traffic scenarios and varied driving environments.

**Why It Matters:** Important for verifying the generalizability and robustness of physics-aware uncertainty propagation and conformal calibration beyond standard datasets like highD.

**Evidence:** Future work will focus on more diverse highway and traffic scenarios and improving conformal calibration methods.

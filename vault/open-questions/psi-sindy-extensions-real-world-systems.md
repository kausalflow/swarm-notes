title: Generalizing Post-Selection Inference for SINDy
---

**Background:** Sparse identification of nonlinear dynamics (SINDy) discovers governing equations from time-series data by selecting a sparse combination of terms from a candidate library. Conducting statistical inference for selected dynamical terms is complicated by selection bias, shared response-design noise, and measurement errors in candidate terms evaluated from noisy states.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend post-selection inference methods for sparse identification of nonlinear dynamics (PSI-SINDy) beyond scalar trajectories, fixed polynomial libraries, and independent Gaussian noise of known variance to accommodate finite-sample calibration, multistate systems, broader libraries, dependent noise, and estimated noise variance.

**Why It Matters:** Extending post-selection inference to handle more realistic settings such as unknown noise variance, correlated noise, and multidimensional systems is crucial for applying rigorous statistical reliability quantification to real-world physical and biological dynamical systems.

**Evidence:** Finite-sample calibration and extensions to multistate systems, broader libraries, dependent noise, and estimated noise variance remain open.

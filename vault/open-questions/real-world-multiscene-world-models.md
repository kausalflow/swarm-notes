**Background:** World models capture environment dynamics for forecasting and policy optimization, but their application to wireless networks remains largely unexplored.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work will incorporate real-world measurements, unified training across multiple urban scenes, and more comprehensive network planning and optimization tasks to extend world models to cellular environments.

**Why It Matters:** Extending world models from simulation to real-world deployment with multi-scene generalization is crucial for practical wireless network automation and management.

**Evidence:** Future work will incorporate real-world measurements, unified training across multiple urban scenes, and more comprehensive network planning and optimization tasks.

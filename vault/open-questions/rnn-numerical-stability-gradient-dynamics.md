title: RNN Numerical Stability and Gradient Dynamics
---

**Background:** Investigating numerical stability and gradient dynamics in multi-lag recurrent neural networks to improve scalability and robustness for time-series forecasting.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work will focus on a systematic study of numerical stability and gradient dynamics to further enhance the robustness and scalability of multi-lag RNN models for broader time-series forecasting applications.

**Why It Matters:** Critical for understanding how multi-lag recurrent neural network architectures handle gradient vanishing/exploding behaviors over extended sequences during training.

**Evidence:** Building on this foundation, future developments will focus on a systematic study of numerical stability and gradient dynamics, aiming to further enhance the robustness and scalability of RNN(p) models for broader forecasting applications.

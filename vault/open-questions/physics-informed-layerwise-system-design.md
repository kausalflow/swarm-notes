title: Physics-Informed Layerwise System Design
---

**Background:** Neural Dynamical Transfer Learning (NDTL) relies on multi-layer feature representations to generate new nonlinear dynamical systems by transferring content and style features between parent models.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigating layer-wise physical interpretations of neural representations in transfer learning frameworks for nonlinear dynamical systems to enable physics-informed system design and modulation remains an important direction for future work.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the distinct physical roles of different network layers in dynamical system generation bridges deep learning representations with rigorous dynamical systems theory, enabling targeted control over system creation and modulation.

**Evidence:** Leveraging this controllability for physics-informed system design and modulation is a promising direction for future work.

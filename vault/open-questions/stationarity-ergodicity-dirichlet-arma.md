title: Stationarity and Ergodicity of Dirichlet ARMA

**Background:** Observation-driven Bayesian Dirichlet ARMA models lack full primitive conditions for stationarity and ergodicity beyond limited special cases.

**Question / Future Work:** Establishing general, rigorous primitive conditions for stationarity and ergodicity in observation-driven Dirichlet ARMA models remains an open theoretical problem.

**Why It Matters:** Important for establishing formal asymptotic properties, consistency, and stability guarantees for Bayesian Dirichlet time series models.

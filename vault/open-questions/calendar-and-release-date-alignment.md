title: Calendar and Release-Date Alignment
---

**Background:** Financial time-series benchmarks often align temporal observations to standard calendar period ends rather than preserving series-specific reporting calendars and point-in-time release dates.

**Question / Future Work:** Improve point-in-time temporal alignment by integrating calendar-aware preprocessing and explicit release-date metadata for fiscal, crop-year, macroeconomic, and filing-date-sensitive financial time series.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for eliminating look-ahead bias and enabling realistic backtesting in financial forecasting models.

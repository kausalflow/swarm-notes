title: Multivariate Text-Controlled Time Series Generation
---

**Background:** Text-controlled time series generation relies on discrete symbolization of local shapes and continuous modeling of global scale, but existing methods are primarily restricted to univariate sequences.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the ShapeLex framework from univariate time series generation to handle complex multivariate time series where inter-variable dependencies and cross-channel shape alignments must be preserved.

**Why It Matters:** Multivariate time series generation introduces complex cross-channel dependencies and alignment challenges that univariate shape vocabularies cannot capture directly.

**Evidence:** Enriching the vocabulary with rarer shapes and extending it to the multivariate setting are promising directions for future work.

title: Standardized Post-Disaster Recovery Datasets
---

**Background:** Post-disaster recovery monitoring relies heavily on visual interpretation and proxy data due to the scarcity of systematic ground-truth datasets describing multi-temporal reconstruction processes.

**Question / Future Work:** The machine learning and remote sensing community lacks standardized reference datasets capturing the evolving dynamics of post-disaster recovery, which currently restricts the ability to perform rigorous quantitative accuracy assessments of unsupervised and data-driven recovery mapping frameworks.

**Why It Matters:** Without standard ground-truth datasets, benchmarking and validating unsupervised deep learning models for long-term disaster recovery remain fundamentally qualitative and constrained.

**Evidence:** The development of standardized reference datasets for post-disaster recovery monitoring therefore remains an open challenge for the EO community.

title: Cycle-to-Cycle Persistence of Flow-Activity Lags
---

**Background:** Lagged correlations between magnetic activity indices and cumulative longitudinal displacements in the solar near-surface shear layer exhibit significant time lags that vary across latitudes and hemispheres.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine whether the inferred time lags and correlations between the cumulative longitudinal displacement of zonal flows and magnetic activity persist across multiple solar cycles and whether they exhibit dependence on cycle phase or hemispheric offsets in cycle progression.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the cycle-to-cycle persistence of these time lags is crucial for clarifying the physical coupling between magnetic-field evolution and large-scale flows in the solar near-surface shear layer.

**Evidence:** It will be useful to test whether the inferred lags persist from one cycle to the next and whether they depend on cycle phase.

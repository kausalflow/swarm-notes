title: Physics-Guided Extensions for Polar Forecasting
---

**Background:** Machine learning models for sea ice concentration forecasting often operate entirely data-driven without integrating underlying physical laws or environmental conservation principles.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the integration of physical knowledge, thermodynamic constraints, and sea-ice dynamics into data-driven forecasting frameworks to enhance physical consistency, improve fine-scale predictions around sharp ice edges, and ensure robustness under future climate regimes and extreme events.

**Why It Matters:** Incorporating physical laws into data-driven polar forecasting is critical to prevent unphysical predictions, maintain robustness under climate change extremes, and accurately capture sharp boundary dynamics.

**Evidence:** The current framework is primarily data-driven and does not explicitly use physical knowledge of the Earth system, such as thermodynamic constraints, sea-ice dynamics, or coupled ocean-atmosphere information... Future work will investigate physics-guided extensions and evaluate the proposed design across broader geophysical prediction settings.

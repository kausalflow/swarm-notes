title: Theoretical Justification of Enhanced Algorithm
---

**Background:** Two-way grouped network quantile autoregressive models require iterative optimization algorithms to estimate latent row and column group memberships simultaneously.

**Question / Future Work:** Providing theoretical justification for the enhanced iterative optimization algorithm designed to escape local minima during column membership estimation remains an open challenge.

**Why It Matters:** Important for bridging the gap between empirical algorithm effectiveness and rigorous statistical guarantees in non-convex bi-clustering procedures.

**Evidence:** However, providing theoretical justification for Algorithm 2 poses a significant challenge and remains an intriguing avenue for future research.

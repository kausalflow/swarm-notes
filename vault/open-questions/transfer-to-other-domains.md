title: Transfer to Other Domains
---

**Background:** Counterfactual explanation methods are typically evaluated via single-instance metrics, lacking a global, class-level structural perspective on how classifiers connect different classes across the data space.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how counterfactual transition graph evaluation frameworks transfer and generalize to diverse time-series and sensor domains beyond hand-movement datasets.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for establishing whether global counterfactual transition topologies reveal universal properties of time series classifiers or remain domain-specific artifacts.

**Evidence:** We therefore propose the CFTG as a general diagnostic idea, but evaluate it here as a solution motivated by this concrete use case; testing its transfer to other domains remains future work.

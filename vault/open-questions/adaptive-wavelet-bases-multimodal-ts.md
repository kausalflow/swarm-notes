title: Adaptive Wavelet Bases and Multi-Modal Temporal Systems
---

**Background:** Self-supervised pre-training for time series models is predominantly driven by generative paradigms or basic vision-style augmentations that often disrupt temporal dynamics and fail to capture invariant structures.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work will explore adaptive, learnable wavelet bases and extend joint-embedding distillation to scale-free, multi-modal temporal systems.

**Why It Matters:** This is a concrete, multi-faceted direction that proposes specific technical extensions (learnable wavelet bases and scale-free multi-modal systems) to address limitations in current time-series self-supervised learning.

**Evidence:** Future work will explore adaptive, learnable wavelet bases and extend joint-embedding distillation to scale-free, multi-modal temporal systems.

title: Transferability of Demographic Embeddings for Health Outcomes
---

**Background:** Environmental epidemiology estimates mortality risk from temperature using Distributed Lag Non-linear Models (DLNMs), which currently do not incorporate demographic and geographic vulnerability context.

**Question / Future Work:** Assess whether the demographic and geographic representations learned by Risk Graph Neural Networks (RGNNs) transfer across broader geographic contexts and generalise to other types of public health outcome data beyond heat-related mortality.

**Why It Matters:** Determining whether spatial demographic embeddings can transfer zero-shot or few-shot across diverse geographic regions and health outcomes is vital for scaling predictive public health infrastructure.

**Evidence:** A natural extension is to assess whether the learned demographic embeddings transfer across geographic contexts, enabling risk curve estimation for areas with limited mortality data. ... We also intend to explore whether representations learned over England and Wales generalise to other health outcome data.

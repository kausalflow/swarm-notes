title: Broader Institutional Validation and Transferability
---

**Background:** Effective resource allocation in higher education requires reliable enrolment forecasts, but institutional planners frequently face data series disrupted by structural shifts and extreme data sparsity.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research should extend the proposed forecasting framework across additional institutions and planning contexts to evaluate how well the data-sparse workflow and leakage-safe covariate protocols transfer beyond a single case study setting.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for establishing external validity and generalizability of zero-shot time series foundation models and covariate-conditioning protocols in higher education planning.

**Evidence:** The first is broader validation across additional institutions and planning contexts to assess how well the forecasting workflow transfers beyond a single case setting.

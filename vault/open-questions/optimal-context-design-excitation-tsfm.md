title: Optimal Context Design and Excitation
---

**Background:** Context design is a central pillar for enabling time-series foundation models to perform accurate zero-shot or fine-tuned system identification for control.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work should determine how to optimally retain control-informative observations in context and precisely quantify how much excitation is required for reliable input-output identification.

**Why It Matters:** Addressing this question helps minimize operational disruptions caused by active system excitation while maximizing model fidelity for predictive control.

**Evidence:** how to retain control-informative observations and how much excitation is required for reliable input-output identification remain open questions.

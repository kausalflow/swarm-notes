title: Statistical Uncertainty Quantification for Estimators
---

**Background:** Persistent homology provides a topological framework to detect dynamical transitions from time series, but robust statistical uncertainty quantification for these topological estimators remains underdeveloped.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing statistical uncertainty quantification for the critical-parameter estimator, including repeated-noise experiments, bootstrap or subsampling strategies adapted to dependent time-series data, and finite-sample confidence statements for topological transition localization, remains an open direction.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for establishing confidence intervals and statistical rigor when applying topological data analysis to empirical and noisy physical time series.

**Evidence:** A first direction is the development of statistical uncertainty quantification for the critical-parameter estimator, including repeated-noise experiments, bootstrap or subsampling strategies adapted to dependent time-series data, and finite-sample confidence statements for topological transition localization.

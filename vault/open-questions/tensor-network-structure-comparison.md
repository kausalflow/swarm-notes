title: Comparative Analysis of Tensor Network Structures
---

**Background:** Tree Tensor Network Reservoir Computing utilizes hierarchical tree structures as random reservoirs for temporal learning tasks.

**Question / Future Work:** Conduct a comprehensive comparative analysis of performance and computational costs across different tensor network structures, such as Matrix Product States and Tree Tensor Networks, on various time-series prediction benchmarks.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for understanding the inductive biases and computational trade-offs of different tensor network topologies in reservoir computing.

**Evidence:** Although this study focused on a comparison between TTN-RC and the ESN baseline, a detailed comparative analysis of the performance and computational costs among different tensor network structures on various tasks remains an important topic for future work.

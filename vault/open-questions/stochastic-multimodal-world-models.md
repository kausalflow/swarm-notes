title: Stochastic and Multimodal World Models
---

**Background:** World models construct latent states through joint-embedding predictive architectures (JEPAs) that predict future or hidden target states in representation space.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending orthogonal predictive factorization and state synthesis to stochastic forecasting regimes, such as modeling multimodal futures, continuous physical fields, or pixel-based closed-loop control environments.

**Why It Matters:** Enables joint-embedding predictive architectures to handle uncertainty and multimodal distributions over future states in complex world-modeling environments.

**Evidence:** Finally, the present predictor is deterministic and does not explicitly represent multimodal futures. ... Pixel-based closed-loop control, stochastic futures, continuous physical fields, and tasks with known causal factors would provide complementary tests.

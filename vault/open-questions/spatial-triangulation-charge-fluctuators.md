title: Spatial Triangulation of Charge Fluctuators
---

**Background:** Discrete charge fluctuations and random telegraph noise in semiconductor quantum dot devices severely limit qubit performance and coherence, requiring robust methodologies to model multi-level fluctuators and their spatial interactions.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine the precise physical locations and spatial configurations of interacting charge fluctuators through advanced multi-level fluctuation analysis and triangulation techniques in semiconductor quantum dot architectures.

**Why It Matters:** Spatial triangulation of charge fluctuators is crucial for connecting abstract noise models to physical defect locations in semiconductor heterostructures, directly informing material growth and device fabrication improvements to mitigate charge noise.

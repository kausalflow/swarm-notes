**Background:** Large language model probability judgments lack human baseline comparisons regarding directional optimism bias.

**Question / Future Work:** Evaluate directional probability biases and alignment effects in language models against a matched human baseline, extending current internal coherence metrics to human psychological standards.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for understanding whether model optimism mirrors human cognitive biases or represents distinct machine artifacts.

**Evidence:** Skew measures internal asymmetry against the complementarity axiom rather than deviation from human judgment; a matched human baseline (Weinstein, 1980) remains future work.

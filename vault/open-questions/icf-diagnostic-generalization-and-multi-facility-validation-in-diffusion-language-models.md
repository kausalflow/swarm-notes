title: ICF Diagnostic and Facility Generalization
---

**Background:** Inertial confinement fusion prediction surrogates map laser pulses and target designs to neutron-rate waveforms, but expanding validation to alternative diagnostics and facilities remains an open challenge.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work involves extending the evaluation and application of decomposition-guided diffusion language models from neutron-rate diagnostics to other critical inertial confinement fusion diagnostics, such as X-ray imaging and neutron time-of-flight, as well as testing generalization across facilities with different driver geometries.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for establishing whether the proposed physics-typed decomposition and diffusion language modeling approach is generalizable across diverse experimental setups and diagnostic modalities in fusion research.

**Evidence:** First, all experimental data come from a single ICF facility and a single diagnostic, the neutron-rate waveform; generalization to other diagnostics (X-ray imaging, neutron time-of-flight) and to facilities with different driver geometries is left to future work.

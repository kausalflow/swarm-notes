title: Multivariate and Multi-step Joint Tail Calibration
---

**Background:** Probabilistic time-series forecasting models often rely on marginal conditional cumulative distribution function calibration, which does not inherently guarantee optimal performance for multistep or multivariate joint tail dependencies.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend structured halting mechanisms and diffusion-based shrinkage methods to handle multi-step forecasting horizons, multivariate joint tails, energy scores, variogram scores, and asymmetric decision losses.

**Why It Matters:** Moving beyond marginal CDF calibration to handle multivariate joint distributions and complex multi-step dependency structures is a crucial open challenge for probabilistic time-series forecasting.

**Evidence:** Moreover, our theory calibrates marginal conditional CDFs, not multistep or multivariate joint tails. Extending structured halting to energy or variogram scores and asymmetric decision losses is therefore the main next step.

title: Image Resolution and Continual Learning in TSAD
---

**Background:** Evaluating the impact of image resolution on detection performance and exploring continual learning scenarios remain open challenges in vision-based time series anomaly detection.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how varying image resolutions affect anomaly detection performance, evaluate continual learning frameworks for non-stationary streams, and develop data-driven window selection strategies.

**Why It Matters:** Addresses fundamental scaling and adaptation bottlenecks when bridging computer vision backbones with time series analysis workflows.

**Evidence:** In the future, we aim to study the impact of image resolution on detection performance, explore continual learning scenarios, and leverage data-driven window selection.

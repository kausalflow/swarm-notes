title: Asymptotic Normality for Estimated Transport Maps
---

**Background:** Extending asymptotic normality results for transport map estimators from fully observed settings to cases involving estimated or partially observed maps remains challenging due to nonlinear error propagation.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending the asymptotic normality results of autoregressive transport map (ATM) parameters to scenarios where the underlying maps are discretely or partially observed—thereby requiring estimation from empirical grid observations—remains an important open challenge, as the estimation error propagates nonlinearly through the composition and contraction operations.

**Why It Matters:** In real-world applications, probability distributions are rarely observed continuously; extending theoretical asymptotic guarantees to estimated maps is vital for practical validity.

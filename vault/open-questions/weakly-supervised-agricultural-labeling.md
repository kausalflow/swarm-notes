title: Weakly Supervised Agricultural Labeling
---

**Background:** Automated agricultural monitoring frameworks face challenges regarding label noise and dependency on administrative agricultural declarations.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the integration of weakly supervised learning, self-training, and vision-language foundation models to automatically infer semantic information directly from street-level imagery, thereby reducing the reliance on potentially noisy administrative declarations and generating reliable pseudo-labels for unannotated observations.

**Why It Matters:** Addressing label noise and reliance on administrative ground truth is critical for scaling automated agricultural monitoring systems across diverse geographical regions without manual data collection bottlenecks.

title: Dynamic Agentic Orchestration Optimization
---

**Background:** Agentic time series forecasting frameworks coordinate multiple decision steps, including forecasting model selection, context retrieval, and reflective evaluation, but often rely on heuristic or rigid orchestration strategies.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how to dynamically optimize agentic orchestration policies (such as the Fast-Slow-Reflect workflow) via end-to-end multi-turn reinforcement learning under diverse temporal dynamics and shifting data regimes, moving beyond static distillation and fixed multi-step reward shaping.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for improving the autonomy, adaptability, and sample efficiency of LLM-based agents in time-dependent environments.

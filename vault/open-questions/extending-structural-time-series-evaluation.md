title: Extending Structural Time Series Evaluation
---

**Background:** Time series foundation models are predominantly evaluated using static leaderboards with single aggregate scores, which obscure fine-grained capabilities across diverse structural conditions and input relationships.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the extension of capability-focused evaluations (such as CaFE) to broader relational and covariate conditions, incorporating more diverse data sources, multi-seed training recipes, and controlled calibration methodologies for response metrics.

**Why It Matters:** Broadening structural evaluation frameworks to handle complex relational and covariate settings is crucial for developing robust, multi-dimensional benchmarking standards for time series foundation models.

**Evidence:** Future work includes extending to relational and covariate conditions, more sources, and multi-seed recipes, along with controlled calibration of the response metric.

title: Probabilistic Temporal Predictive Multiplicity
---

**Background:** Models with near-identical predictive performance in time-series forecasting can yield substantially different forecast trajectories across multiple horizons, a phenomenon known as temporal predictive multiplicity.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend temporal predictive multiplicity frameworks to quantile, probabilistic, and distributional forecasts to capture uncertainty and distributional disagreement among near-optimal models.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for reliable decision-making under uncertainty, as models with identical aggregate point performance may assign radically different probabilities to tail events.

**Evidence:** extending the framework to quantile and probabilistic forecasts would enable the study of distributional disagreement.

title: Richer Retrieval Geometries and Covariates
---

**Background:** Retrieval-augmented forecasting relies on finding relevant past-future windows from a reference set to adapt time series foundation models to new domains.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and develop richer retrieval geometries, including multivariate and covariate-informed retrieval settings, to extend the applicability and performance of closed-form aligned in-context learning.

**Why It Matters:** Extending alignment-based retrieval-augmented forecasting to multivariate and covariate-rich time series is crucial for real-world deployment where univariate assumptions do not hold.

**Evidence:** Natural future directions are richer retrieval geometries, including multivariate and covariate-informed settings, and replication of the retriever × alignment ablation across additional backbones to test the generality of the attribution.

title: Testing wavelength dependence of stellar convection models
---

**Background:** Stellar oscillation and granulation amplitudes exhibit strong wavelength dependence governed by convective energy transport and surface temperature perturbations.

**Question / Future Work:** Future investigations need to test how well 3D convection models and theoretical scaling relations predict the time and wavelength dependence of granulation across diverse stellar types and wider infrared passbands, particularly to refine 1D stellar structure model grids and account for spectral feature variability induced by granulation.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for validating 3D convective models from first principles, improving 1D stellar evolutionary grids, and mitigating systematic noise floors in multi-wavelength exoplanet transmission and phase-curve spectroscopy.

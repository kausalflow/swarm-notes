title: High-Dimensional Nonstationary Change Point Detection
---

**Background:** Change point detection methods are traditionally developed under stationary noise assumptions or simple linear processes, limiting their application to genuinely nonstationary time series with complex temporal dynamics.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research should explore adapting the bootstrap-assisted robust binary segmentation (BARBS) framework to high-dimensional time series settings.

**Why It Matters:** High-dimensional change point detection under nonstationary and dependent noise is an important and challenging frontier in modern statistics and machine learning.

**Evidence:** Another interesting extension is to adapt the BARBS framework to high-dimensional time series.

title: Integrating Perceptual Modalities in Forecasting
---

**Background:** Media-bridged time series forecasting integrates numerical time series with diverse auxiliary media such as natural language narratives.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the unified media-bridged time series forecasting framework to integrate perceptual modalities including raw images, audio, and video as auxiliary information.

**Why It Matters:** Extending text-based narrative flows to rich perceptual modalities (images, audio, video) is a major architectural step toward truly general multimodal time series forecasting.

**Evidence:** For future research, we may extend to integrate perceptual modalities (e.g., images, audio and video).

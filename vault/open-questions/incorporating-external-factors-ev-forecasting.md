title: Incorporating External Factors in EV Forecasting
---

**Background:** Electric vehicle charging demand forecasting relies heavily on station-specific operational constraints and internal load dynamics, while broader external influences remain underexplored.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the behavior-guided forecasting framework by explicitly incorporating external behavioral factors, such as real-time pricing signals, weather conditions, and regional traffic mobility patterns, to improve generalizability across broader and more diverse charging environments.

**Why It Matters:** Incorporating exogenous drivers is crucial for scaling load forecasting frameworks to real-world smart grid applications where weather and price signals heavily perturb user behavior.

**Evidence:** Future work will incorporate external behavioral factors and investigate the generalizability of the framework across broader charging environments.

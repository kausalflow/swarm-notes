title: Adaptive Koopman Rank and Time-Varying Operators
---

**Background:** Time-series forecasting models using Koopman operator theory often rely on static latent transition operators or fixed low-rank subspaces estimated from finite training data.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work can extend this framework by allowing the Koopman rank and transition operator to adapt dynamically across time, and by developing advanced uncertainty mechanisms that separate random temporal perturbations from genuine structural changes in underlying system dynamics.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for handling non-stationary time series and distribution shifts where a fixed low-rank transition operator fails to capture evolving system physics.

**Evidence:** Future work can extend this direction by allowing the Koopman rank and transition operator to adapt across time, and by developing uncertainty mechanisms that separate random perturbations from genuine changes in the dynamics.

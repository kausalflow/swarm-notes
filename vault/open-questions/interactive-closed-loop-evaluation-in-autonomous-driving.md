title: Interactive Closed-Loop Evaluation in Autonomous Driving
---

**Background:** Autonomous driving models rely on future prediction world models, but current approaches are frequently evaluated in non-reactive simulation settings without interactive agent feedback.

**Question / Future Work:** Transitioning from non-reactive simulation to interactive closed-loop evaluation is critical for validating the robustness and safety of foresight-guided autonomous driving world models under realistic multi-agent interactions.

**Why It Matters:** Testing under interactive closed-loop settings remains a key requirement for verifying whether foresight-guided planning generalizes past open-loop playback.

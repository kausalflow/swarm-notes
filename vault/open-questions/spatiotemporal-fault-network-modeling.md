title: Spatiotemporal Fault Network Modeling
---

**Background:** Integrating multi-physics equations with AI-assisted manifold detection enables deterministic earthquake forecasting from geodetic displacement data, yet current frameworks lack full spatial network coordination and local rupture degrees of freedom.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work must incorporate higher degrees of freedom for localized rupture and implement full network station coordinates to gain a spatiotemporal understanding of the entire fault system, thereby resolving spatial variance observed at distant stations such as PAWA.

**Why It Matters:** Extending physics-informed machine learning models from single-station temporal forecasting to fully coupled spatiotemporal network triangulation is vital for accurately resolving rupture extents and spatial limits across complex subduction zones.

**Evidence:** This spatial variance can be addressed in future studies by incorporating higher degrees of freedom for localized rupture and implementing full network station coordinates to gain a spatiotemporal understanding of the entire fault system.

title: Unsupervised Concept Discovery for Classification
---

**Background:** Time series regression models often lack interpretability or depend on rigid global assumptions, while concept-based architectures typically require manual concept annotations that are unavailable in most time series domains.

**Question / Future Work:** Explore the extension of annotation-free, localized concept discovery models to time series classification tasks, where temporal localization and concept-level reasoning are equally valuable.

**Why It Matters:** Extending concept bottleneck and mask-based architectures from regression to classification broadens their applicability across diverse time series downstream tasks.

**Evidence:** Finally, extending MAGNETS to time series classification, where temporal localization and concept-level reasoning are equally valuable, represents a natural next step.

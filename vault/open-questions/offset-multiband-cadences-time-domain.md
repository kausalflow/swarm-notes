title: Offset Multi-band Cadences Needed
---

**Background:** Extragalactic time-domain surveys typically utilize single-band or identically timed multi-band observations, limiting the independence of period uniqueness checks and window-function disambiguation.

**Question / Future Work:** Future extragalactic time-domain programs would benefit from intentionally offset multi-band cadences that provide complementary temporal constraints.

**Why It Matters:** Using identical multi-band cadences limits the effectiveness of multi-band data for resolving aliases and confirming period uniqueness, a critical bottleneck for ground- and space-based time-domain astronomy.

**Evidence:** First, although the M101 series is two-band, the F555W and F814W cadences are very similar, so their window functions are effectively the same. Two-band agreement therefore confirms coherent color behavior, but it does not provide a fully independent test of period uniqueness... Future extragalactic time-domain programs would benefit from intentionally offset multi-band cadences that provide complementary temporal constraints.

title: Extending TSWM Supervision and Control
---

**Background:** Time series world models (TSWMs) are evaluated on prediction error under executed plans, leaving their response to unexecuted plans and counterfactual action shifts largely unverified.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate extending directional supervision mechanisms to handle event-based actions, provide rigorous clinical validation of declared pharmacological action-state signs, and evaluate model performance and safety under closed-loop control deployment.

**Why It Matters:** Extending consistency supervision to discrete/event actions and closed-loop control environments is critical for safe deployment in high-stakes domains like clinical care and autonomous engineering systems.

**Evidence:** Future work includes extending the supervision to event actions, clinical validation of the declared pharmacological signs, and evaluation under closed-loop control.

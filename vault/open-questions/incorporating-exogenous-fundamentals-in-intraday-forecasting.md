title: Exogenous Fundamentals in Intraday Forecasting
---

**Background:** Continuous intraday electricity markets rely on orderbook dynamics and order interactions to determine short-term price movements under uncertainty.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the integration of periodic exogenous variables related to supply and demand conditions alongside trades from neighboring delivery periods to further improve intraday price forecasting accuracy.

**Why It Matters:** Incorporating exogenous fundamental data alongside orderbook microstructure can bridge the gap between technical market microstructure models and fundamental power-system economic drivers.

**Evidence:** Thus, as CID trading for sequential delivery periods occurs in parallel, incorporating trades from neighboring delivery periods, as well as periodic exogenous variables related to supply and demand conditions would be expected to further improve forecasting.

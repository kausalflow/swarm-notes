title: Advanced Directions in Electronics Forecasting
---

**Background:** Forecasting resale prices of second-hand electronics is critical for subscription-based business models where pricing errors directly translate into financial risk.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate hybrid architectures that combine deep learning time-series models with explicit probabilistic or conformal uncertainty quantification, multimodal extensions incorporating textual listing descriptions and product images, cross-market evaluations across different regulatory and platform structures, and the adaptation of large time-series foundation models for zero-shot forecasting in sparse, volatile resale markets.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for advancing operational pricing risk management and determining whether large pre-trained time-series foundation models can overcome the data sparsity and high volatility inherent in second-hand alternative markets.

**Evidence:** Future research should explore: (i) hybrid architectures with explicit probabilistic or conformal uncertainty quantification; (ii) multimodal extensions incorporating textual listing descriptions or product images; (iii) cross-market evaluation to assess generalisability beyond the Polish market; and (iv) time series foundation models such as Chronos Ansari et al., 2024a and TimesFM Das et al., 2024a as zero-shot baselines.

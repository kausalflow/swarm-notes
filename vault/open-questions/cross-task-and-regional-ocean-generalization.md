title: Cross-Task and Regional Ocean Generalization
---

**Background:** Multimodal ocean modeling requires integrating diverse observation sources such as satellite imagery, gridded fields, and in situ buoy measurements, but learning unified, transferable representations across these heterogeneous data structures remains an open challenge.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and develop broader cross-task adaptation, cross-regional generalization, and robust modeling approaches capable of handling incomplete and heterogeneous ocean observations across diverse marine environments.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for ensuring that ocean foundation models can generalize effectively beyond regional case studies and cope with missing, noisy, or sparse multi-sensor marine data.

**Evidence:** broader cross-task and cross-regional evaluations remain necessary to assess its generalization capabilities

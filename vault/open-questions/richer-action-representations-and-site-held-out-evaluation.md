title: Richer Action Representations and Evaluation
---

**Background:** Many time-series forecasting models in intensive care units depend on future clinical actions (such as vasopressor administration) alongside past observations, yet existing hybrid or recursive frameworks either accumulate error during rollout or fail to effectively condition predictions on alternative treatment sequences.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work should explore richer action representations, model uncertainty, and evaluate performance on prospective or site-held-out cohorts across diverse hospital settings to better understand generalization and causal treatment effects.

**Why It Matters:** Evaluating action-conditioned forecasting models on site-held-out or prospective cohorts is critical to verify clinical generalizability and prevent performance degradation across diverse hospital environments.

**Evidence:** Future work should evaluate richer action representations, predictive uncertainty, and prospective or site-held-out cohorts.

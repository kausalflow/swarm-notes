title: Resolution and Backbone Dependence
---

**Background:** Weather forecasting models often rely on short input windows that neglect unresolved atmospheric processes and history dependence, prompting the use of temporal memory and learned corrections.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine how the benefit of non-Markovian memory-based corrections depends on varying spatial resolutions and different choices of neural network backbones in global weather forecasting.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for understanding the generalizability and scalability of hybrid memory-augmented correction architectures across diverse operational weather prediction systems.

**Evidence:** How this benefit depends on spatial resolution and the choice of backbone remains an open question.

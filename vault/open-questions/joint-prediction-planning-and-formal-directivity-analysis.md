title: Joint Prediction-Planning and Directivity Analysis
---

**Background:** Autonomous mobile robot navigation frameworks often decouple environment prediction from motion planning or rely on heuristic-based cost functions that lack formal analysis.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate formal analytical properties of the directivity cost function and develop joint prediction-planning architectures that simultaneously forecast future robot motions and occupancy grid maps to account for their interactive feedback loops.

**Why It Matters:** Bridging the gap between separate environmental forecasting and trajectory optimization with formal guarantees is crucial for safety-critical mobile robotics in dynamic settings.

**Evidence:** Future work will investigate the formal analysis of the directivity cost and joint prediction framework that simultaneously predicts future robot motions and OGMs, enabling more flexible planning that accounts for their interactions.

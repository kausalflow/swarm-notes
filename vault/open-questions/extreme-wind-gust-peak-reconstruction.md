title: Extreme Wind Gust Peak Reconstruction
---

**Background:** Data-driven models struggle to accurately reconstruct extreme high-speed wind gust peaks during severe typhoon events due to data scarcity and high nonlinearity.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate advanced modeling techniques, data augmentation strategies, and cost-sensitive learning approaches to better capture and reconstruct extremely high-intensity wind gust peaks during severe tropical cyclones, where current deep learning architectures tend to underpredict peak magnitudes.

**Why It Matters:** Accurate reconstruction of peak gusts is critical for structural safety and wind turbine fatigue mitigation under severe typhoon conditions.

**Evidence:** Furthermore, WDANet still exhibits limitations in reproducing extremely high wind gust peaks. Similar behavior is also observed in other deep learning models evaluated in this study, suggesting that accurate extreme peak reconstruction remains a challenging problem for data-driven approaches.

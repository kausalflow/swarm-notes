title: Observer-Validated Appearance Fidelity Evaluation
---

**Background:** Mesopic and low-light display appearance transforms rely on a per-pixel scotopic-to-photopic luminance ratio (S/P) driving signal, but existing formulations are evaluated solely against physical spectral values rather than human visual perception.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work must perform psychophysical evaluations, such as haploscopic matching, to validate the appearance fidelity and visual adequacy of mesopic display transforms driven by closed-form S/P estimators.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for confirming whether physical closed-form approximations of S/P translate to actual perceptual accuracy in mesopic image display applications.

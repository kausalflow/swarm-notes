title: Atmosphere-Ocean Coupling Bias in AMIP Simulations
---

**Background:** Quantifying temporal variations in Earth's climate feedback parameter over the historical period often relies on atmosphere-only simulations forced by prescribed sea-surface temperatures and sea-ice concentration without interactive ocean-atmosphere coupling.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine whether atmosphere-only models with prescribed sea-surface temperatures and sea-ice concentrations can accurately replicate the true feedback response and variability of fully coupled climate systems when accounting for omitted atmosphere-ocean coupling feedbacks.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the structural limitations of atmosphere-only prescribed SST simulations is critical for constraining transient climate sensitivity and feedback parameters from historical observational records.

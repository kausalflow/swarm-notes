title: Conditional Bias Mitigation in Volatility Forecasting
---

**Background:** Volatility forecasting models are traditionally evaluated using aggregate accuracy metrics such as RMSE and MAE, which often obscure conditional failures and systematic biases during unstable market regimes.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work involves leveraging latent-regime audit frameworks to design and train calibration and forecasting methodologies that explicitly minimize conditional bias and tail underprediction in high-risk financial regimes.

**Why It Matters:** This direction bridges the gap between post-hoc model auditing and active model training, enabling the creation of risk-aware forecasting architectures tailored for extreme market conditions.

**Evidence:** Future work can use these audits to design calibration and forecasting methods that directly reduce conditional bias in high-risk regimes.

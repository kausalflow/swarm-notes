title: Cross-Scenario Generalization for Heat Flux Reconstruction
---

**Background:** Directly reconstructing divertor heat-flux profiles from multi-source macroscopic plasma-state signals in magnetic-confinement fusion devices remains challenging regarding cross-scenario generalization and real-time operational efficiency.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work must expand the dataset coverage, improve model generalization across different tokamak devices and operational scenarios, and further optimize reconstruction efficiency for online deployment.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for transitioning machine-learning-based divertor monitoring systems from offline benchmarks to reliable online control tools in burning-plasma devices.

**Evidence:** Future work will expand the dataset, improve cross-scenario generalization, and further enhance reconstruction efficiency.

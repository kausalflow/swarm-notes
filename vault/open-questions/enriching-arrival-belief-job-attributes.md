title: Enriching Arrival Belief Representations
---

**Background:** Dynamic scheduling methods require anticipating future job arrivals to optimize nonlinear tardiness objectives without incurring excessive computational overhead or relying on rigid parametric assumptions.

**Question / Future Work:** Explore the integration of richer job-level information, such as processing routes, priorities, or machine requirements, into the exogenous arrival belief representation to improve guidance when future workloads are highly heterogeneous.

**Why It Matters:** Capturing fine-grained job attributes rather than coarse aggregate counts could significantly enhance decision-making quality in heterogeneous, highly complex shop environments.

**Evidence:** However, richer representations that additionally capture job attributes, such as processing routes, priorities, or machine requirements, may provide more informative guidance when future workloads are highly heterogeneous.

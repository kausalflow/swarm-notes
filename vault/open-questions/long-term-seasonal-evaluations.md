title: Long-Term Seasonal and Academic Cycle Evaluation
---

**Background:** Network traffic forecasting and anomaly detection models often rely on short temporal datasets that fail to capture annual academic cycles and long-term seasonal trends.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend temporal observation windows beyond multi-week durations to capture long-term seasonal effects and variations tied to annual academic calendars and large-scale scientific campaigns across backbone networks.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for evaluating the temporal stability and concept drift resilience of security baseline models in critical infrastructure over extended operational periods.

**Evidence:** First, our 57-day temporal scope cannot capture long-term seasonal effects tied to annual academic cycles; extended longitudinal studies are needed.

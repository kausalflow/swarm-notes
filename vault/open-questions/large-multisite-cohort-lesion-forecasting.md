title: Multi-Site Cohort Lesion Forecasting
---

**Background:** Prospective spatial forecasting of future multiple sclerosis lesions relies on baseline MRI scans, but determining whether patient-specific deep learning models outperform population-prior atlases requires validation on larger datasets.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work involves investigating the integration of baseline lesion masks and atlas-informed models, as well as evaluating prospective lesion forecasting architectures across larger multi-site cohorts to improve statistical power and generalizability.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for establishing whether deep learning models can genuinely extract patient-specific predictive signals for multiple sclerosis lesion formation beyond population priors.

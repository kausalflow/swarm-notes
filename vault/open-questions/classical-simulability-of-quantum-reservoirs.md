title: Classical Simulability of Quantum Reservoirs
---

**Background:** Programmable quantum annealers can function as large-scale quantum reservoirs for temporal information processing, but evaluating their capabilities requires understanding whether their finite-time out-of-equilibrium dynamics can be efficiently simulated classically.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine whether, and at what accuracy and computational cost, state-of-the-art classical dynamical simulations can reproduce the experimentally accessible reservoir map generated by large-scale quantum annealers.

**Why It Matters:** This question is technically important because establishing the classical simulability of large-scale out-of-equilibrium quantum machine learning models remains a central challenge in quantum advantage research.

**Evidence:** Determining whether, and at what accuracy and computational cost, state-of-the-art classical dynamical simulations can reproduce the experimentally accessible reservoir map is an important direction for future work.

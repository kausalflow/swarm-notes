title: Computational Efficiency and Multi-Record Alignment
---

**Background:** Bayesian synchronization frameworks align noisy paleoclimate proxy records with reference chronologies, but existing computational implementations face challenges regarding execution efficiency and scaling to complex multi-record workflows.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work will focus on improving the computational efficiency of BSync through optimized implementations in compiled languages, exploring more refined approaches for modeling prior information from Bayesian age-depth models, and extending the framework to the joint alignment of multiple proxy records and mixed targets.

**Why It Matters:** Improving computational efficiency and multi-record joint alignment capabilities is critical for scaling Bayesian proxy synchronization tools to handle large-scale paleoclimate data compilations and fully end-to-end uncertainty quantification workflows.

**Evidence:** Future work will focus on improving the computational efficiency of BSync, through optimized implementation in a compiled language. We will also explore more refined approaches for modeling prior information from Bayesian age–depth models and for extending the framework to joint alignment of multiple proxy records, i.e mixing multiple targets.

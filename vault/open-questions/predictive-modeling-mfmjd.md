title: Predictive Modeling and Forecasting
---

**Background:** Extending parameter estimation methodologies for mixed fractional jump-diffusion processes toward predictive modeling and price forecasting is essential for practical financial engineering applications.

**Question / Future Work:** While the current framework successfully establishes parameter estimation, model fit, and residual diagnostics for mixed fractional Merton jump-diffusion models, future efforts should be directed toward developing out-of-sample forecasting and asset price prediction methodologies using the estimated parameters.

**Why It Matters:** Bridges the gap between theoretical econometric estimation and practical quantitative trading or risk management applications.

**Evidence:** Also, since this research focuses mainly on empirical validation and establishment of facts, we suggest that further research should be conducted on forecasting and prediction of the stock prices.

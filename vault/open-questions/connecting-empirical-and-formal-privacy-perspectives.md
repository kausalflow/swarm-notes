title: Connecting Empirical and Formal Privacy
---

**Background:** Evaluating privacy-preserving synthetic time series generation relies primarily on distance-based empirical metrics such as Distance to Closest Record (DCR) and Nearest Neighbour Distance Ratio (NNDR), which lack formal differential privacy guarantees.

**Question / Future Work:** Connecting empirical privacy metrics (like DCR and NNDR) with formal differential privacy guarantees remains an open challenge in privacy-preserving time series generation, as distance-based measures do not provide provable bounds on adversarial inference.

**Why It Matters:** Bridging empirical proximity metrics and formal differential privacy is crucial for ensuring that released synthetic time series carry mathematically rigorous privacy guarantees rather than just heuristic protection.

**Evidence:** Connecting empirical and formal privacy perspectives remains an open direction.

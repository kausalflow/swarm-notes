title: Causal Disentanglement in Counterfactual Rollouts
---

**Background:** E-commerce demand forecasting often suffers from autoregressive inertia and confounded state transitions when evaluating counterfactual merchant action schedules.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigating advanced causal decoupling and structural identifiability methods to further isolate endogenous market evolution from exogenous shocks during multi-step counterfactual rollouts and decision-conditioned simulations.

**Why It Matters:** Important for bridging offline sequence modeling with rigorous causal inference in dynamic, intervention-driven environments.

**Evidence:** CEDAR framework leverages this structural equation to achieve an effective approximation of the interventional distribution... through architectural causal disentanglement.

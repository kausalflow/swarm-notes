title: Predictive Phase Space Forecasting
---

**Background:** Complex dynamical systems lacking closed-form analytical descriptions require data-driven basis constructions that preserve nonlinear attractor geometries while scaling effectively to large, high-dimensional time-series corpora.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing robust predictive frameworks that utilize phase space manifold geometries and transport plan trajectories for quantitative forecasting remains an open challenge, as current architectures primarily demonstrate retrospective regime detection rather than forward-looking prediction.

**Why It Matters:** Extending geometric time-series embeddings from diagnostic and exploratory tools into predictive models is critical for translating topological data analysis methods into practical forecasting applications across economics, finance, and physics.

**Evidence:** While the basis is a strong regime detector, prediction is not demonstrated here and remains the main open direction.

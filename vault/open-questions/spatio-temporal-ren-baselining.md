title: Spatio-Temporal Network Security Baselines
---

**Background:** Many high-speed network traffic forecasting methods rely on independent univariate modeling per router, ignoring network-wide spatial dependencies and multi-site routing dynamics.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend forecasting and anomaly-aware security baselining frameworks to incorporate graph neural networks or multi-variate spatial architectures that capture inter-router dependencies across global backbone networks.

**Why It Matters:** Important for improving global anomaly detection accuracy by leveraging multi-router spatial topologies rather than isolated single-node observations.

**Evidence:** Second, our univariate, per-router approach serves as a necessary first line of defense, but ignores broader network-wide spatial dependencies.

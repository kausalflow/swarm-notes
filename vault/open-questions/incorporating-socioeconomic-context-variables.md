title: Incorporating Socioeconomic Context Variables
---

**Background:** Spatiotemporal urban forecasting models often struggle to accurately capture residential patterns for specific demographic groups due to complex, unmodeled socioeconomic factors.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research can incorporate additional contextual variables such as policy frameworks, economic conditions, or historical development patterns to improve prediction accuracy for minority and hard-to-predict demographic populations.

**Why It Matters:** Addressing demographic disparities and improving minority population prediction accuracy is crucial for equitable and unbiased urban planning.

**Evidence:** The reduced accuracy for certain demographic groups (particularly minority populations) may reflect the complex, non-spatial factors influencing residential patterns. Future research could incorporate additional contextual variables such as policy frameworks, economic conditions, or historical development patterns.

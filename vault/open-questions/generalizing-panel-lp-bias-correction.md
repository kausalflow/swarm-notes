title: Generalizing Panel LP Bias Correction
---

**Background:** Panel local projections are widely used in macro-finance to estimate impulse response functions, but standard fixed-effect estimators suffer from weak-exogeneity bias when control variables are persistent.

**Question / Future Work:** The theoretical analysis assumes a stylized framework in which structural shocks are directly observed by the researcher. Extending the half-panel jackknife bias-correction and bias-correction-adjusted standard error procedures to more general panel local projection settings—such as those with multiple persistent control variables, generated regressors, or population-residualized regressors—remains an open direction for future research.

**Why It Matters:** Extending bias correction and valid inference methods to richer, more realistic panel local projection specifications is crucial for empirical macroeconomics where structural shocks are unobserved or estimated via panel VARs.

**Evidence:** A formal analysis of these more general settings is left for future research.

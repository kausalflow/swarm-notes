title: Extending Physics-Guided Regularization Strategies
---

**Background:** Atmospheric water budget diagnostics provide useful physical constraints for precipitation forecasting but introduce uncertainties related to spatial, temporal, and physical-model approximations.

**Question / Future Work:** Further research is needed to extend asymmetric physics-guided regularization strategies to a wider variety of atmospheric variables, physical diagnostics, and diverse climatic regimes while isolating the individual contributions of multiscale physical constraints.

**Why It Matters:** Extending physics-guided constraints to broader atmospheric variables improves the generalizability and robustness of hybrid data-driven physical models across different climate zones.

**Evidence:** More broadly, the asymmetric physics-guided formulation could be extended to other atmospheric variables and physical diagnostics.

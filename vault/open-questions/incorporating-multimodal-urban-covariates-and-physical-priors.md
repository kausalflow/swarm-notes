title: Multimodal Urban Atmospheric Modeling
---

**Background:** Air quality forecasting models often rely on limited environmental covariates and fail to fully account for how multi-modal external factors intervene in atmospheric dynamics.

**Question / Future Work:** Explore urban sensing scenarios supported by multi-source heterogeneous big data to investigate how to integrate physical priors with multi-modal features, thereby further enhancing model reasoning capabilities within complex urban atmospheric systems.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for advancing air quality prediction models from purely historical/univariate-multivariate time series approaches to robust, physically grounded multi-modal urban environmental systems.

**Evidence:** In the future, we expect to extend our research to urban sensing scenarios supported by multi-source heterogeneous big data. We aim to investigate how to integrate physical priors with multi-modal features, thereby further enhancing the model’s reasoning capabilities within complex urban atmospheric systems.

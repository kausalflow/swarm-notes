title: Prospective Forecasting Architectures and Leagues
---

**Background:** Evaluating the forecasting capabilities of large language models (LLMs) on real-world sports events typically relies on static retrospective datasets, leaving open how models synthesize continuously evolving, real-time information under uncertainty.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work includes extending the benchmarking platform to regularly cover additional competitions (such as the English Premier League and German Bundesliga), testing advanced agent architectures, exploring more sophisticated retrieval procedures and tool uses, and examining alternative prompting strategies that incorporate intermediate calculations or consistency checks.

**Why It Matters:** Extending live prospective benchmarks across diverse sports leagues and refining agentic tool use and prompting strategies are crucial for understanding the limits and robustness of LLM-based forecasting in dynamic real-world environments.

**Evidence:** Our platform itself is flexible, and we are currently extending it to regularly cover additional competitions, including the English Premier League and German Bundesliga... This provides many opportunities for future research to examine alternative prompting strategies, more advanced agent architectures, retrieval procedures, and additional forms of tool use.

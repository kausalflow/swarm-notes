title: Cross-Chain External Validity and Temporal Generalization
---

**Background:** Evaluating forecasting models and digital asset datasets within specific blockchain ecosystems requires understanding their external validity and cross-chain transferability.

**Question / Future Work:** Assessing cross-chain external validity remains a critical open direction, requiring replication of the proposed data design and forecasting benchmarks on other blockchain ecosystems such as EVM and non-EVM chains, as well as evaluating temporal generalization through forward-rolling vintages beyond the initial study window.

**Why It Matters:** Determining whether specialized blockchain dataset structures and forecasting models generalize across different Layer-1 or Layer-2 network architectures is vital for building universally robust financial and on-chain predictive systems.

**Evidence:** Assessing cross-chain external validity is left to future work, including replication on EVM and non-EVM chains and temporal generalization tests using forward-rolling vintages beyond our study window.

title: Predicting Large-Scale Synchronization Transitions
---

**Background:** Reproducing collective behaviors and synchronization transitions from data for large-scale networked dynamical systems remains an open challenge in machine learning and nonlinear dynamics.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing data-driven models that can accurately capture emergent collective properties and synchronization transitions across large-scale coupled oscillator systems remains a fundamental open problem.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding how to scale data-driven predictions to large-scale collective phenomena is essential for building reliable digital twins of complex networked systems.

**Evidence:** Collective behaviors such as synchronization transitions are emergent properties of coupled oscillator systems, and how to reproduce them from data for large-scale systems remains an open problem.

title: Joint Dependence in AI Weather Models
---

**Background:** Data-driven AI weather forecasting models have achieved high skill on global metrics but require rigorous evaluation of their probabilistic performance and physical consistency on rare, extreme events like tropical cyclones.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how the capabilities of probabilistic weather forecasting models emerge across training phases, and how marginal versus joint training objectives influence the recovery of physical joint dependence structures such as the wind-pressure relationship in extreme storms.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding whether joint physical relationships emerge from structural model constraints or specific training objectives is crucial for designing reliable AI architectures for extreme weather forecasting.

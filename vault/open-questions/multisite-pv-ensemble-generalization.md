title: Multisite PV Ensemble Generalization
---

**Background:** Short-term photovoltaic power forecasting ensembles combine heterogeneous predictors, but determining the optimal subset of experts and validating their transferability across unseen solar sites remains an open methodological challenge.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work must examine forward residual fitting, reduced expert sets, matched computational budgets, and longer multisite evaluations with verified forecast reception times to determine how smaller, optimized configurations transfer to unseen plants.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for moving beyond site-specific heuristic ensembles toward generalizable, computationally efficient photovoltaic forecasting systems.

**Evidence:** Future work should examine forward residual fitting, reduced expert sets, matched computational budgets, and longer multisite evaluations with verified forecast reception times.

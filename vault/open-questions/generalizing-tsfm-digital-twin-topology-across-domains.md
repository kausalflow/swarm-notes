title: Generalizing TSFM Topological Grounding
---

**Background:** Time-series foundation models are increasingly applied to diverse downstream industrial workflows, but evaluating their efficacy across broader task varieties and industrial domains remains an open empirical challenge.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate whether the proposed topology-informed fusion approach and findings yield comparable performance gains on alternative downstream predictive maintenance tasks and diverse industrial application domains beyond turbofan engine prognostics.

**Why It Matters:** Essential for validating the generalizability and domain-independence of grounding time-series foundation models in digital twin topological knowledge across heterogeneous industrial systems.

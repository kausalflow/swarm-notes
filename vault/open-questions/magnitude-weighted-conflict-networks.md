title: Magnitude-Weighted Conflict Networks
---

**Background:** Conflict event records capture timestamps and locations, but lack direct measurement of event magnitude and cross-country spatiotemporal interactions due to incomplete fatality reporting and organization-level attribution limits.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research should incorporate complete fatality data to construct magnitude-weighted time series for improved quantification of conflict intensity, and develop country-level conflict networks by systematically mapping organization-level actors and countries using entity-matching approaches such as large language models.

**Why It Matters:** Addressing event magnitude weighting and cross-country conflict networks is critical for moving beyond simple frequency baselines to capture the true intensity and propagation dynamics of regional conflicts.

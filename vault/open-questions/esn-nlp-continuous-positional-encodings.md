title: ESNs for NLP and Continuous Positional Encodings
---

**Background:** Reservoir computing and Echo State Networks are predominantly evaluated on continuous time-series forecasting and dynamical systems, leaving their boundaries on discrete sequence modeling and content-aware natural language tasks less explored.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how distributional, architectural, and optimization variants of Echo State Networks perform on content-aware tasks in natural language processing and analyze their behavior when processing continuous positional encodings rather than discrete one-hot vectors.

**Why It Matters:** Expanding reservoir computing to natural language processing and continuous positional encodings bridges the gap between randomized recurrent models and modern sequence models like Transformers and state-space models.

**Evidence:** Next steps will focus on exploring how our insights apply to content-aware tasks in natural language processing, investigate potential synergies with modern linear-attention frameworks, and analyze how distributional, architectural, and optimization ESN variants behave when processing continuous positional encodings rather than discrete one-hot vectors.

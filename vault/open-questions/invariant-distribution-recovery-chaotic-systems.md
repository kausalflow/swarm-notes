title: Invariant Distribution Recovery in Chaos
---

**Background:** Time series foundation models primarily output marginal quantiles or point forecasts, which leaves joint temporal and cross-variable dependencies uncaptured and creates significant challenges when recovering the invariant distributions of chaotic systems.

**Question / Future Work:** Accurately recovering the true invariant distribution of complex chaotic dynamical systems over long horizons remains an open challenge, as current generative models and foundational trajectory forecasting approaches still exhibit significant divergence from held-out ground truth dynamics.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for evaluating the physical and dynamical fidelity of generative time series foundation models across chaotic and complex real-world domains.

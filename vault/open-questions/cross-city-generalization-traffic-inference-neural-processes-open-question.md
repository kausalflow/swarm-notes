title: Cross-City Generalization in Traffic Inference
---

**Background:** Global traffic state inference (GTSI) relies on fusing heterogeneous data sources such as floating car data and sparse fixed-detector measurements across urban road networks.

**Question / Future Work:** Evaluate the cross-city transferability and generalization capabilities of neural process frameworks for global traffic state inference when applied to urban road networks with distinct topological structures and sensing configurations.

**Why It Matters:** Determining whether neural process models can generalize across cities is critical for deploying scalable, out-of-the-box traffic state inference systems without requiring extensive localized retraining or data collection.

**Evidence:** First, the evaluation is performed on a single city; cross-city generalization therefore remains untested due to limited data, despite the neural process framework’s theoretical ability to facilitate transfer.

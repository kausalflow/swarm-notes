title: Stability-Plasticity Trade-off in ECTS
---

**Background:** Early Classification of Time Series (ECTS) systems must operate in dynamic environments subject to distribution shifts, requiring models to balance rapid adaptation against the retention of previously learned concepts.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the theoretical bounds of the stability-plasticity trade-off and explore additional forms of non-stationarity, such as drifting cost functions or evolving class priors in early classification systems.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding this trade-off is critical for preventing catastrophic forgetting while adapting to streaming distribution shifts.

**Evidence:** Future work could include a more rigorous theoretical analysis of the stability–plasticity trade-off in ECTS, as well as the study of additional forms of non-stationarity, such as drifting cost functions or evolving class priors.

title: Asymptotic Normality and Statistical Inference
---

**Background:** The paper introduces a multilevel main effects matrix factor model (MMEFM) for grouped matrix-valued time series, establishing its identification, estimation algorithms via iterative projection, and asymptotic convergence rates under piecewise-stationary linear processes.

**Question / Future Work:** The paper establishes convergence rates under specific assumptions and provides simulation studies, but does not explore or establish the exact central limit theorem (CLT) or asymptotic normality distributions for the estimators of the global grand means, main effects, common factors, and factor loading matrices. Future work could investigate the asymptotic distribution theory to enable formal statistical inference and confidence interval construction.

**Why It Matters:** Establishing central limit theorems and asymptotic normality is crucial for valid statistical inference, hypothesis testing, and constructing confidence intervals for the estimated parameters in matrix factor models.

**Evidence:** arXiv:2610.18335v1 [stat.ME] 13 Oct 2026 ... As a generalization of a multilevel matrix factor model, we provide rigorous definitions of MMEFM and identifications, together with iterative algorithms for the estimators of all global and local components... with rates of convergence spelt out.

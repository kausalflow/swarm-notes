title: Continuous Intrinsic Mutual Information
---

**Background:** Computing intrinsic mutual information for continuous random variables remains challenging due to the difficulty of establishing a proper repertoire of potential transformations over which to minimize conditional mutual information.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing efficient, scalable, and exact methods for calculating or estimating intrinsic mutual information and secret-key agreement rates in continuous and high-dimensional random variables remains an important open methodological challenge.

**Why It Matters:** Essential for extending multimodal information flow analysis beyond discrete time series to continuous physical and biological dynamical systems.

**Evidence:** there are significant difficulties in computing the intrinsic mutual information for continuous random variables. In essence, and barring a particularly clever and unforeseen method, one must establish a repertoire of potential transformations to apply to the variable Z.

title: Generalizing Aperiodic TSF Models
---

**Background:** Time-series forecasting models often struggle with aperiodic visuo-motor datasets where exogenous variables heavily depend on complex scenarios.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and explore simpler yet more effective neural network layers or architectural components to generalize aperiodic multimodal time-series forecasting models across diverse domains beyond robotics.

**Why It Matters:** Improving generalization and discovering lightweight architectural modules for aperiodic time series is crucial for expanding visuo-motor forecasting beyond robotics.

**Evidence:** We also noticed that the aperiodic data is still limited especially when exogenous variables highly depend on scenarios and other simple yet effective layers are left unexplored, which may further simplify our model and generalize it to different domains.

title: Preserving Temporal Variation in Code-Based Forecasting
---

**Background:** Code-based editing frameworks for time series forecasting often struggle to preserve fine-grained temporal variations and local fluctuations while enforcing numerical constraints.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work involves developing agentic forecasters that couple numerical constraint enforcement with more flexible mechanisms for preserving high-frequency temporal variation, fine-grained fluctuations, and realistic local dynamics during forecast editing.

**Why It Matters:** Addressing this limitation is crucial for ensuring that code-assisted time series forecasting agents produce realistic, high-fidelity trajectories rather than overly smooth or plateaus-dominated paths.

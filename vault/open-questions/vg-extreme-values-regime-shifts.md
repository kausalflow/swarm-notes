title: Visibility Graphs for Regime Shifts
---

**Background:** Complex dynamical systems exhibit extreme fluctuations that serve as crucial precursors to regime shifts and dynamic transitions, but characterizing these events in nonstationary processes remains methodologically challenging.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how visibility graph-based identification of extreme values connects to system-specific transitions, such as regime shifts or the onset of exogenous events, across various domains like climate science, finance, and neuroscience.

**Why It Matters:** Connecting network-based extreme value detection directly to physical regime shifts and dynamical tipping points bridges abstract topological time-series features with concrete mechanistic insights in complex systems.

**Evidence:** For future work, it will be interesting to analyze how the VG-based identification of extreme values relates to system-specific events such as regime shifts or the onset of exogenous events.

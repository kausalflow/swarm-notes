title: Continuous Alternatives in Self-Normalised Changepoint Tests
---

**Background:** Self-normalising change point tests in time series commonly fail to remain pivotal when the underlying noise process exhibits general non-stationarity, as standard multiplicative self-normalisation assumes a scaled Brownian motion limit.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending global self-normalised change-point testing frameworks to continuous trend deviations, general smooth alternatives, or wider classes of nonstationary dependent processes remains an open challenge requiring alternative normalisation functionals.

**Why It Matters:** Extending self-normalised change detection beyond piecewise constant means to continuous alternatives under non-stationarity is crucial for broad applicability in nonparametric time series econometrics and climatology.

**Evidence:** For smooth deviations from constancy, the denominator of the test statistic does not shrink, and the procedure is inconsistent, so other normalisations may be more natural.

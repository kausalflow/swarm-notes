title: Asymptotic Regime with Growing Trajectories
---

**Background:** Statistical methods for count time series network inference often rely on specific asymptotic frameworks, such as assuming many independent short trajectories or a fixed number of trajectories with increasing lengths.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending the current asymptotic theory, which focuses on an increasing number of independent short trajectories, to an asymptotic regime with a fixed number of trajectories and increasing trajectory lengths under suitable conditions on temporal dependence remains an important open direction.

**Why It Matters:** Theoretical results for growing trajectory lengths instead of growing sample sizes would broaden the applicability of sparse latent VAR network recovery to long-term ecological or biological time series data.

**Evidence:** It would also be useful to study an asymptotic regime with a fixed number of trajectories and increasing trajectory lengths, under suitable conditions on temporal dependence, complementing our current theory for many short trajectories.

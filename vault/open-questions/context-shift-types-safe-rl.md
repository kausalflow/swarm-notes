title: Taxonomy of Context Shifts in Safe RL
---

**Background:** Reinforcement learning agents deployed in nonstationary environments must adapt safety constraints over time, but existing methods often rely on fixed specifications or reactive updates.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how distinct types of context shifts (such as covariate shift, label shift, concept shift, or simultaneous combinations of visible and latent shifts) affect the validity, conservativeness, and responsiveness of context-dependent safety constraints in nonstationary reinforcement learning.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the interaction between specific distribution shift categories and context-dependent constraint generation is crucial for developing robust, theoretically sound safe reinforcement learning algorithms for unconstrained real-world environments.

title: Data Frequency and Calibration Size
---

**Background:** Probabilistic forecasting methods convert point predictions into full predictive distributions or quantiles using error post-processing techniques across various time frequencies and domains.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work needs to investigate how data frequency, calibration-sample size, and probabilistic forecast performance interact when extending error post-processing frameworks to intermediate and lower frequencies such as weekly, quarterly, or yearly time series.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the impact of data frequency and sample size on post-processing helps determine the boundary conditions and generalizability of in-sample versus out-of-sample calibration strategies across diverse application domains.

**Evidence:** Examining the relationship between data frequency, calibration-sample size, and probabilistic forecast performance remains an important direction for future research.

title: Unseen Parameters and Geometries Flow Forecasting
---

**Background:** Recursive neural surrogates for complex fluid flows suffer from accumulated phase, amplitude, and transport errors over long rollout horizons.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending future-aligned latent dynamics and motion inference methodologies to zero-shot generalization across unseen physical parameters and completely new geometric configurations remains an important open challenge for complex-flow forecasting surrogates.

**Why It Matters:** Evaluating generalization to unseen physical parameters and geometries is crucial for validating whether recursive latent dynamics surrogates can operate robustly outside training domains.

**Evidence:** Natural extensions are forecasting at unseen parameters and in new geometries, three-dimensional flows and longer horizons.

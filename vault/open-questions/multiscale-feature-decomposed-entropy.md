**Background:** Time-series complexity metrics typically compute a single scalar value, conflating temporal and amplitude variations without theoretical bounds or explicit multiscale feature decomposition.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing multiscale extensions of feature-decomposed entropy metrics to attribute feature-specific complexity across multiple time scales, alongside establishing rigorous theoretical bounds and principled parameter selection guidelines.

**Why It Matters:** Extending feature-decomposed entropy to multiple scales is critical for robust analysis of complex physiological and geophysical signals where temporal dynamics operate across hierarchical frequency bands.

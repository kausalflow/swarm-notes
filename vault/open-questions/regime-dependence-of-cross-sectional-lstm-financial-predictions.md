title: Regime-Dependence of Cross-Sectional LSTMs
---

**Background:** Predicting financial asset returns using Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks is complicated by low signal-to-noise ratios, non-stationarity, and market efficiency, raising questions about how architectural enhancements like sector embeddings and macro-financial covariates perform across different economic regimes.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how deep learning architectures incorporating cross-sectional heterogeneity and temporal components perform across shifting macroeconomic regimes, and determine the conditions under which sector-level momentum strategies break down during major market rotations or crises.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the regime-dependence of machine learning anomalies and cross-sectional representations in finance is critical for bridging the gap between static backtests and deployable algorithmic strategies.

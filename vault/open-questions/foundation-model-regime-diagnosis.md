title: Foundation Model Regime Diagnosis
---

**Background:** Forecasting foundation models and deep neural networks are predominantly trained using globally pooled optimization objectives that rely on fixed statistical loss priors, leading to systematic train-deploy performance gaps.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the pathology-coverage audit framework to evaluate dataset industrial readiness, apply the Regime-wise Relative Bias Vector (RBV) diagnostic protocol to emerging time-series foundation models, and design regime-aligned training objectives grounded in quantified trade-offs.

**Why It Matters:** This connects diagnostic auditing frameworks directly with the evaluation and alignment of modern zero-shot time-series foundation models and robust loss design.

**Evidence:** future work can extend the pathology-coverage audit to dataset industrial readiness, apply RBV to diagnose emerging forecasting foundation models, and design regime-aligned training objectives grounded in quantified trade-offs.

title: Scaling Causalized Granger Causality
---

**Background:** Granger causality often struggles with high computational complexity when applied to high-dimensional systems due to the necessity of conditioning on extensive past states across numerous variables.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work needs to focus on optimizing and scaling the causalized Granger causality (c-GC) framework to effectively handle high-dimensional datasets without prohibitive computational overhead.

**Why It Matters:** Scalability to high dimensions is a persistent bottleneck in time-series causal structure learning and vector autoregressive methods.

**Evidence:** Future work will focus on implementing c-GC to scale effectively for high-dimensional datasets.

title: Foundation Model Latent Structure Consistency
---

**Background:** Model-based closed-loop neural stimulation is limited by prediction failures across sessions and heavy per-session calibration requirements.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine whether the shared latent structure of neural stimulation responses is sufficiently consistent across substantial biological heterogeneity to support a common foundation model.

**Why It Matters:** Determines the feasibility of scaling meta-learning and pretraining approaches into universal cross-subject and cross-modality foundation models for neural stimulation.

**Evidence:** The key open question is whether the shared latent structure of stimulation responses is sufficiently consistent across the substantial biological heterogeneity of such a corpus to support a common prior.

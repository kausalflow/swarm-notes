title: Improving Uncertainty Interpretation and Actionability
---

**Background:** Machine learning predictions inherently contain uncertainty, but standard visualization formats like prediction intervals are frequently misunderstood by non-expert users and lack actionable integration with real-world workflows.

**Question / Future Work:** Explore how to design alternative uncertainty representations and interaction modalities (such as frequency-based formats, hypothetical outcome plots, or progressive disclosure designs) that improve both the understandability of prediction uncertainty and its direct actionability for professional decision-makers in domains like healthcare workforce planning.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for bridging the gap between statistical machine learning outputs and human-centered decision support, ensuring that uncertainty information is correctly interpreted and practically useful rather than confusing or confidence-reducing.

**Evidence:** Future work should address several open challenges. Alternative uncertainty representations, such as frequency-based formats or hypothetical outcome plots, should be explored to better support intuitive interpretation. Additionally, future work should investigate design and interaction approaches for explaining prediction uncertainty in ways that improve both understandability and actionability.

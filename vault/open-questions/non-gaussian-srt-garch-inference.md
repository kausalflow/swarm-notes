title: Non-Gaussian Extensions for SRT-GARCH
---

**Background:** The semiparametric real-time GARCH (SRT-GARCH) model combines a smooth deterministic long-run volatility component with a stationary short-run process driven by contemporaneous return innovations under the assumption of Gaussian innovations.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the exact-likelihood analysis and boundary inference of the semiparametric real-time GARCH model beyond Gaussian innovations to allow for more flexible treatment of heavy-tailed and asymmetric returns.

**Why It Matters:** Important for generalizing time-series volatility models to real-world financial data that frequently exhibit non-Gaussian features, heavy tails, and asymmetries.

**Evidence:** Second, extending the exact-likelihood analysis beyond Gaussian innovations would broaden the applicability of the model and allow more flexible treatment of heavy-tailed and asymmetric returns.

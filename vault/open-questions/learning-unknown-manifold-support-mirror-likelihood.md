title: Learning Unknown Manifold Support
---

**Background:** Manifold-valued data analysis often requires likelihood-based extrinsic approaches using generalized projections or generator functions like the Fenchel mirror map.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research directions involve extending the mirror likelihood framework to scenarios where the data manifold is unknown, partially known, or learned dynamically rather than specified a priori.

**Why It Matters:** Extending manifold-based models to learn or adapt to unknown supports is crucial for handling real-world noisy data where the precise manifold constraint is misspecified or missing.

title: Relational Link Prediction and Recommendation
---

**Background:** Relational foundation models are presently limited in their capacity to handle tasks outside standard forecasting or table-level attribute prediction.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate extending relational foundation model architectures to support complex relational tasks such as link prediction and recommendation.

**Why It Matters:** Link prediction and recommendation are core tasks in relational learning and knowledge graph reasoning; enabling foundational support for them is crucial for broad enterprise adoption.

**Evidence:** RT-J is unable to solve link prediction and recommendation tasks, sharing this limitation with RT [23].

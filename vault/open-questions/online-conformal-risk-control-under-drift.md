title: Online Conformal Risk Control Under Drift
---

**Background:** Machine learning models deployed in telecommunication networks encounter severe non-stationarity and distribution shifts that degrade decision-making reliability.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing robust online conformal prediction and risk control techniques that can maintain valid distribution-free guarantees under continuous concept drift and label shifts without requiring full data re-exchangeability.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for ensuring safety, robustness, and reliability of autonomous network management and control loops under real-world non-stationary telemetry.

**Evidence:** Under label shift, pooled batch calibration exceeds that tolerance structurally, by 10.9 standard errors on the production corpus. Online calibration restores compliance.

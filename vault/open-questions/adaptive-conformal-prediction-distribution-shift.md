title: Adaptive Conformal Prediction for Distribution Shifts
---

**Background:** Aggregators participating in ancillary service markets rely on probabilistic flexibility forecasting, but existing uncertainty quantification approaches are predominantly evaluated in single-output settings without accounting for multivariate multi-time-step constraints or robustness to input uncertainties.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and develop context-aware, adaptive conformal prediction methods or distribution-shift-robust uncertainty quantification frameworks that can dynamically adapt prediction intervals to changing input uncertainties and temporal forecast-error processes, thereby maintaining both empirical coverage and economic performance under severe input perturbations.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for ensuring that data-driven demand response aggregators maintain regulatory compliance and economic viability when deployed in volatile real-world operating environments characterized by persistent distributional shifts in load, solar, price, and activation signals.

**Evidence:** Future work should therefore investigate context-aware and adaptive conformal methods that can account for changing input uncertainty and distributional shifts, as well as more realistic temporally dependent forecast-error processes.

title: Evaluating Extreme Weather Extremes
---

**Background:** Probabilistic data-driven weather models optimized on point-wise continuous ranked probability scores often struggle to reliably predict high-intensity, rare meteorological events due to data sparsity and extreme event rarity.

**Question / Future Work:** Evaluating and improving model skill for extreme precipitation rates and rare meteorological extremes remains a major open challenge, as true positives become very sparse at high accumulation thresholds.

**Why It Matters:** Accurate forecasting of extreme weather events is vital for disaster mitigation and warning systems, making the extension of probabilistic models to high-intensity extremes an important open direction.

**Evidence:** For more extreme values, true positives become very sparse, making reliable evaluation difficult. For this reason, we leave the evaluation of extreme precipitation for future work.

title: Generalizing Control Foundation Models across Diverse Systems
---

**Background:** Control foundation models combine multi-experiment records and dynamic graph block diagrams to unify response prediction and control-input generation.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the evaluation and generalization of control foundation models across a wide range of target systems, multi-input multi-output (MIMO) configurations, and joint learning setups involving heterogeneous physical domains such as linear systems and rigid-body arms.

**Why It Matters:** Demonstrating cross-system and cross-domain generalization is crucial for establishing the practical viability and transferability of control foundation models in diverse real-world engineering tasks.

**Evidence:** Ongoing work applying these results to a wide range of target systems is also beginning to bear fruit... This work has already been extended to unseen system instances, multi-input multi-output (MIMO) systems, and joint learning from linear systems and rigid-body arms, and we are evaluating its generalization across different systems and signal dimensions.

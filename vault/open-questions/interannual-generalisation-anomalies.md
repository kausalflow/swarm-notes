title: Mitigating Interannual Climate Anomalies
---

**Background:** Operational crop mapping models are frequently evaluated under conditions of temporal distribution shifts, but existing approaches do not fully resolve why anomalous weather years or interannual phenological variability cause severe degradations in accuracy.

**Question / Future Work:** While temperature-based phenological alignment and temporal positional encodings mitigate some year-to-year distribution shifts, substantial performance drops persist during anomalous years such as 2024, indicating that current temporal generalisation strategies do not fully account for interannual environmental variability.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding and bridging performance gaps during climate anomalies is critical for deploying robust, operational national agricultural monitoring systems across diverse multi-year weather patterns.

title: Extending to Imagined Speech
---

**Background:** Non-invasive brain-to-text decoders relying on word-aligned continuous neural recordings often exploit overlapping input windows as a timing shortcut, inadvertently learning word durations rather than true neural representations of speech.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work should focus on extending independent word decoding and multimodal integration methods to internal speech generation paradigms, such as imagined speech or inner monologues, where word onset times and external auditory stimuli are entirely absent.

**Why It Matters:** Moving from perceived speech to imagined speech is the ultimate goal for clinically viable non-invasive brain-computer interfaces, making the transferability of these decoding frameworks a critical open direction.

**Evidence:** More importantly, extending these results to decoding internally generated speech, such as imagined speech, will be a key step towards practical non-invasive communication interfaces.

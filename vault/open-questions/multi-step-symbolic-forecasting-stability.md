title: Multi-Step Symbolic Forecasting Stability
---

**Background:** Symbolic forecasting models are predominantly evaluated on rolling-window nowcasting (one-step-ahead prediction) settings, leaving multi-step-ahead forecasting performance and the temporal stability of discovered equations under iterative forecasting unexplored.

**Question / Future Work:** Evaluate multi-step-ahead forecasting performance using symbolic machine learning models and investigate the stability and error accumulation of discovered algebraic equations under iterative forecasting over extended horizons.

**Why It Matters:** Assessing multi-step-ahead iterative stability is vital for determining whether symbolic equations can serve as true generative dynamical models rather than short-term autoregressive smoothers.

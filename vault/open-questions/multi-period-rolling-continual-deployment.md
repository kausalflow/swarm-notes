title: Multi-Period Rolling Continual Deployment
---

**Background:** Adapting forecasting systems to changing regimes traditionally requires costly weight retraining, raising the question of whether continual learning for multimodal time series can be achieved via lightweight prompt evolution.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate whether multi-period rolling continual deployment loops can successfully manage stability-plasticity trade-offs and mitigate long-term forgetting in text-evolved guidance artifacts without relying on periodic weight retraining.

**Why It Matters:** Extending continual deployment from single executed cycles to robust multi-period evaluations is essential to prove the practical viability of training-free prompt evolution in production environments subject to continuous structural breaks.

title: Generalizing Visual World Models
---

**Background:** Predictive world models for robotic manipulation leverage joint-embedding predictive architectures (JEPAs) to connect visual observations and scene geometry with action-conditioned state transitions.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate extending geometry-grounded state representations and action-conditioned world models to handle moving-camera setups, zero-shot transfer across diverse robotic embodiments, and broader variations in objects and workspaces.

**Why It Matters:** Important for scaling visual world models to unconstrained environments where camera motion and morphological changes are prevalent.

**Evidence:** Moving-camera prediction, zero-shot transfer across embodiments, and broader object and workspace changes remain outside the demonstrated scope.

title: Statistical Inference for Discrete Morse Theory
---

**Background:** Statistical inference for discrete Morse theory on graphs remains undeveloped due to the challenges of quantifying uncertainty and asymptotic distributions over topological skeletons.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop formal statistical inference methods and asymptotic guarantees for discrete Morse theory and topological skeletons on graphs, bridging low-dimensional topology and mathematical statistics.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for establishing rigorous confidence intervals and hypothesis testing for topological features extracted from complex networks.

**Evidence:** We find that one of the main current limitations of the proposed ideas on the Morse skeleton is the lack of formal statistical inference. However, this is a fundamental question at the interface of low dimensional topology and mathematical statistics, and we leave it a standalone future research direction.

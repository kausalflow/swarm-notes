title: Nonlinear Time-Varying Carbon Sinks
---

**Background:** The Global Carbon Budget dataset summarizes Earth's carbon cycle through time series for atmospheric CO2 concentrations, anthropogenic emissions, and land and ocean sinks, which can be modeled using cointegrated vector autoregressive frameworks assuming linear relationships.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and model the Global Carbon Budget using frameworks that account for time-dependence, nonlinearity, and potential sink saturation in the relationship between carbon sinks and atmospheric concentrations.

**Why It Matters:** As global temperatures rise and carbon sinks approach potential saturation points, assuming linear cointegrating relationships becomes less accurate; developing nonlinear and time-varying cointegrated systems is crucial for robust long-term climate projections.

**Evidence:** Analyzing the GCB data in a model that allows for time-dependence and nonlinearity in the relation between the sinks and atmospheric concentrations is an interesting topic for future research.

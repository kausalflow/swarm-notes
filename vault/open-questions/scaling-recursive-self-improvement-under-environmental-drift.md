title: Scaling Recursive Self-Improvement Under Environmental Drift
---

**Background:** Recursive self-improvement in AI agents requires living, open-ended environments that continuously supply verifiable tasks, yet studying these systems presents challenges regarding environment non-stationarity, task quality, and long-horizon stability.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how to broaden the sources of experience in both quantity and quality when resolvable, informative events are rare or strongly culture-dependent, and address challenges related to skill forgetting, time-weighting, and failure detection as the world drifts and underlying regularities shift.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding how to sustain recursive self-improvement under data scarcity and environmental drift is crucial for scaling autonomous learning agents beyond narrow domains or predictable task distributions.

**Evidence:** The first concerns the experience the environment supplies, its fuel: where resolvable, informative events are rare or strongly culture-dependent, trajectories stay thin, so broadening the sources of experience in both quantity and quality is a natural way to raise the ceiling of the recursive loop.

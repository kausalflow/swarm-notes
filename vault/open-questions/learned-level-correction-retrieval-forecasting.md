title: Learned Level Correction for Retrieval
---

**Background:** Multivariate time-series forecasting models struggle on non-stationary series at long horizons due to level drift in retrieved future sequences.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop a learned level correction mechanism for retrieved futures to prevent level drift and improve forecasting accuracy on non-stationary time-series data at long horizons.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for expanding retrieval-augmented forecasting to non-stationary real-world domains where level shifts severely degrade performance.

**Evidence:** Future work includes a learned level correction of retrieved futures, better per-variate retrieval (App. I), approximate search, and forecasting-based anomaly detection.

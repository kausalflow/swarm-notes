title: Adaptive Partition Theory for Time Series
---

**Background:** Probabilistic time-series forecasting frameworks often rely on static partition structures or heuristic tuning parameters without formal asymptotic guarantees under general stochastic processes.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop formal analysis for adaptive partitions under suitable mixing and recurrence assumptions to establish theoretical guarantees for conditional regime analog forecasting methods.

**Why It Matters:** Theoretical rigor for adaptive partitioning and nonparametric conditional regime forecasting is crucial for understanding consistency and convergence properties in time-series prediction.

**Evidence:** Future work should provide large-scale benchmark evidence and formal analysis for adaptive partitions under suitable mixing and recurrence assumptions.

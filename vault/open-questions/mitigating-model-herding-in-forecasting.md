title: Mitigating Model Herding in Forecasting
---

**Background:** Evaluating large language models on prospective real-world forecasting tasks reveals that models often herd and track public consensus or betting markets rather than sharply differentiating themselves in predictive capability.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how to mitigate herding and convergence behaviors among frontier language models when performing prospective forecasting, such that multi-model systems or ensembles can capture diverse and non-consensus perspectives rather than converging on standard bookmaker lines or prototypical results.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding and overcoming model herding is crucial for developing robust forecasting agents that can provide independent, value-add insights beyond existing market consensus.

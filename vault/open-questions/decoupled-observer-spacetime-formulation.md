title: Decoupled Observer Space-Time Formulations
---

**Background:** Observer State-Space Models (OSSMs) introduce a recurrent forecasting framework that separates latent-state propagation from measurement correction, but current instantiations remain tied to specific linear or representation-space architectures.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research will focus on combining the proposed observer structure with stronger encoders and a more general, decoupled SpaceTime-compatible formulation where the innovation map and output readout are parameterized independently.

**Why It Matters:** Extending OSSMs with decoupled innovation maps and more powerful nonlinear encoders is crucial for generalizing observer-based state-space models beyond linear or tightly coupled state-space constraints.

**Evidence:** Future work can combine this observer structure with stronger encoders and a more general SpaceTime-compatible formulation.

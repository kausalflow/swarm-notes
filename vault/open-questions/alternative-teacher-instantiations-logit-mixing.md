title: Alternative Teacher Instantiations and Vocabulary-Bridging Logit Mixing
---

**Background:** Hinted scaling laws extrapolate benchmark performance across model scales by introducing a continuous sequence of easier versions of a benchmark using privileged information, yet optimizing these techniques across diverse post-trained model families remains challenging.

**Question / Future Work:** Exploring alternative teacher instantiations, vocabulary-bridging logit mixing variants, and broader classes of hinting mechanisms across diverse model architectures represents an open direction for future research.

**Why It Matters:** This is crucial because extending hinted scaling laws and logit mixing to post-trained models without tokenizer constraints is necessary for making the method broadly applicable across proprietary or diverse open-source ecosystems.

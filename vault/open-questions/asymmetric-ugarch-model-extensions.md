title: Asymmetric UGARCH Model Extensions
---

**Background:** Time series volatility modeling under uncertainty theory relies on symmetric frameworks, leaving asymmetric volatility responses unmodeled.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research should develop flexible extensions of the uncertain generalized autoregressive conditional heteroskedasticity (UGARCH) model, specifically incorporating asymmetric structures to capture differing impacts of positive and negative shocks on volatility under uncertainty theory.

**Why It Matters:** Capturing leverage and asymmetry effects is crucial for financial volatility forecasting and risk management under uncertainty theory, mirroring the historical evolution of classical GARCH models into EGARCH and GJR-GARCH.

**Evidence:** Future research may consider more flexible extensions of the UGARCH model (2.3). For example, asymmetric UGARCH models may be developed to capture different effects of positive and negative shocks on volatility.

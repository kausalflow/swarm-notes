title: Query-Dependent Multimodal Fusion
---

**Background:** Time series language models typically expose language models to a single visual or textual representation, imposing a fixed inductive bias across downstream tasks without fully capturing both absolute measurements and relative temporal dynamics.

**Question / Future Work:** Explore additional representations and encoder combinations, including more than two views and query-dependent or adaptive mechanisms for combining them dynamically based on the specific reasoning task.

**Why It Matters:** Developing adaptive or query-dependent multimodal fusion techniques is crucial for handling diverse time-series reasoning tasks that require different representational trade-offs.

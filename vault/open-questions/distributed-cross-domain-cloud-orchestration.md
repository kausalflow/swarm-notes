**Background:** Cloud orchestration frameworks must adapt to multi-domain and decentralized infrastructure topologies as modern workloads span distributed resources and cross-domain boundaries.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the closed-loop decision-focused learning and neural-guided optimization framework to distributed cross-domain computing environments and broader robust optimization scenarios.

**Why It Matters:** Extending DFL and cloud orchestration beyond single-cluster or localized data centers to heterogeneous, multi-domain computing environments remains an important frontier for modern large-scale distributed systems.

**Evidence:** Future work will focus on extending the framework to distributed cross-domain computing environments and broader robust optimization scenarios.

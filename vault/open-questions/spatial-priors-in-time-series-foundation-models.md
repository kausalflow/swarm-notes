title: Spatial Priors in Time-Series Foundation Models
---

**Background:** Pretrained time-series foundation models lack specialized spatial prior mechanisms required to capture complex local relation dynamics and cross-region contexts in unseen road networks.

**Question / Future Work:** Incorporate spatial prior knowledge into pretrained time-series foundation models, combining transferable local relation modeling with cross-region context interaction to enhance zero-shot forecasting on unseen road networks.

**Why It Matters:** Connecting specialized traffic forecasting inductive biases (such as local road heterogeneity and region partitions) with foundation models is a crucial step toward generalized, zero-shot spatio-temporal forecasting.

**Evidence:** In future work, we will explore how to incorporate spatial prior knowledge into pretrained time-series foundation models, combining transferable local relation modeling with cross-region context interaction to enhance zero-shot forecasting on unseen road networks.

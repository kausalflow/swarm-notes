title: Probabilistic Extension of Gated Correction Modules
---

**Background:** Deterministic long-term time series forecasting models typically output point estimates without probabilistic uncertainty or localized reliability indicators.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the lightweight plug-in correction and uncertainty-aware framework to full probabilistic forecasting architectures to provide comprehensive density estimations rather than point predictions alone.

**Why It Matters:** Transitioning deterministic residual correction modules to handle full probabilistic outputs is crucial for rigorous risk assessment and decision-making under uncertainty in long-term forecasting.

**Evidence:** Future work includes extending CARE to probabilistic forecasting frameworks, exploring mechanisms to calibrate the gate weights, and validating generalization under richer covariate information and large-scale practical settings.

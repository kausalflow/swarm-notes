title: Fully Coupled S2S Emulators
---

**Background:** Fully coupled atmosphere-ocean data-driven emulators are being explored to advance subseasonal-to-seasonal (S2S) forecasting systems beyond models driven by prescribed atmospheric forcing.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop and evaluate fully coupled atmosphere-ocean data-driven emulators to investigate bidirectional feedback mechanisms and enhance long-term predictability in subseasonal-to-seasonal forecasting systems.

**Why It Matters:** Coupled atmosphere-ocean feedback is crucial for accurate multi-week and seasonal climate prediction, as uncoupled emulators rely on prescribed atmospheric forcing and miss critical two-way interactions.

**Evidence:** At last, another direction of research should exploit a fully coupled S2S atmosphere-ocean emulator.

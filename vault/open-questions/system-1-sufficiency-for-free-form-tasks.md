title: System 1 Sufficiency for Free-Form Tasks
---

**Background:** Current time series reasoning frameworks rely heavily on decision settings with fixed candidate answers or predefined tasks, limiting their direct application to general-purpose time series modeling.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the formulation of System 1 sufficiency and compression frameworks from tasks with fixed candidate answers to free-form output tasks, including time series forecasting, missing value imputation, and open-ended question answering.

**Why It Matters:** Generalizing System 1 efficiency to generative and continuous tasks is critical for broad applicability across modern time series analytics beyond simple classification or multi-choice QA.

**Evidence:** First, our formulation of System 1 sufficiency focuses on tasks that require a decision over a fixed set of candidate answers... extending the framework to tasks with free-form outputs such as forecasting, imputation, or open-ended question answering remains an important direction.

title: Balancing Physics and Learning
---

**Background:** Hybrid structural system identification combines data-driven networks with physics-based reduced-order models or governing equations, but optimal allocation principles remain largely undefined.

**Question / Future Work:** Determining how to systematically balance explanatory power between prescribed physical operators and learnable neural network corrections without losing parameter identifiability remains an open research direction in hybrid structural system identification.

**Why It Matters:** Balancing physics and learning is central to building robust hybrid digital twins that generalize accurately beyond training conditions without overfitting to measurement noise or violating physical laws.

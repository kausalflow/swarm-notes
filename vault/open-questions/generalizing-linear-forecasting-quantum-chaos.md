title: Generalizing Linear Forecasting in Quantum Chaos
---

**Background:** Evaluating time-series forecasting models on specific quantum chaotic systems like the PXP model leaves open how findings extend across broader many-body physical settings.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine how broadly the robust performance of linear forecasting models compared to nonlinear architectures extends to other quantum many-body Hamiltonians exhibiting qualitatively different ergodic and non-ergodic regimes, as well as to digital quantum circuits and systems with non-Markovian memory effects.

**Why It Matters:** It is theoretically critical to understand whether the surprising efficacy of linear models over complex nonlinear transformers in quantum chaotic forecasting is a universal feature of many-body dynamics or specific to particular conserved sectors or entanglement structures.

**Evidence:** Establishing how broadly the observed behavior extends beyond this setting therefore remains an open question.

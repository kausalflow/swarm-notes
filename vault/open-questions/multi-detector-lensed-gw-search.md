title: Multi-Detector Lensed GW Searches
---

**Background:** Matched-filtering searches for gravitational waves must account for gravitational lensing effects across multi-detector networks and real observational data to effectively detect lensed signals.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending marginalized two-image lensed detection statistics to multiple detectors and running the pipelines on real LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA observational data remain important directions for future work.

**Why It Matters:** Demonstrates the practical viability and real-world performance of proposed search statistics on actual detector networks and observing runs.

**Evidence:** We leave extending our statistic to multiple detectors, beyond the estimate of the trials factors in Appendix G, and running it on real LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA data [1, 14, 15] to future work.

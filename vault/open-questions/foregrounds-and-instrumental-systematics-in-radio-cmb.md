title: Foreground and Systematic Limitations
---

**Background:** Foreground contamination and instrumental systematics such as mutual coupling present major obstacles in precision 21-cm cosmology and radio CMB experiments.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work must establish through end-to-end simulation whether realistic foreground cleaning, interferometer chromaticity, Planck residual systematics, and mutual coupling effects can be adequately managed within the error budget of high-redshift hydrogen optical depth measurements.

**Why It Matters:** Foreground residuals and hardware systematics are the primary limiting factors preventing confident extraction of cosmological radio signals, making end-to-end validation critical for determining instrumental requirements.

**Evidence:** A robust map-level, end-to-end analysis that incorporates realistic radio foregrounds, the chromatic response of the interferometer, and Planck residual systematics is therefore the essential next step in establishing the near-term feasibility of this measurement.

title: Adaptive Lags and Hidden Confounders
---

**Background:** Causal discovery from nonlinear multivariate time series in high-dimensional systems is computationally challenging because candidate directed relations grow quadratically with the number of variables.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work aims to develop adaptive lag selection, adaptive task sampling, and explicit modeling of hidden confounders to overcome limitations such as sensitivity to hidden common causes and collinear non-parents.

**Why It Matters:** Important for extending scalable meta-learning causal discovery frameworks to handle realistic real-world settings with unobserved confounding and unknown temporal delays.

**Evidence:** Future work will study adaptive lag selection, adaptive task sampling, and explicit modeling of hidden confounders.

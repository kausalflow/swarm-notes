title: Spread-Skill Relationship in Ensembles
---

**Background:** The spread-skill relationship in meteorological forecasting demonstrates that ensemble forecast variance can be indicative of future prediction error, but the empirical relationship between ensemble variance and error in multi-model infectious disease forecasting remains inadequately understood.

**Question / Future Work:** Further investigation is required to thoroughly understand the spread-skill relationship of multi-model epidemic forecasting ensembles, particularly regarding how component forecast variability relates to future prediction errors across diverse datasets and alternative collections of component models.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the spread-skill relationship in epidemiological ensembles is vital for reliably quantifying prediction uncertainty and assigning confidence intervals to public health forecasts during active outbreaks.

**Evidence:** The spread.-skill relationship of ensembles that we have uncovered also requires more investigation, in particular in the context of other component models and datasets.

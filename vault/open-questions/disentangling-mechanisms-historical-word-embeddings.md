title: Disentangling Drivers of Historical Stereotype Shifts
---

**Background:** Historical word embeddings and text corpora are increasingly used to quantify the long-term evolution of social stereotypes and cultural discourse across major historical transitions.

**Question / Future Work:** Disentangling whether observed shifts in historical word embeddings are driven by direct effects of institutional reforms, demographic shifts in actual population participation, or broader transformations in cultural discourse remains an unresolved challenge.

**Why It Matters:** This question highlights the fundamental identification problem in computational cultural sociology and digital humanities when using text corpora to study historical social change.

**Evidence:** We note, however, that the present analysis cannot distinguish between three related mechanisms: the observed changes may reflect the direct effect of institutional reforms, demographic shifts in women’s actual participation in work and political life, or broader transformations in cultural discourse.

title: High-Resolution Gas Giant Atmospheric Modeling
---

**Background:** Atmospheric models of giant planets currently face limitations in line lists and higher spectral resolutions, restricting the detailed exploration of giant planet science cases.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop advanced atmospheric models at higher spectral resolutions (R > 1,000) incorporating expanded line lists and additional species (such as refractories and isotopologues) to fully unlock the potential of moderate-to-high-resolution spectroscopy for characterizing gas giant atmospheres.

**Why It Matters:** Essential for improving retrieval accuracy, detecting minor/refractory species, and extracting formation constraints from high-contrast observations.

**Evidence:** However, the detailed exploration of giant planet science cases is limited by the availability of atmospheric models, in particular at sufficient spectral resolution (R > 1,000). This is due, in part, to limitations of the line lists [113].

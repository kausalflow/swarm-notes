title: Optimal Multi-Scale Loss Hyperparameters
---

**Background:** Machine-learned weather prediction models often struggle to properly constrain small-scale spatial variability when trained with standard scale-unaware scoring rule objectives.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine the optimal set of hyper-parameters—including the number of scales, smoothing filters, and scale-weighting strategies—for multi-scale loss formulations in various prediction tasks such as global weather forecasting, long-range forecasting, and downscaling.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for extending multi-scale proper score optimization to diverse meteorological applications and spatial resolutions without manual hyper-parameter tuning bottlenecks.

**Evidence:** More work will be required to assess what the best set of hyper-parameters is for weather forecasting.

title: Asynchronous Federated Traffic Forecasting
---

**Background:** Federated spatial-temporal traffic forecasting frameworks rely on synchronous client participation rounds, limiting applicability to real-world edge settings with varying device availability and dropouts.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work should extend federated spatial-temporal forecasting frameworks to support asynchronous client participation, accommodating intermittent availability and heterogeneous update frequencies across distributed nodes.

**Why It Matters:** Asynchronous federated learning is critical for real-world deployment where clients join and drop out dynamically, yet standard spatial-temporal architectures currently assume synchronized communication rounds.

**Evidence:** Future work may aim extend the framework for asynchronous participation and stronger formal privacy mechanisms for boundary-message exchange.

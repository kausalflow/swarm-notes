title: Improving Long-Horizon Human-Agent Collaboration Understanding
---

**Background:** Real-world professional work involves long-horizon, interdependent workflows where humans collaborate with AI agents across diverse software environments, requiring models to anticipate contribution adoption and judge collaboration quality over time.

**Question / Future Work:** Explore methods to improve multimodal large language models' capabilities in understanding long-horizon human-agent collaboration by constructing specialized training datasets or developing sandbox environments where models can practice collaborating with simulated users before real-world deployment.

**Why It Matters:** This direction addresses a fundamental limitation in current frontier models—their inability to accurately forecast human adoption and evaluate collaboration quality in long-horizon professional workflows—which is critical for deploying reliable collaborative agents.

**Evidence:** As a future work, we will explore ways to improve these capabilities by constructing training datasets or developing sandbox environments where models can practice collaborating with simulated users before real-world deployment.

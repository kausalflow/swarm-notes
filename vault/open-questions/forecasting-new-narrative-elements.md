title: Forecasting New Narrative Elements
---

**Background:** Forecasting structural developments in dynamic text-derived networks currently focuses on predicting relations between existing entities, leaving the arrival of entirely unobserved narrative elements unaddressed.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend narrative network forecasting architectures to anticipate the cold-start arrival of brand-new actors, entities, and actions alongside new relational structures between already established elements.

**Why It Matters:** Essential for real-world crisis and news forecasting applications where breaking developments constantly introduce previously unknown actors and events.

**Evidence:** We predict relations between elements already present in the window and do not model the arrival of entirely new elements, which is a distinct cold-start problem... forecasting the arrival of new elements rather than only new relations among existing ones.

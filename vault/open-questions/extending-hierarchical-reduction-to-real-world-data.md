title: Extending Hierarchical Reduction to Real-World Data
---

**Background:** Almost-linear recurrent neural networks use piecewise-linear activation patterns to partition state space into linear regions, but extending these models to broader classes of real-world or noisy time-series data remains challenging.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the generalizability and extension of the fixed-point-guided hierarchical reduction framework to noisy, partially observed, and diverse real-world dynamical systems beyond standard low-dimensional chaotic attractors like the 3-scroll Chua system.

**Why It Matters:** Assessing how well fixed-point-guided graph reduction and retraining perform under observational noise and partial observability is crucial for transitioning these methods from synthetic benchmarks to real-world scientific machine learning applications.

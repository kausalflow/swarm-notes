title: Long-Term 2D-3D Occupancy Forecasting
---

**Background:** Camera-only 4D occupancy forecasting predicts future 3D semantic scenes from historical multi-view images, but existing models often struggle with complex spatio-temporal dynamics and long-term error accumulation across modalities.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how to further improve the multi-modal interaction and long-term consistency between 2D appearance representations and 3D volumetric dynamics in camera-only 4D occupancy forecasting, particularly under highly complex or occluded driving environments.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for pushing the accuracy limits of vision-based forecasting and reducing error accumulation over extended temporal horizons in autonomous driving.

**Evidence:** Strong spatial-temporal modeling between the input multi-view frames is still underexplored, which limits the performance of those methods in future 4D forecasting.

title: Higher-Dimensional Isotropic Correlation Coordinates
---

**Background:** Higher-dimensional isotropic correlation functions fail to be adequately characterized by one-dimensional Toeplitz-positivity conditions, leading to an open geometric problem for generalizing PACF-like natural coordinates.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the natural coordinate formulation and PACF/Levinson-Durbin geometry beyond one-dimensional Toeplitz correlation functions to genuinely higher-dimensional isotropic correlation functions, where the ordered Toeplitz structure is lost and constraints rely on multidimensional spectral representations and generalized moment problems.

**Why It Matters:** Extending natural coordinates to higher-dimensional isotropic spaces is critical for cosmological observables like cosmic shear and 3D large-scale structure where spatial symmetry is multi-dimensional rather than purely 1D, but currently lacks a systematic geometric coordinate framework.

**Evidence:** The extension to genuinely higher-dimensional isotropic correlation functions is a distinct and more fundamental problem. There, the one-dimensional Toeplitz-positivity conditions remain necessary but are no longer sufficient to characterise the admissible correlation vectors... Rather than seeking a direct analogue of the one-dimensional reflection coefficients, one may then ask whether these moment spaces admit natural coordinates and recursive extensions as additional lags are included.

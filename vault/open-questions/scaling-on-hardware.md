title: Hardware validation of scaling
---

**Background:** Quantum many-body systems operated as reservoirs can be evaluated for machine learning performance scaling.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine whether the superextensive performance scaling observed in numerical simulations of quantum reservoirs at the onset of information scrambling persists on physical quantum hardware under realistic noise, finite shot budgets, and experimental constraints.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for bridging theoretical quantum machine learning proposals with practical quantum advantage on near-term hardware.

**Evidence:** Whether the scaling persists on hardware is an urgent and exciting question that can be explored on today’s devices.

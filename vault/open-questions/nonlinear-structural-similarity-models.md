title: Nonlinear Structural Similarity Models
---

**Background:** Structural economic similarity measures rely on linear approximations of complex systems, raising questions about their applicability to models with nonlinear dynamics.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend the cosine-based structural similarity framework beyond linear models to accommodate nonlinear specifications, threshold effects, multiple regimes, and other complex structural representations.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for capturing real-world economic mechanisms that exhibit threshold effects or nonlinear responses to shocks without falling into model misspecification traps.

**Evidence:** Extending the methodology to nonlinear specifications and more flexible structural representations constitutes a natural direction for future research.

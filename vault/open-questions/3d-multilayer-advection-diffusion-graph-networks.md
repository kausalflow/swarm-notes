title: 3D Multi-Layer Advection-Diffusion Modeling
---

**Background:** Spatiotemporal dynamics like cloud transport and air pollution are frequently modeled on irregular sensor networks using graph-based physical frameworks restricted to horizontal planes.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending the 2D horizontal advection-diffusion modeling framework to three dimensions or multi-layer representations using tensor-based discretizations remains an open problem for handling complex atmospheric flows and vertical transport phenomena.

**Why It Matters:** Real-world transport phenomena such as cloud dynamics and weather patterns possess significant vertical structure and multi-layer interactions that cannot be fully captured by 2D horizontal approximations.

**Evidence:** Extending PING to a three-dimensional or multi-layer representation using tensor-based discretizations would be a promising direction for future work, as it would broaden the model’s applicability.

title: Improving AI Precipitation Forecasting Targets
---

**Background:** Atmospheric AI foundation models must be finetuned for precipitation forecasting using high-fidelity training targets and raw satellite observation ingestion to overcome limitations in reanalysis-based datasets.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work should explore adopting advanced machine-learning-based precipitation retrieval algorithms (such as upcoming IMERG versions) to improve the quality and quantity of precipitation training targets, and fully exploit the global satellite observing system by ingesting a broader subset of available multi-sensor observations to further boost medium-range precipitation forecast skill.

**Why It Matters:** Addressing the quality of training targets and extending the observation ingestion mechanism are critical bottlenecks for bridging the performance gap between AI precipitation models and advanced conventional numerical weather prediction systems at longer lead times.

**Evidence:** The first is improving the quality and quantity of precipitation training data... The second pathway is the direct use of satellite observations.

title: Optimization for Large Channel Dimensions
---

**Background:** Time series forecasting models often struggle with high dimensionality and computational overhead, particularly when scaling to large channel sizes and extended context lengths.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing enhanced optimization techniques and improving convergence rates when scaling to large channel dimensions remains a key challenge for sparse-encoder time series forecasting models.

**Why It Matters:** High-dimensional channel scaling frequently causes slow convergence and computational bottlenecks in multivariate time series architectures, making optimization improvements critical for practical deployment.

**Evidence:** We observe that SETTer exhibits slow convergence when the channel dimension is large; therefore, improving it via enhanced optimization techniques is another promising future direction.

title: Sub-Kilometer Global Atmospheric Modeling
---

**Background:** Sub-kilometer global climate and weather modeling requires transitioning grid spacings below 1 kilometer to explicitly resolve boundary-layer turbulence and individual convective cells.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work involves scaling global atmospheric models to grid spacings below 1 km to explicitly resolve individual convective cells and boundary-layer turbulence globally, transitioning beyond convection-permitting resolutions.

**Why It Matters:** Pushing global models into the sub-kilometer sub-grid regime represents the ultimate frontier in weather and climate modeling, requiring significant algorithmic and hardware scalability breakthroughs.

**Evidence:** Our ongoing work targets grid spacings below 1 km, which would enable the explicit resolution of individual convective cells and boundary-layer turbulence at the global scale, a frontier that has remained computationally inaccessible until now.

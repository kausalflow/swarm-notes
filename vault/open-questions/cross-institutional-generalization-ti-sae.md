title: Cross-Institutional Generalization of TI-SAE
---

**Background:** Deep learning models for electronic health records achieve strong predictive performance but remain opaque, and existing post-hoc attribution methods fail to produce temporally extended, cross-variable, reusable clinical concepts.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate whether cross-institutional generalization of the Temporal Island Sparse Autoencoder (TI-SAE) can be achieved beyond temporal and population shifts within the same hospital system.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for understanding whether learned clinical concept dictionaries and sparse spatiotemporal representations are robust across entirely different hospital environments and data collection practices.

**Evidence:** While the disjoint cohort eliminates patient and temporal overlap, both datasets originate from the same institution, so this evaluation captures temporal and population shift rather than fully external, cross-institutional generalization.

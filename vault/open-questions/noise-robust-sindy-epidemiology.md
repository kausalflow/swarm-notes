title: Noise-Robust SINDy for Epidemiology
---

**Background:** Sparse Identification of Nonlinear Dynamics (SINDy) relies on numerical differentiation of time-series measurements, which amplifies noise and causes performance degradation in high-noise or sparse data regimes typical of epidemiological surveillance.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing noise-robust extensions of SINDy, such as weak-form formulations that bypass explicit derivative estimation or compartment-wise/weighted regression variants, remains an open problem for accurately recovering disease transmission dynamics from real-world surveillance data.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for applying data-driven discovery methods to real-world epidemiological data where noise and underreporting are major bottlenecks.

**Evidence:** Despite these remarkable efforts, reliably recovering dynamics from noisy epidemiological observations remains an open problem.

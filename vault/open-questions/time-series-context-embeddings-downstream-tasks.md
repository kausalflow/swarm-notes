title: Context Representations for Downstream Tasks
---

**Background:** Time series foundation models leverage context representations for forecasting, but their utility for broader downstream tasks remains underexplored.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the application of the learned context representations from time series foundation models as general-purpose embeddings for downstream tasks such as classification and anomaly detection.

**Why It Matters:** Expanding time series foundation models beyond pure forecasting into universal representation learning for classification and anomaly detection is a critical step toward general-purpose time series intelligence.

title: Adaptive Rendering and Cluster Estimation
---

**Background:** Multivariate time series clustering models typically rely on fixed rendering schemes for visual-temporal representations and require preset numbers of clusters.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate adaptive waveform rendering techniques and automated cluster-number estimation methods to eliminate the requirement for predefined cluster counts and fixed plotting parameters in visual-temporal time series clustering frameworks.

**Why It Matters:** Overcoming the dependency on fixed rendering strategies and known cluster counts is crucial for making visual-temporal clustering applicable to fully unsupervised, dynamic real-world environments.

**Evidence:** WAVE currently assumes fixed waveform rendering and known cluster numbers, leaving adaptive rendering and cluster-number estimation for future work.

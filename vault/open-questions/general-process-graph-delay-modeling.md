title: General Process Graph Delay Modeling
---

**Background:** Multistage manufacturing quality prediction models often rely on adjacent-stage delay modeling, overlooking complex process topologies such as non-adjacent interactions, branching structures, or feedback loops.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending delay-guided multistage forecasting frameworks to more general process graphs involving non-adjacent interactions, branching structures, or feedback loops remains an important direction for future work.

**Why It Matters:** Real-world manufacturing plants frequently feature complex routing, recycling loops, and skipped stages that cannot be captured by simple linear adjacent-stage chains.

**Evidence:** First, the current framework focuses on adjacent-stage delay modeling, whereas more complex manufacturing systems may involve non-adjacent interactions, branching process structures, or feedback loops. Extending DAMF to more general process graphs would broaden its applicability.

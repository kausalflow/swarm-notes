title: Natively Multivariate Time Series Retrieval
---

**Background:** Time series foundation models currently rely on univariate knowledge bases and architectures, limiting their direct application to multivariate scenarios that require capturing complex inter-variable dependencies.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending retrieval-augmented time series foundation models to native multivariate classification by constructing multi-variable knowledge bases and designing general retrieval architectures that explicitly capture inter-variable dependencies rather than relying on input channel adapters.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for scaling retrieval-augmented time series models to complex real-world multivariate domains such as multi-sensor monitoring and healthcare diagnostics.

**Evidence:** Future work will explore more general retrieval module designs and build natively multivariate knowledge bases, so that inter-variable dependencies are captured by retrieval rather than by an input adapter.

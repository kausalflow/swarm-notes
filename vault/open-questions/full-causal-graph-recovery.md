title: Full Causal Graph Recovery
---

**Background:** Causal discovery from single event sequence observations currently relies on pairwise assumptions and score comparisons, leaving open the problem of scaling these frameworks to recover complete multi-variable causal graphs.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work includes extending the pairwise causal sequence inference framework beyond pairs to recover full causal graphs over multiple interacting event sequences.

**Why It Matters:** Moving from pairwise causal direction discovery to full graph recovery is a critical scaling step for practical applications like root-cause diagnosis in complex IT and telecommunications networks.

**Evidence:** Our future work will focus on incorporating temporal information into the scoring mechanism and extending this pairwise framework to recover full causal graphs.

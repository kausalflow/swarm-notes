title: Advanced Ionospheric and IISM Modeling
---

**Background:** Pulsar polarimetry data is utilized to search for signatures of ultralight axion-like dark matter and investigate magnetospheric and interstellar medium properties.

**Question / Future Work:** Future investigations require more sophisticated physical modelling and more precise Total Electron Content (TEC) maps to better account for ionospheric Faraday rotation components and complex interstellar medium variations that are missed by simple single-layer models.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for distinguishing between genuine exotic dark matter signals and complex astrophysical foregrounds in pulsar polarization arrays.

**Evidence:** More sophisticated models and precise TEC maps may better account for the ionospheric FR components that may be missed with the simple SLM used in this work [Porayko et al. 2023], and with more sensitive data, more detailed ionospheric analysis should be warranted.

title: Persistence and Transience of Blazar QPOs
---

**Background:** Blazar light curves frequently exhibit quasi-periodic oscillations (QPOs) and long-term trends, yet confirming their stationarity and physical origins remains challenging due to limited observational baselines in gamma-ray regimes.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate whether long-term quasi-periodic oscillations observed in blazars persist indefinitely or exhibit transience on multi-year timescales, and establish robust methodologies to distinguish between geometric models (such as supermassive black hole binaries and jet precession) and stochastic processes.

**Why It Matters:** Resolving the transience versus persistence of blazar QPOs is critical for validating supermassive binary black hole candidates and understanding relativistic jet emission physics.

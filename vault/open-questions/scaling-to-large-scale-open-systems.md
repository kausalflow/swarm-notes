title: Scaling to Large-Scale Open Systems
---

**Background:** Scientific dynamics forecasting evaluates models across different observation, rollout, compute, and distributional conditions, but existing benchmarks often average across these factors or treat architecture choice independently of deployment protocols.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine whether model architecture rankings and protocol-dependent performance characteristics generalize to open quantum-many-body systems, noisy measurement regimes, and higher-dimensional or larger-scale spatial domains beyond small-scale closed spin systems.

**Why It Matters:** Scaling model-selection protocols to large-scale, noisy, and open quantum systems is critical for understanding whether observed ranking reversals and failure-sensitivity findings persist in realistic physical deployment scenarios.

**Evidence:** Our evidence comes from exact simulated closed-system spin dynamics, up to four spins with complete Pauli observations and six spins with local observations. It does not estimate rankings for large many-body systems, noisy measurements, or open-system dynamics, and the constraint study uses one independent dataset rather than three.

title: Establishing Causal Validity in Medical World Models
---

**Background:** Medical world models rely on historical observational data where treatments are confounded by patient severity and physician choice, making causal validity difficult to establish without unmeasured confounding and proper target-trial emulation.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how medical world models can move beyond observational trajectory forecasting to valid causal treatment effect estimation, addressing challenges such as time-varying confounding, unmeasured patient factors, and the lack of proper target-trial emulation.

**Why It Matters:** Essential for ensuring that medical world models can support safe, effective clinical decision-making rather than merely replicating historical observational biases.

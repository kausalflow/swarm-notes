title: Robust Metrics and Distance Functions
---

**Background:** Long-term time series forecasting models frequently produce inconsistent prediction outputs for the same timestamps across lagged input sequences, a phenomenon known as low output alignment.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research should explore the development of more robust evaluation metrics for inherently volatile and abrupt out-of-distribution datasets where standard time alignment metrics may fail, as well as investigate alternative distance functions that better capture complex shape characteristics of time series data beyond standard mean squared error.

**Why It Matters:** Identifying robust alignment metrics and specialized distance functions is critical for extending output alignment techniques to highly volatile, real-world industrial and financial applications.

**Evidence:** Designing more robust metrics for inherently volatile data is our next research objective... To further enhance performance, alternative distance functions... may serve as promising directions for future exploration.

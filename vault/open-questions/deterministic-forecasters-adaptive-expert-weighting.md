title: Deterministic Forecasters and Adaptive Expert Weighting
---

**Background:** Human trajectory prediction models often rely on numerical coordinate regression or language-based token generation, but each formulation possesses distinct limitations in balancing continuous metric accuracy with high-level contextual reasoning.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop stronger deterministic trajectory forecasters and adaptive expert weighting mechanisms to provide more reliable coordinate-level guidance during reward-based model refinement.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for overcoming current limitations in deterministic trajectory forecasting and improving the robustness of multi-expert consensus rewards in reward-guided language policies.

**Evidence:** Determinism gains remain small because available deterministic experts provide limited improvement, motivating stronger deterministic forecasters. Future work will investigate stronger deterministic experts and adaptive expert weighting to provide more reliable guidance.

title: Fully Coupled AI Earth System Modeling
---

**Background:** Machine learning Earth system models are often evaluated under prescribed forcing conditions to isolate component errors, leaving their performance in fully coupled configurations with prognostic atmospheres unquantified.

**Question / Future Work:** Future research needs to assess the performance, stability, and predictive skill of deep learning ocean and sea ice models when fully coupled with prognostic atmospheric modules (such as parsimonious atmospheric models or complex physically constrained probabilistic variants), particularly for subseasonal-to-seasonal forecasting applications.

**Why It Matters:** Transitioning from forced ocean simulations to fully coupled interactive Earth system models is critical for operational subseasonal-to-seasonal forecasting and climate projections using artificial intelligence.

**Evidence:** Future work will assess the performance of such a coupled model, including coupling with either the parsimonious atmospheric module in the original DLESyM or more complex physically constrained probabilistic variants of that model.

title: Systematic simulation boundary validation

**Background:** Heavy-tailed time series exhibiting regime-switching dynamics are widely modeled using Hidden Markov Models, yet the predictive advantages of flexible multi-parameter distributions like the Singh-Maddala family over simpler emission alternatives require broader systematic validation across diverse data-generating regimes.

**Question / Future Work:** A systematic simulation study over a grid of data-generating parameters is needed to establish the boundaries within which the predictive advantage of Singh-Maddala hidden Markov models holds relative to simpler emission distributions.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the precise performance boundaries and regimes where heavy-tailed multi-parameter emissions outperform simpler alternatives is crucial for methodological guidance in applied time series modeling.

**Evidence:** A systematic simulation study over a grid of data-generating parameters would be required to establish the boundaries within which the advantage holds, and we identify this as future work.

title: Direct Customer-Level Substitution Tracking
---

**Background:** Validating item-to-item demand transfer coefficients typically relies on proxy metrics like forecast error reductions because ground-truth customer substitution decisions are unobserved in standard retail transaction data.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop customer-level substitution tracking methodologies to enable direct validation of estimated demand-transfer coefficients rather than relying solely on indirect forecast improvement metrics.

**Why It Matters:** Direct validation using customer-level tracking addresses a fundamental observability bottleneck in empirical retail analytics and choice modeling.

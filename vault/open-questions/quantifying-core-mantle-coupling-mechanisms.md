title: Quantifying Core-Mantle Coupling Mechanisms
---

**Background:** Earth's length of day exhibits multidecadal variations strongly correlated with core angular momentum derived from geomagnetic field models, yet the relative contributions of individual core-mantle boundary coupling mechanisms remain unquantified.

**Question / Future Work:** Determine the specific partitioning of electromagnetic, topographic, gravitational, and viscous coupling mechanisms responsible for the core-related multidecadal modulation of Earth's length of day.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the exact coupling mechanisms at the core-mantle boundary is fundamental to deep Earth geodynamics and accurately modeling long-term angular momentum exchange between the core and mantle.

**Evidence:** The results are consistent with a core-related contribution to low-frequency rotational variability, but they do not uniquely separate the contributions of electromagnetic, topographic, gravitational, and viscous core–mantle coupling mechanisms.

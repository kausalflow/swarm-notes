title: Overcoming Spring Predictability Barriers
---

**Background:** Deep learning models for El Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO) forecasting experience a persistent drop in predictive skill during spring months, known as the Spring Predictability Barrier (SPB).

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate whether the Spring Predictability Barrier (SPB) in deep learning ENSO forecasts arises from an intrinsic limitation of current ocean-atmosphere predictor variables or inherent chaotic barriers in physical dynamics, and determine whether integrating multi-modal ocean and atmospheric variables can effectively transcend these limitations.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding and overcoming the Spring Predictability Barrier is crucial for improving long-range climate forecasting reliability and advancing operational deep learning deployment in meteorology.

**Evidence:** This suggests that the SPB might be an intrinsic component of ENSO dynamical processes, or that the information embedded in the input data is no longer sufficient for the model to make accurate predictions. In the future, more modalities of the ocean and the related atmosphere may be integrated to break through the spring prediction barrier.

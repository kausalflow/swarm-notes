title: Real-World Educational Validation
---

**Background:** University trajectory forecasting systems evaluate historical student observations to improve multi-step predictions, but comparing historical models against current-state predictors can conflate true memory benefits with calendar-based feature changes or future plan disclosures.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work must extend these findings beyond synthetic, rule-based university trajectory simulators to real-world educational data while maintaining rigorous information-timing audits, calendar controls, and failure disclosures across diverse student populations.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for ensuring that findings from controlled simulation environments translate to actual empirical student performance and robust educational decision-making.

**Evidence:** Real educational outcomes require additional evidence... Simulator accuracy and educational effectiveness should therefore remain separate conclusions.

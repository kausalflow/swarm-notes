title: Probabilistic Ocean Ensemble Forecasting
---

**Background:** Current data-driven ocean forecasting frameworks are predominantly deterministic and lack probabilistic prediction capabilities for operational uncertainty quantification.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop ensemble forecasting strategies leveraging the computational efficiency of geometry-adaptive unstructured mesh representations to enable reliable uncertainty quantification and probabilistic predictions for operational oceanography.

**Why It Matters:** Probabilistic forecasting and uncertainty quantification are essential for risk assessment in extreme marine events and robust operational oceanography.

**Evidence:** Beyond the mesh construction strategy, another limitation lies in the forecasting paradigm itself: the current forecasting system is deterministic and does not provide probabilistic predictions.

title: Latent Capacity in Complex Topography
---

**Background:** High-resolution regional weather forecasting models operating over complex topography require robust latent representation capacities to accurately resolve steep gradients and localized weather phenomena across neighbouring grid cells.

**Question / Future Work:** Explore the capacity of latent representations in terms of spatial coverage and number of channels, including dynamically increasing the density of latent nodes in demanding regions to introduce an inductive bias towards complex terrain.

**Why It Matters:** This is crucial for overcoming performance bottlenecks in data-driven weather models over steep orography and complex mountain topographies where adjacent grid cells experience vastly differing microclimates.

**Evidence:** Future work should investigate the capacity of the latent representation in terms of spatial coverage and the number of channels. A promising research direction could involve dynamically increasing the density of the latent nodes in more demanding regions, therefore helping the model by introducing an inductive bias towards complex terrain.

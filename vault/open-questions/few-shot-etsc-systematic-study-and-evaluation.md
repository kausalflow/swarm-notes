title: Systematic Study of Few-Shot ETSC
---

**Background:** Early time-series classification methods predominantly focus on full-data settings, leaving per-class few-shot supervision largely unexplored.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate the systematic extension and evaluation of early time-series classification (ETSC) under per-class few-shot supervision, addressing the challenges of limited labeled data and sample-level halting mechanisms.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for enabling reliable early prediction in specialized, data-scarce domains such as healthcare and industrial monitoring where annotations are scarce.

**Evidence:** To the best of our knowledge, ETSC under per-class few-shot supervision has not yet been systematically studied. Existing methods are primarily developed under full-data settings, leaving their behavior under limited supervision unclear.

title: Impact of CME Magnetic Fields
---

**Background:** Coronal mass ejection (CME) and CME-driven shock propagation models typically rely on simplified hydrodynamic descriptions or idealized magnetic structures like cone models, which neglect internal magnetic fields.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate how the interaction between the ambient solar wind and the internal magnetic field of a Coronal Mass Ejection (CME) influences the 3D evolution, deformation, and arrival times of the CME and its driven shock wave using advanced magnetised CME models (such as FRi3D or Horseshoe).

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for bridging the gap between idealized hydrodynamic cone models and realistic magneto-hydrodynamic behavior, directly improving space weather prediction accuracy for CME arrivals and shock structures.

**Evidence:** As a further step towards better understanding of the propagation of the CMEs and CME-driven shock waves, we aim to employ magnetised CME models, like the FRi3D or Horseshoe. We expect such, even more advanced modelling setup will, show how the interaction of the background solar wind and CMEs magnetic field influence the evolution of the CME and CME-driven shock and their arrival time at any place in space and time in the inner heliosphere

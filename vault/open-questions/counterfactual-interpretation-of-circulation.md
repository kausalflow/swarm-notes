title: Counterfactual Interpretation of Statistical Circulation
---

**Background:** Measured arrows of time and statistical circulations quantify temporal asymmetry and information flow in reduced-form models, yet they do not inherently provide structural counterfactual or policy-relevant interpretations.

**Question / Future Work:** Determining the precise conditions, assumptions, and identifying restrictions under which a measured statistical circulation or arrow of time can be mapped into a structural, intervention-relevant causal model.

**Why It Matters:** Bridging the gap between descriptive observational time-reversibility metrics and rigorous policy counterfactual analysis is crucial for causal inference in macroeconomics and systems biology.

**Evidence:** Interventions. The strongest version of a directional claim is counterfactual, and the circulation is not counterfactual. What conditions turn a measured circulation into an intervention-relevant object is, in our view, the most important open question in this line of work...

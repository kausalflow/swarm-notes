title: Joint Interval-Valued Circular and Linear Models
---

**Background:** Score-driven time series models for circular and cylindrical data are typically formulated for univariate or simple bivariate settings without accounting for interval-valued structures across multiple coupled meteorological variables.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop joint score-driven time series frameworks capable of simultaneously modeling interval-valued circular variables (such as wind direction intervals) and linear variables (such as wind speed) for renewable energy and environmental applications.

**Why It Matters:** Joint modeling of interval-valued circular and linear variables addresses a key operational need in wind energy forecasting, where both directional intervals and wind speeds must be predicted simultaneously.

**Evidence:** For future work, score-driven approaches can be explored for joint models capable of modelling interval-valued wind direction and wind speed, which would be of applicable use in wind energy applications.

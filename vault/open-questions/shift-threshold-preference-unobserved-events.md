title: Critical Shift Threshold for Unobserved Events
---

**Background:** The evaluation of spatial verification methods for probabilistic weather forecasts relies on idealized synthetic datasets to isolate specific error characteristics like displacement and dispersion.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate what specific factors determine the critical shift threshold above which a forecast that completely fails to predict an observed event is preferred over a spatially displaced forecast across different spatial verification scores.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the critical shift threshold helps characterize the limits of the double-penalty effect and informs the design of robust spatial verification metrics for probabilistic models.

**Evidence:** Shifted forecasts seem to have comparable relative scores as P00 for shift values of about 15 for all scores. This encourages a deeper investigation into what influences the shift value above which a forecast that does not predict the observed event is preferred.

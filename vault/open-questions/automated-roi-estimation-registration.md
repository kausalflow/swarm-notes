title: Automated ROI Estimation and Registration
---

**Background:** Manual region of interest (ROI) placement in phenotyping software introduces operator subjectivity and limits processing throughput for large image sets.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate automated or semi-automated ROI estimation methods, such as template-matching or feature-based image registration, to generate initial bounding boxes that users can subsequently confirm or adjust, thereby reducing operator subjectivity and improving throughput across large datasets.

**Why It Matters:** Manual ROI delineation is a major bottleneck in high-throughput plant phenotyping pipelines, limiting scalability when handling large temporal image collections.

**Evidence:** ROI placement also remains a manual step, introducing operator subjectivity and limiting throughput for large image sets; template-matching or feature-based image registration could in future provide an initial automated ROI estimate for the user to confirm or adjust.

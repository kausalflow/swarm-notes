title: In-Context Learning in Dynamical Systems Foundation Models
---

**Background:** Continuous-valued forecast foundation models for dynamical systems demonstrate strong in-context learning capabilities during inference, but the theoretical foundations and underlying mechanisms governing how this emergence occurs remain poorly understood.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate whether foundation models truly learn generalizable in-context forecasting strategies and characterize the emergence of in-context learning in continuous-valued forecast model settings.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding the emergence of in-context learning in continuous dynamical systems bridges discrete language models and continuous scientific foundation models, guiding the design of more robust architectures.

**Evidence:** However, it remains an open question whether these foundation models truly learn generalizable in-context forecasting strategies.

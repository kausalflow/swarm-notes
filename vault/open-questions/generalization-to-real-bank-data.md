title: Real-World Financial Data Generalization
---

**Background:** Financial crime detection models often rely on synthetic datasets like AMLWorld, but the extent to which these findings generalize to real-world banking data with incomplete labels remains an open challenge.

**Question / Future Work:** The authors note that AMLWorld is a simulated environment mirroring real transaction behavior, and an open direction for future work is to validate whether the performance gains of TSFM-based edge enrichment transfer to real bank data containing incomplete labels and different typologies.

**Why It Matters:** Assessing generalization to real-world financial data is critical for validating the practical utility of time-series foundation models in production anti-money laundering systems.

**Evidence:** Whether these results hold on real bank data, where labels are incomplete, should be explored.

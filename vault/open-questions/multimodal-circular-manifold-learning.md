title: Multimodal Circular Manifold Learning
---

**Background:** Atmospheric wind direction forecasting on the circular manifold requires probabilistic models that can adequately capture structural uncertainty and bifurcating error distributions during extreme directional shear events.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and develop conditional multimodal circular distributions and manifold learning techniques capable of explicitly preserving separated probability mass on the unit circle to resolve structural under-coverage under extreme wind shear.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for accurate uncertainty quantification and risk-aware wind turbine control during rapid atmospheric transitions and frontal passages.

**Evidence:** yielding persistent under-coverage that cannot be resolved solely by expanding single-center interval bounds... motivating future multimodal circular manifold learning.

**Background:** Traditional statistical tools for assessing forecast calibration assume fixed evaluation samples, which can be invalidated by continuous real-time monitoring and optional stopping.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigating the theoretical and practical implications of extending e-value-based sequential calibration tests to handle dependent, multi-step-ahead forecasts and overlapping information structures where standard supermartingale properties do not directly apply.

**Why It Matters:** Multi-step macroeconomic forecasting inherently involves overlapping information sets, making the development of powerful and non-conservative anytime-valid testing procedures critical for real-time central bank monitoring.

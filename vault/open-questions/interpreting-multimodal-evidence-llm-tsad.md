title: Interpreting Multimodal Evidence in LLMs
---

**Background:** Zero-shot time-series anomaly detection relies primarily on text or visual prompt representations, which often hide internal decision paths and make it difficult to attribute how specific input modalities guide anomaly localization.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work may investigate attribution or intervention-based analyses to better understand how language models use time-series, visual, and frequency-domain evidence during anomaly localization.

**Why It Matters:** Important for understanding the causal mechanisms and faithfulness of multimodal and evidence-augmented reasoning in time-series anomaly detection.

**Evidence:** Future work may investigate attribution or intervention-based analyses to better understand how LLMs use time-domain, visual, and frequency-domain evidence during anomaly localization.

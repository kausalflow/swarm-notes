title: Handling Coupled Sampling and Feature Shifts
---

**Background:** Domain generalization methods for irregularly sampled multivariate time series face challenges when real-world sampling patterns vary across domains and become entangled with shifts in the underlying feature distribution.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and develop principled techniques to understand and handle coupled shifts, where changes in the sampling process are entangled with shifts in the underlying feature distribution across domains.

**Why It Matters:** Real-world distribution shifts rarely affect only observation patterns or feature values in isolation; handling their coupling is crucial for robust deployment in critical domains like healthcare.

**Evidence:** Understanding and handling such coupled shifts remains an important direction for future work.

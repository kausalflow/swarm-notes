title: Robust Loss Model Averaging
---

**Background:** Model averaging methods for expected shortfall prediction currently rely on quadratic loss functions, leaving robust loss alternatives theoretically unaddressed under model misspecification.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending model averaging procedures for expected shortfall regression to incorporate robust loss functions, such as the adaptive Huber loss, poses theoretical challenges under model misspecification due to the difficulty of tuning parameter selection. Developing theoretically justified robust model averaging weights remains an important direction for future research.

**Why It Matters:** Extending model averaging methods to handle heavy-tailed data and outliers robustly without theoretical breakdown under misspecification is a crucial frontier in financial risk forecasting.

**Evidence:** However, selecting the Huber tuning parameter under model misspecification poses theoretical challenges. We leave this issue for future research.

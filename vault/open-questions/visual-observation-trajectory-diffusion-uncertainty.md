title: Visual Observation Trajectory Diffusion Uncertainty
---

**Background:** Trajectory diffusion models represent complex multimodal distributions over future motion, but extracting uncertainty from them typically requires costly Monte Carlo sampling that limits real-time control applications.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending score-curvature precision estimation and control-ready uncertainty frameworks from low-dimensional states and geometry inputs to raw, high-dimensional visual observations such as images or point clouds.

**Why It Matters:** Enables uncertainty-aware trajectory diffusion and planning directly from high-dimensional sensor streams without requiring explicit lower-dimensional state tracking.

**Evidence:** Extending SCOPE to raw visual observations, such as images or point clouds, is an important direction for future work.

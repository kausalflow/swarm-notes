title: Joint MHD-SEP Parameter Estimation
---

**Background:** Physics-based forecasting models for solar energetic particle (SEP) events rely on numerous free parameters that are often poorly constrained by observations, requiring ad-hoc manual tuning that hinders operational generalization.

**Question / Future Work:** Developing a joint magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) and solar energetic particle (SEP) parameter estimation framework by accumulating optimized parameter sets across a statistically meaningful sample of diverse events to construct empirical prior distributions and reduce systematic uncertainties.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for transitioning from event-specific physics-based hindcasting to generalized, operational real-time space weather forecasting.

**Evidence:** Joint MHD–SEP parameter estimation is therefore a necessary next step towards generalized, operational SEP forecasting.

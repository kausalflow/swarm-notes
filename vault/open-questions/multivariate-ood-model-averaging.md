title: Multivariate Out-of-Distribution Model Averaging
---

**Background:** Travel demand forecasting models often need to generalize to out-of-distribution contexts, such as trip characteristics or continuous attribute values extending beyond the range observed during model estimation.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate out-of-distribution model averaging and predictive performance considering attributes beyond trip distance alone, such as socio-economic characteristics or multivariate combinations of trip attributes that extend outside the range covered by estimation data.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding how model averaging and behavioural versus machine learning models perform across multidimensional and socio-economic out-of-distribution settings is crucial for expanding practical transport demand forecasting methodologies.

**Evidence:** A core opportunity for future work is thus the investigation of out-of-distribution performance considering attributes besides trip distance alone, for example, going outside the range of socio-economic characteristics covered by the estimation data, or combinations of socio-economic characteristics not covered in the estimation data.

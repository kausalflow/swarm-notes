title: Multivariate Symbolic Equation Discovery
---

**Background:** Multivariate time series data involving exogenous drivers and complex interactions are common in real-world climate and epidemiological forecasting tasks, yet current symbolic forecasters primarily focus on univariate lagged representations.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend symbolic neural and tree-based forecasting architectures to incorporate multivariate inputs and exogenous drivers, assessing how multidimensional functional discovery impacts both predictive accuracy and interpretability.

**Why It Matters:** Real-world physical and biological systems are driven by multiple interacting variables, making multivariate extension essential for practical scientific discovery.

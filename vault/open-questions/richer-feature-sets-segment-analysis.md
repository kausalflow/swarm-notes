title: Richer Feature Sets for Segment Analysis
---

**Background:** Time series analysis frameworks often rely on minimal feature representations to avoid overfitting on small cohorts, yet richer geometric feature sets remain largely unexplored.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate whether richer feature sets incorporating directional deviations, curvature metrics, and local density measures can further enhance segment-based alignment analysis without overfitting on small clinical cohorts.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding how to expand the feature space without inducing overfitting is vital for extending segment-based alignment methods to highly heterogeneous clinical datasets.

**Evidence:** Systematic evaluation of richer feature sets on larger cohorts remains an open direction for future work.

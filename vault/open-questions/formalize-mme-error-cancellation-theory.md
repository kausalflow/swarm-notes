title: Formalizing Multi-Model Ensemble Theory
---

**Background:** Multi-model ensembles (MMEs) improve short-term forecasting performance across various chaotic dynamical systems by leveraging model diversity and error cancellation along unstable manifolds.

**Question / Future Work:** Formalize theoretically the numerical findings regarding the geometric error cancellation along unstable manifolds and the statistical convergence properties of multi-model ensemble forecasts.

**Why It Matters:** Providing a rigorous mathematical framework for the observed geometric error cancellation and ensemble-size scaling would bridge empirical weather/climate forecasting heuristics with rigorous dynamical systems theory.

title: Comprehensive Exomoon and Planet Yield Modeling
---

**Background:** Prior yield studies and observational planning for direct imaging missions often use simplifying assumptions regarding coronagraph inner/outer working angles and specific orbital phases.

**Question / Future Work:** Incorporate integrated yield studies combining joint mass, orbital radius, orbital inclination distributions, and coronagraph working angle constraints into future direct imaging mission simulators to accurately quantify expected gas giant and exomoon yields.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for optimizing mission observing schedules, coronagraph design parameters, and target prioritization for exoplanet and satellite discoveries.

**Evidence:** A reliable estimate of the exomoon “yield” that HWO could achieve would require a yield study similar to those undertaken for exo-Earths [78, 98, 62, 109, 135], where HWO observations are simulated for exoplanets and exomoons randomly generated from joint mass, orbital radius, and orbital inclination distributions.

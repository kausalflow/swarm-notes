title: Bridging Understanding-Action Gap in Urban Agents
---

**Background:** Urban intelligence relies on multi-layer spatio-temporal datasets, but current agentic frameworks struggle to bridge the gap between perceptual data retrieval and reliable quantitative decision-making, often suffering from mismatched external tool usage, lack of domain-specific urban knowledge, and inappropriate spatio-temporal scales.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate and develop agentic architectures that robustly bridge the gap between multimodal urban perception and quantitative action, specifically by improving domain-specific urban reasoning, mitigating tool-use failures, and correctly aligning spatio-temporal scales across heterogeneous urban systems.

**Why It Matters:** This is a core foundational limitation in current urban AI agents, directly dictating whether multimodal urban datasets can be successfully operationalized for real-world policymaking and scientific planning.

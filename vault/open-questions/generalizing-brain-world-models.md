title: Generalizing Brain World Models
---

**Background:** Forecasting naturalistic human brain activity from continuous sensory stimuli requires simulating latent brain-state dynamics under causal temporal constraints.

**Question / Future Work:** Extending latent brain world models to independent cohorts, unseen participants, more efficient subject adaptation, longer and uncertainty-aware rollout, and additional naturalistic paradigms and neuroimaging modalities remains an important direction for future research.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for validating the generalizability, scalability, and clinical or cognitive utility of brain world models across diverse populations and imaging settings.

**Evidence:** Important directions for future work include evaluation in independent cohorts and unseen participants, more efficient subject adaptation, longer and uncertainty-aware rollout, and extension to additional naturalistic paradigms and neuroimaging modalities.

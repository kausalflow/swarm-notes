title: Out-of-Distribution Action Extrapolation
---

**Background:** Action-conditioned world models for time series must generalize to novel or unsupported action regimes beyond the training data distribution.

**Question / Future Work:** Investigate methods to reliably model and extrapolate action-conditioned time series dynamics under distribution shifts and substantially different action regimes unsupported by the training data.

**Why It Matters:** Crucial for deploying world models safely in real-world environments where agents may encounter out-of-distribution control actions.

**Evidence:** Characterizing uncertainty under such distribution shifts and adapting the dynamics with limited new interaction are useful directions for future work.

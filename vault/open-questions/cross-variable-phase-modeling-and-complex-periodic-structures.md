title: Cross-Variable Phase Modeling
---

**Background:** Time series forecasting models often struggle with periodicity drift, where cycle timings and phase progressions vary over time rather than recurring at fixed intervals.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work will explore cross-variable phase modeling and extend the framework to more complex periodic structures beyond single-variable and basic cyclical formulations.

**Why It Matters:** Extending phase-aware models to capture inter-variable dependencies and complex multi-frequency periodic structures is a major open challenge in multivariate time series forecasting.

**Evidence:** Future work will explore cross-variable phase modeling and extend the framework to more complex periodic structures.

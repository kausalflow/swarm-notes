title: Utility Forecasting for Multimodal Iterative RAG
---

**Background:** Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) frameworks currently lack systematic methods to forecast utility and deployment value across candidate retrievers prior to large-scale execution under shared generation budgets.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend current evidence-state frameworks and interventional response calibration techniques to support variable retrieval depths, iterative multi-step retrieval mechanisms, and multimodal knowledge environments.

**Why It Matters:** Expanding utility forecasting frameworks to dynamic, iterative, and multimodal RAG setups is critical for scaling deployment decision-making to complex reasoning tasks.

**Evidence:** Future work will extend evidence states to variable depths, iterative retrieval, and multimodal knowledge environments.

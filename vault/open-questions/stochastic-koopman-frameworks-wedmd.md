title: Stochastic Koopman Frameworks and Process Noise
---

**Background:** Weak-form Extended Dynamic Mode Decomposition (WEDMD) utilizes Galerkin projections onto compactly supported test functions to approximate the Koopman operator and generator from time-series data while mitigating noise.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend weak-form extended dynamic mode decomposition to explicitly incorporate and analyze stochastic differential equations (SDEs), addressing how process noise and measurement noise jointly impact the generator and observable dictionaries.

**Why It Matters:** Extending weak-form Koopman frameworks to rigorously handle stochastic process noise is critical for data-driven modeling in systems where stochastic perturbations significantly alter the generator dynamics rather than acting purely as additive measurement noise.

**Evidence:** Future work will focus on extending WEDMD to stochastic Koopman frameworks, where a more careful examination of the impacts of process noise and the concomitant measurement noise are addressed and better accounted for in our methodology.

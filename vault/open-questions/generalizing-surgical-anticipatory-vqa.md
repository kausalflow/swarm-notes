title: Generalizing Surgical Anticipatory VQA
---

**Background:** Surgical vision-language models struggle to evaluate future-aware procedures across multiple clinical specialties and diverse question distributions.

**Question / Future Work:** Extend current video-based surgical visual question answering benchmarks across multiple types of surgeries and broader question distributions, while further investigating temporal evaluation strategies and the comprehension of complex temporal dynamics in surgical workflows.

**Why It Matters:** Important for generalizing anticipatory vision-language models beyond single-procedure datasets and verifying cross-specialty robustness in real-time medical assistance.

**Evidence:** Future work will extend this benchmark across multiple surgeries with broader question types, explore alternative video embedding strategies, and further assess VLMs’ understanding of temporal dynamics in surgical workflows.

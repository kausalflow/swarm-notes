title: Balancing Temporal Contrast Trade-offs
---

**Background:** Time-series representation learning models trained with local temporal contrasts induce global geometric structures in representation spaces, but balancing global structure learning with component-level classification remains an open optimization challenge.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work needs to investigate methods to control the trade-off between self-similarity and temporal interaction transformation rates, and introduce more complex and nuanced transformation definitions to balance global representation geometry with dedicated component classification.

**Why It Matters:** Understanding how to balance contrastive transformation rates is critical for designing universal time-series representation models that simultaneously preserve fine-grained component details and global trajectory structures.

**Evidence:** Future work will focus on finding methods to control this trade-off, balancing the self-similarity and temporal interaction transformation rates, and introducing more complex and nuanced transformation definitions are avenues that can provide great insight into this.

title: Human Intent and Contact Conditioning
---

**Background:** Continuous 4D interaction forecasting models translate egocentric video inputs into anticipated 3D interaction locations and full-body motion trajectories.

**Question / Future Work:** Future work involves exploring human intent modeling and contact-aware conditioning to enable more physically consistent forecasts over longer horizons.

**Why It Matters:** Addressing human intent and detailed contact constraints is critical for bridging the gap between coarse spatial trajectory forecasts and physically plausible human-object manipulations in robotics and assistant systems.

**Evidence:** Future work will explore human intent modeling and contact-aware conditioning to enable more physically consistent forecasts over longer horizons.

title: Stronger Anonymization and Broader Events
---

**Background:** Large language models and their agent wrappers are predominantly evaluated on task-completion capabilities rather than their social-world reasoning and forecasting abilities.

**Question / Future Work:** Develop stronger anonymization techniques and expand the event pool to better separate true forecasting from pre-training memory recall while refining discrimination among near-peer models.

**Why It Matters:** Enhances the robustness of counterfactual social-world evaluation, ensuring benchmarks remain resistant to memorization as foundation models scale.

**Evidence:** Useful next steps are stronger anonymization to separate forecasting from memory even more cleanly, a broader event pool, finer discrimination among near-peer models, and community-contributed events and systems.

# Uncertainty-Aware Trajectory Forecasting

> *Auto-generated stub. Edit this file to add more details.*

A trajectory forecasting framework that propagates tracking-derived reliability cues via Gaussian representations combined through the law of total variance.


## Why It Matters

Provides a principled plug-in formulation using the law of total variance to combine localization uncertainty and association ambiguity from imperfect multi-object trackers.

## Evidence

> We propose a plug-in uncertainty-aware formulation in which each observed state is encoded as an uncertain state representation, modeled by a Gaussian distribution whose covariance combines detection-level localization uncertainty and association-level ambiguity through the law of total variance.

# Causalised Granger Causality

A causalised formulation of Granger causality that combines bivariate and multivariate checks under causal graphical principles to recover true directed causal structures.


## Why It Matters

Introduces a novel extension of Granger causality grounded in causal Bayesian networks and Reichenbach's principles to distinguish direct causal effects from indirect paths and common causes.

# The Radius-Luminosity Relationship for Active Galactic Nuclei with Hierarchical Bayesian Reverberation Mapping

**Authors**: Hugh Gareth McDougall, T. M. Davis, Benjamin J. S. Pope, Paul Martini, Zhefu Yu, Christopher E. Lidman, Geraint F. Lewis, Christian Adamcewicz, Rahma Alfarsy
**Date**: 2026-10-15
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.22096](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22096)

## Summary

This paper presents an end-to-end Bayesian framework for reverberation mapping of active galactic nuclei, simultaneously fitting time lags and population models like the radius-luminosity relation while fully marginalising over data uncertainty and seasonal aliasing. Applied to the OzDES sample, the method doubles population-level constraining power and yields an order-of-magnitude increase in recovered lags and masses compared to traditional techniques.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces an end-to-end Bayesian framework for fitting time lags and population models (such as the radius-luminosity relation) for active galactic nuclei at scale.
- Fully marginalises over data uncertainty and data quality to generalise traditional stacking and quality cut procedures.
- Demonstrates on the OzDES sample more than double the constraining power on population-level properties and an order-of-magnitude increase in recovered lags and masses (expanding from 24 to approximately 297 high-quality lag recoveries).

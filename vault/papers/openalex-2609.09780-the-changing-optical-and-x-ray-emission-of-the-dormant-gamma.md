# The changing optical and X-ray emission of the dormant $\gamma$ Cas star HD 45314

**Authors**: G. Rauw, Y. Nazé, E. Bryssinck, W. Vollmann, J. Guarro Fló, X. Dupont, M. A. Smith, C. Motch, G. W. Henry, J. Robrade, A. de Bruin, S. Charbonnel, A. Favaro, P. Fricker, O. Garde, O. **Date**: 2026-09-09
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.09780](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09780)

## Summary

This paper investigates the changing optical and X-ray emission of the dormant gamma Cas star HD 45314 using long-term optical spectroscopy, photometry, and X-ray spectroscopy. The analysis reveals optical oscillations on a 230 d timescale, a 1190 d modulation in the violet-to-red peak intensity ratio, and short-term photometric variations linked to beta Cep-type pulsations. Notably, unlike other gamma Cas stars, HD 45314's X-ray emission dropped to a low state as its circumstellar disc dissipated, offering new insights into the origin of hard X-rays in these systems.

## Key Contributions

- Multi-wavelength monitoring of the dormant gamma Cas star HD 45314 reveals optical oscillations on a 230 d timescale and a 1190 d violet-to-red peak intensity ratio modulation.
- Discovery of short-term photometric variations at 3.369 d^-1 attributed to beta Cep-type pulsations that strengthen during overall brightness changes.
- Observation that HD 45314's X-ray spectrum switched to and remained in a low state as its circumstellar disc dissipated, contrasting with other gamma Cas systems.

# Probabilistic Deep Learning for Drought Forecasting: Role of Internal Climate Variability

**Authors**: Henri Funk, Cornelia Gruber, Göran Kauermann, Helmut Küchenhoff, Magdalena Mittermeier
**Date**: 2026-08-03
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.01864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01864)

## Summary

This paper proposes a deep-learning-based forecasting framework for European drought prediction that integrates internal climate variability from large climate model ensembles to improve risk-averse adaptation planning. The authors introduce an uncertainty-aware drought bound representing physically plausible lower-tail trajectories of future drought conditions. Empirical evaluations demonstrate that this ensemble-informed bound is better calibrated during anomalously dry conditions compared to lower bounds derived solely from reanalysis data, which tend to underestimate lower-tail drought risk.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a deep-learning-based forecasting framework for European drought prediction that incorporates internal climate variability from large climate model ensembles.
- Introduces an uncertainty-aware drought bound representing a physically plausible lower-tail trajectory of future drought conditions.
- Demonstrates that the ensemble-informed bound is better calibrated across regions and seasons, especially during anomalously dry conditions where reanalysis data alone underestimates lower-tail drought risk.

# reGRAF: a global MPAS reforecast data set with convection-allowing refinements over the US and Europe

**Authors**: Thomas M. Hamill, Akshay Subramaniam, Montgomery L. Flora, Raghu Raj Prasanna Kumar, Karthik Kashinath, Carl G. Ponder, Tao Ge, Sepideh Khajehie, John Wong, Brett Wilt, Peter Neilley
**Date**: 2026-09-30
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.40133](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40133)

## Summary

The paper introduces reGRAF, a global high-resolution reforecast dataset generated by NVIDIA and The Weather Company using the MPAS-based GRAF atmospheric model. The dataset features variable mesh refinement with ~4 km resolution over the US, Caribbean, and Europe, and 15 km globally, spanning 1,836 cases between 2004 and 2024 initialized from ERA5. Designed around high-impact weather events, the reforecasts provide ultra-high temporal resolution (15-min regular fields, 5-min precipitation) up to +27 h lead times and are publicly accessible on AWS.

## Key Contributions

- Introduced reGRAF, a global MPAS reforecast dataset generated using TWC's GRAF model with convection-allowing refinements (~4 km over the US and Europe, 15 km elsewhere).
- Comprises 1,836 reforecast cases (~5 years of effective data) spanning 2004–2024, initialized from ERA5 and targeted at high-impact weather scenarios.
- Provides high-frequency output (15-min intervals for most fields, 5-min cadence for precipitation) up to +27 h lead times, hosted publicly on AWS Open Data.

## Limitations

Reforecasts are primarily limited to +27 h lead times with a 3-h spin-up, focusing on targeted high-impact weather events rather than contiguous multi-year daily sequences.

# INFERENCE ON COMMON TRENDS IN FUNCTIONAL TIME SERIES

**Authors**: Morten Ørregaard Nielsen, Won‐Ki Seo, Dakyung Seong
**Date**: 2026-07-29
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2312.00590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00590)

## Summary

This paper investigates statistical inference for unit roots and cointegration of time series taking values in a Hilbert space. The authors develop methods to determine the number of common stochastic trends and test hypotheses regarding nonstationary and stationary subspaces. Their framework is asymptotically valid even when the time series occupies a subspace of possibly unknown or arbitrarily large dimension, making it applicable to high-dimensional factor models and curve-valued time series.

## Key Contributions

- Developed statistical inference methods for unit roots and cointegration in Hilbert space-valued functional time series.
- Created tests to determine the number of common stochastic trends (dimension of the nonstationary subspace).
- Formulated hypothesis tests for both the nonstationary and stationary subspaces that remain asymptotically valid in arbitrarily large or unknown dimensions.

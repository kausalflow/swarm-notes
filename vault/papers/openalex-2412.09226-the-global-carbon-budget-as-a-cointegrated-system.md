# The Global Carbon Budget as a cointegrated system

**Authors**: Mikkel Bennedsen, Eric T. Hillebrand, Morten Ørregaard Nielsen
**Date**: 2026-09-22
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2412.09226](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09226)

## Summary

This paper analyzes the Global Carbon Budget's four primary annual time series—atmospheric CO2 concentrations, anthropogenic emissions, and land/ocean uptake—as a cointegrated multivariate system. Using statistical tests, the authors show that the system is cointegrated with rank three, driven by anthropogenic CO2 emissions as a single stochastic trend. They introduce a parametrically restricted error-correction model that captures physical conservation and uptake relations, demonstrating successful out-of-sample climate projections under Shared Socioeconomic Pathways scenarios.

## Key Contributions

- Identifies the Global Carbon Budget's four core time series (atmospheric CO2, anthropogenic emissions, land uptake, ocean uptake) as a cointegrated multivariate system of rank three.
- Reveals that anthropogenic CO2 emissions serve as the single stochastic trend driving the nonstationary dynamics of the global carbon cycle.
- Establishes a parametrically restricted error-correction model embodying underlying physical conservation and uptake relations that cannot be rejected via likelihood ratio tests.
- Demonstrates out-of-sample climate projections using Shared Socioeconomic Pathways scenarios that align with established climate science.

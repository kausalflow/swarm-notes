# Topological Compression of Prediction Market Belief States for Event Forecasting

**Authors**: Aadit Jerfy
**Date**: 2026-10-19
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.25756](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25756)

## Summary

This paper investigates the use of Topological Data Analysis (TDA) and Persistent Homology (PH) to model the underlying structure of prediction market belief states for event forecasting. The author demonstrates that single-parameter persistent homology enhances the predictive power and calibration of linear models, whereas two-parameter multipersistence delivers comparable improvements for nonlinear models. These findings establish that capturing the topological shape of market belief states provides valuable predictive insight across forecasting architectures.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates that single-parameter persistent homology improves predictive ability and calibrated accuracy for linear forecasting models on prediction market belief states.
- Shows that two-parameter multipersistence yields similar predictive and calibration benefits when applied to nonlinear forecasting models.
- Incorporates topological data analysis and persistent homology into forecasting models over a prediction market universe to capture underlying shape and belief state structure.

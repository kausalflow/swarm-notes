# Endogenous supply-chain transformation via dynamically calibrated nonneutroelastic processing networks

**Authors**: Satoshi Nakano, Kazuhiko Nishimura
**Date**: 2026-09-14
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.15452](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15452)

## Summary

Understanding endogenous supply chain transformations requires calibrating Cascaded CES production functions with non-neutral substitution elasticities, which is hindered by non-convexity, high dimensionality, and concavity constraints. This paper proposes a structure-exploiting algorithm that leverages upstreamness topology through alternating vertical and horizontal coordinate descents. The resulting framework successfully calibrates a 10-sector macroeconomic model of the United States, providing a tractable engine for endogenous supply chain prediction.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a structure-exploiting calibration algorithm using upstreamness topology to overcome ill-conditioning in non-neutral elasticity production networks.
- Implements a hybrid heuristic alternating between vertical cascade-sequential descent and horizontal block coordinate descent under strict microeconomic concavity constraints.
- Successfully calibrates a 10-sector macroeconomic model of the United States to endogenize complex supply-chain transformations.

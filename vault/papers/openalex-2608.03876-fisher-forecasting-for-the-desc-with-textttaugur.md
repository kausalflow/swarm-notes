## Summary

The paper introduces Augur, a software tool developed for the LSST Dark Energy Science Collaboration (DESC) to perform computationally efficient Fisher forecasting for cosmological inference. The pipeline is validated through comparisons with external codes and direct nested sampling posteriors, showing reliable consistency. Additionally, Augur is used to study modeling and hyperparameter choices for 3x2pt harmonic space analyses, providing diagnostics for future cosmological investigations.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces Augur, a Fisher forecasting tool for cosmological inference designed for the LSST Dark Energy Science Collaboration (DESC).
- Validates the Augur pipeline by comparing its forecasts against external codes and direct posterior sampling via nested sampling methods, demonstrating good agreement.
- Investigates various modeling and hyperparameter choices for a 3x2pt harmonic space analysis to supply reliable diagnostic tools for cosmological forecasting.

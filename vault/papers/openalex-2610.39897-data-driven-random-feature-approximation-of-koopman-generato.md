# Data-driven Random-feature Approximation of Koopman Generators of Nonlinear Continuous-time Systems

**Authors**: Ana Cukarska, Maximilian Gollwitzer, Viola Muning Li, Vladyslav Fediukov, Zahra Monfared, Felix Dietrich
**Date**: 2026-10-29
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.39897](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39897)

## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of learning continuous-time dynamical systems by approximating Koopman generators using a data-driven random-feature method for Galerkin methods like gEDMD. By constructing a data-informed function representation, the approach avoids data-agnostic limitations and achieves efficient, accurate forecasting and system analysis without iterative optimization. Additionally, the work provides theoretical convergence guarantees for the approximated vector fields and trajectories, while identifying limitations regarding exponential boundedness in standard gEDMD.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a data-driven random-feature method to construct parsimonious and effective function representations for Galerkin approximation methods such as gEDMD in continuous-time Koopman generator approximation.
- Proves theoretical convergence of the approximated vector field and resulting trajectories, along with a general convergence criterion for the semigroup generated by gEDMD approximation.
- Demonstrates empirically that the proposed data-informed random-feature approach is accurate and faster than iterative optimization for forecasting tasks and system analysis.

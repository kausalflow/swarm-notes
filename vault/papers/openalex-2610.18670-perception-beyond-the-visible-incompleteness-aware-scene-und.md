# Perception Beyond the Visible: Incompleteness-Aware Scene Understanding for Robot Autonomy

**Authors**: Rohit Mohan
**Date**: 2026-10-13
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.18670](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18670)

## Summary

This paper presents a research vision for incompleteness-aware scene understanding in robot autonomy, addressing incomplete evidence, incomplete knowledge, and incomplete sensor reliability. The author discusses contributions spanning amodal perception, semantic occupancy forecasting, open-world scene understanding, and multi-modal fusion. Finally, future directions are outlined toward belief-centric 4D scene representations that jointly model geometry, semantics, motion, and uncertainty to enable active sensing and safe adaptation.

## Key Contributions

- Presents a research vision for incompleteness-aware scene understanding addressing incomplete evidence, incomplete knowledge, and incomplete sensor reliability.
- Outlines contributions across amodal perception, semantic occupancy forecasting, open-world scene understanding, label-efficient learning, and uncertainty-guided multi-modal fusion.
- Proposes future directions toward belief-centric 4D scene representations combining geometry, semantics, motion, and uncertainty for active sensing and safe robot adaptation.

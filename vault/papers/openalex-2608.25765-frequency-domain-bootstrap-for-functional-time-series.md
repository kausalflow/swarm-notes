# Frequency Domain Bootstrap for Functional Time Series

**Authors**: Daniel Rademacher, Jens‐Peter Kreiß, Efstathios Paparoditis
**Date**: 2026-08-26
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.25765](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25765)

## Summary

This paper proposes a novel frequency domain bootstrap procedure for functional time series applied to spectral mean operators. By using an independent pseudo periodogram generation scheme and a projection-based decomposition, the method captures both second-order and fourth-order properties of the process. The asymptotic theory demonstrates validity and consistency under weak conditions when the decomposition parameter increases with the sample size.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a frequency domain bootstrap procedure for functional time series tailored to the class of spectral mean operators.
- Introduces an additive, projection-based decomposition of the bootstrapped spectral mean operator to capture both second-order and fourth-order characteristics.
- Establishes asymptotic validity and consistency under weak conditions for both fixed and increasing decomposition parameter m.

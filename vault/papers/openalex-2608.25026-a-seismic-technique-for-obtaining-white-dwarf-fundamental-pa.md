# A Seismic Technique for Obtaining White Dwarf Fundamental Parameters from Mean Period Spacings and Astrometry

**Authors**: Keaton J. Bell, A. Bischoff‐Kim
**Date**: 2026-08-25
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.25026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25026)

## Summary

This paper presents a novel statistical asteroseismic technique that combines precision astrometry from Gaia with time series photometry to determine fundamental physical parameters of pulsating white dwarf stars. By leveraging the monotonic and opposing variations of mean period spacings and absolute magnitudes across parameter space, the method overcomes traditional period-fitting degeneracies to yield unique solutions for mass and effective temperature. Demonstrated on the helium-atmosphere white dwarf WD 0158-160 using TESS data, the technique provides robust constraints that are largely independent of standard spectroscopic methods.

## Key Contributions

- Introduced a statistical asteroseismic technique combining Gaia astrometry and time series photometry to obtain reliable fundamental parameters (mass, effective temperature) of pulsating white dwarf stars.
- Demonstrated that mean period spacings and absolute magnitudes vary monotonically and in opposing directions across parameter space, resolving longstanding degeneracies in individual pulsation period fitting.
- Demonstrated the technique on the DBV white dwarf WD 0158-160 observed by TESS, yielding Teff = 24584 ± 971 K and M* = 0.608 ± 0.013 M_solar.

# Technical Report on Resilient and Secure Large-Scale Energy Internet Systems

**Authors**: Ioannis Zografopoulos, Karen Largman, Isaac Ortega Romero, S M Zia Ur Rashid, Yexiang Chen, George Fragkos, Charalambos Konstantinou, Subhash Lakshminarayana, Juan Ospina, Airin Rahman, Suman Rath, Vivek Kumar Singh, Mucun Sun, Wei Sun
**Date**: 2026-08-13
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.12916](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12916)

## Summary

This IEEE PES Task Force technical report examines the security and resilience of large-scale Energy Internet systems where electricity, information, and market layers are coupled through digitalization. It surveys cyber-physical threat landscapes, detection and mitigation methods, modeling frameworks for storage and electricity price forecasting, and adversarial risks of artificial intelligence deployment. Finally, it introduces attack-resilient graph-based information routing and provides recommendations for future research, standardization, and regulation.

## Key Contributions

- Surveys the cyber-physical threat landscape of large-scale Energy Internet (EI) systems coupling electricity, information, and market layers.
- Presents modeling, control, and decision-making frameworks capturing cyber-physical interdependencies, storage integration, and electricity price forecasting.
- Examines adversarial risks and trustworthy deployment of artificial intelligence in power systems alongside attack-resilient graph-based information routing.

## Limitations

As a technical report and survey, it outlines landscape challenges and future recommendations rather than reporting specific quantitative benchmark improvements.

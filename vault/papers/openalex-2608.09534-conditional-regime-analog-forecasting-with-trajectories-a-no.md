# Conditional Regime Analog Forecasting with Trajectories: A Nonparametric Framework for Multivariate Probabilistic

**Authors**: Giancarlo Vercellino
**Date**: 2026-08-10
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.09534](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09534)

## Summary

The paper introduces Conditional Regime Analog Forecasting with Trajectories (CRAFT), a nonparametric framework for multivariate probabilistic time-series prediction. CRAFT builds paired backward and forward trajectory profiles, extracts low-dimensional regime labels using singular value decomposition, change-point segmentation, and clustering, and maps backward-to-forward regime correspondence. Forecast distributions are generated by resampling historical future trajectories based on a composite compatibility score combining regime correspondence and backward profile similarity, preserving empirical cross-sectional and multi-horizon dependencies.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces Conditional Regime Analog Forecasting with Trajectories (CRAFT), a nonparametric framework for multivariate probabilistic time-series prediction.
- Constructs paired backward and forward trajectory profiles from cumulative multi-horizon returns, learning recurrent low-dimensional regime labels via singular value decomposition, change-point segmentation, and segment clustering.
- Estimates backward-to-forward conditional regime correspondence and obtains forecast distributions by sampling historical future trajectories using a composite compatibility score.
- Demonstrates preservation of empirical cross-sectional and multi-horizon dependence by resampling complete future paths compared to parametric vector autoregressions and random-forest forecasts.

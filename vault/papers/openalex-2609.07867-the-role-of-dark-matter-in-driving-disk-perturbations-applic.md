# The Role of Dark Matter in Driving Disk Perturbations: Application to LMC–SMC-like Galaxy Interactions

**Authors**: Himansh Rathore, Hayden R. Foote, Gurtina Besla, S. Varela, Nicolás Garavito-Camargo, Martin D. Weinberg, Facundo A. Gómez, Kathryne J. Daniel, Chervin F. P. Laporte
**Date**: 2026-11-02
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.07867](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07867)

## Summary

This paper investigates the role of dark matter halo distortions and satellite torques in driving stellar disk perturbations using an N-body simulation of an LMC-SMC-like galaxy interaction. By applying Basis Function Expansions (BFEs) and multichannel singular spectral analysis (mSSA), the authors quantify and separate the time-dependent torques exerted by the SMC and the LMC's own dark matter halo. The findings reveal that inner LMC disk warps effectively trace quadrupole halo distortions, establishing the LMC-SMC system as a precision laboratory for dark matter physics.

## Key Contributions

- Quantified time-dependent torques exerted on the LMC's disk by the SMC and the LMC's dark matter halo using Basis Function Expansions (BFEs) from N-body simulations of LMC-SMC-like interactions.
- Demonstrated that inner LMC disk warps serve as a promising probe of the quadrupole dark matter halo distortion.
- Showed via multichannel singular spectral analysis (mSSA) that the temporal evolution of LMC warps can be successfully separated into components correlated with halo distortions versus SMC torques.

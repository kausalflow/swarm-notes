# Gravity modes and potential evidence for Rossby Waves in late O-type supergiants

**Authors**: Lydia S. Cidale, Alejandra Christen, A. Ádám, Suryani Guha, A. Lobel, Gunther F. Avila Marín, M. Kraus, Alejandro H. Corsico, Julieta Sánchez Arias
**Date**: 2026-08-11
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.11010](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11010)

## Summary

This study analyses TESS light curves of three late O-type supergiants to investigate their internal dynamics, pulsations, and rotation. By performing period searches and examining phase diagrams against theoretical dispersion relations, the authors identify g-mode oscillations and rotational modulation. Furthermore, they report potential evidence for the excitation of global Rossby waves, shedding light on the origin of low-frequency variability in massive supergiants.

## Key Contributions

- Analyzed TESS light curves of three O-type supergiants (HD 188001, HD 192639, and HD 159952) to identify periodic signatures and internal stellar dynamics.
- Identified gravity mode (g-mode) oscillations (l=1 or l=2) along with evidence of rotational modulation across all analysed stars.
- Found potential signatures of global Rossby waves consistent with their dispersion relation, suggesting a role in the low-frequency variability of massive supergiants.

# Open Information: A Defining Perspective on Web Datasets for Carbon Pricing

**Authors**: Sidharth Mallik, Anastasios Megaritis, Waymond Rodgers
**Date**: 2026-08-05
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.04929](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04929)

## Summary

This paper introduces 'open information' as a novel conceptual category of alternative data derived from web datasets and social media, sitting between traditional public and private information. Using the GDELT dataset and EU Allowance spot prices, the authors apply VAR, GARCH-X, and return forecasting to demonstrate the material significance of open information in carbon pricing.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes 'open information' as a novel defining perspective bridging public and private information sources in financial markets.
- Demonstrates the material significance of web datasets for carbon pricing using the GDELT dataset and EU Allowance spot prices.
- Employs VAR, GARCH-X formulations, and return forecasting to validate the empirical impact of web datasets on pricing mechanisms.

## Limitations

Future work could explore a wider range of web datasets and alternative information vectors beyond GDELT across various carbon and environmental markets.

# Measures of Reliability Risk for the Australian Energy Sector

**Authors**: John Boland, Matthias Fresacher, David Hill, Shijia Jin, Ya Li, Graham Mills, Kihun Nam, Craig Oakeshotte, Andriy Olenko
**Date**: 2026-09-29
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.36751](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36751)

## Summary

This paper establishes a comprehensive set of reliability risk metrics for the Australian Energy Sector to manage power generation risks arising from variable renewable energy. The proposed approach balances economic benefits with rigorous risk management, directly interprets outage severity, and accounts for tail risks. Through simulation studies on expected unserved energy time series, the authors investigate the statistical distributions and dependency structures of these metrics to improve energy sector efficiency.

## Key Contributions

- Identifies key desired properties of reliability risk metrics for energy systems with high variable renewable energy penetration.
- Proposes a comprehensive suite of metrics designed to balance economic benefits against risk management and capture tail risk.
- Investigates statistical properties, sample distributions, and dependency structures of the proposed metrics using expected unserved energy time series simulations.

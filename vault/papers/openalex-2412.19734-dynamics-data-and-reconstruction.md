# Dynamics, Data, and Reconstruction

**Authors**: Suddhasattwa Das, Tomoharu Suda
**Date**: 2026-07-29
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2412.19734](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19734)

## Summary

This paper investigates the data-driven learning of dynamical systems through a category-theoretic framework, addressing fundamental ambiguities such as noninjectivity and topologically interweaving subsystems. The authors formulate time series data rigorously as a chain of measurement sequences with decreasing information content and prove that both data generation and reconstruction are functorial operations. This perspective offers new theoretical insights into the nature and intrinsic limits of dynamical system reconstruction algorithms.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates that ambiguities in data-driven dynamical systems learning are circumvented by considering dynamical systems and measurement maps collectively.
- Provides a rigorous category-theoretic definition of time series data as a chain of measurement sequences with decreasing information content.
- Establishes that the conversion of observed dynamical systems into time series objects and the subsequent reconstruction process are functorial operations under mild consistency conditions.

# Transformer-Based Time-Series Inference of Lindblad Dynamics in Open Quantum Systems

**Authors**: Julian Guam, Jianqing Liu
**Date**: 2026-10-05
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.05647](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05647)

## Summary

This paper presents a Transformer-based time-series inference framework for identifying unknown Lindblad dynamics and Hamiltonian parameters in open quantum systems. By directly ingesting raw trajectories of Pauli expectation values, the proposed model eliminates the need for manual feature engineering while robustly handling multiple dissipation channels and realistic measurement noise. Experiments demonstrate consistently high reconstruction accuracy across various scenarios, offering a scalable approach for quantum environment sensing.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a raw time-series Transformer that directly ingests Pauli expectation value trajectories to infer unknown Lindblad dynamics in open quantum systems.
- Extends the Transformer architecture to jointly learn unknown Hamiltonian parameters and handle multiple dissipation channels without manual feature engineering.
- Demonstrates robust system identification and environment sensing under realistic measurement noise across multiple scenarios.

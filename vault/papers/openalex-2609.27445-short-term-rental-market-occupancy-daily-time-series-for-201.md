# Short-term rental market occupancy - daily time series for 2017-2022 on 500 markets worldwide

**Authors**: Marthe Elisabeth Aastveit, Andreas Buschermöhle, Alex Lenkoski, Þórdís Þórarinsdóttir
**Date**: 2026-09-23
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.27445](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27445)

## Summary

This paper presents a comprehensive global dataset of daily short-term rental market occupancy covering 500 markets worldwide from 2017 through 2022. Sourced from Wheelhouse, the dataset captures both daily occupancy time series and booking trajectory curves up to a year prior to the stay date, bridging time series analysis and survival data. This resource is designed to serve as a robust methodological benchmark for classical statistical and machine learning modeling of tourism and rental demand.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a large-scale global dataset of daily short-term rental occupancy time series spanning 500 markets worldwide from 2017 to 2022.
- Provides booking trajectory curves for each market and stay date up to one year prior, combining time series with survival analysis data.
- Serves as a rigorous methodological benchmark for evaluating both classical statistical models and machine learning forecasting algorithms.

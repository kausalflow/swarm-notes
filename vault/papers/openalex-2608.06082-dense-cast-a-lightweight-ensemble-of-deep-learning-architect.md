# Dense-Cast: A lightweight ensemble of deep learning architectures for precipitation nowcasting

**Authors**: Gourav Jyoti Kalita, Hidam Kumarjit Singh
**Date**: 2026-08-06
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.06082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06082)

## Summary

This paper presents Dense-Cast, a lightweight deep learning ensemble combining DenseNet, residual connections, and transformer encoders for half-hourly precipitation nowcasting. Designed to capture complex temporal dependencies in spatiotemporal data with reduced parameters, the model is evaluated on the GPM IMERG dataset for the North-Eastern region of India. Experimental results demonstrate robust nowcasting performance, achieving a mean absolute error of 0.235 mm, root mean square error of 0.735 mm, and a Kling-Gupta Efficiency score of 0.816.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces Dense-Cast, a lightweight deep learning model combining DenseNet, residual connections, and transformer encoders for precipitation nowcasting.
- Evaluated on the GPM IMERG precipitation dataset over the North-Eastern region of India using half-hourly data.
- Achieves a best MAE of 0.235 mm, RMSE of 0.735 mm, and KGE score of 0.816 at a 30-minute interval predicting the next two half-hours from five past time steps.

# Is Seismic Forecasting Possible with Physics-based AI?

**Authors**: Victoria Keane, Manolis Veveakis, Thomas Poulet
**Date**: 2026-08-31
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.30861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30861)

## Summary

This paper investigates whether seismic forecasting is possible using physics-based AI by introducing a physics-based attractor that couples subduction multi-physics with AI-assisted manifold detection. By filtering out data noise and identifying earthquake footprints in surface displacement series, the approach successfully predicts an earthquake in New Zealand's Hikurangi trench one week early. Furthermore, the results show that predictability limits can extend to 5-6 weeks with decadal repeatability, supporting the fundamental determinism of seismic forecasting.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a physics-based attractor that couples multi-physics of subduction zones with AI-assisted manifold detection to overcome data noise in surface displacement series.
- Demonstrates successful prediction of an earthquake in New Zealand's Hikurangi trench one week in advance.
- Extends predictability limits to 5-6 weeks with decadal repeatability, demonstrating fundamental determinism in physics-based seismic forecasting.

# Revisiting Identity and Spectra Dispersion in Media-Bridged Time Series Forecasting: Linking Multivariate Signals and Narrative Flows

**Authors**: Jierui Lei, Wenjian Zhang, Qingyi Yang, Yuyang Hong, Fangzheng Chen, Zhengbo Zhang, 唐海娜, Shiming Xiang
**Date**: 2026-10-08
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.11924](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11924)

## Summary

This paper introduces the Multimedia Identity-Aware Prism Network (MIDAPN), a unified spatiotemporal forecasting backbone designed to bridge traditional multivariate signals and emerging multimodal narrative flows. MIDAPN combines a Multimedia Identity-Aware Graph (MIDAG) with Contextual Identity Modulation (CIM) to resolve media identity disentanglement, alongside Spectral Prism Convolution (SPConv) to handle temporal-scale mismatches. Comprehensive evaluations across numerous multivariate and multimodal datasets demonstrate consistent superiority over existing state-of-the-art models and time series foundation models.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes the Multimedia Identity-Aware Prism Network (MIDAPN) as a unified spatiotemporal forecasting backbone bridging multivariate signals and narrative flows.
- Introduces the Multimedia Identity-Aware Graph (MIDAG) with Contextual Identity Modulation (CIM) to model static essence, dynamic behavior, and latent commonality across media.
- Develops Spectral Prism Convolution (SPConv) with Adaptive Search Guidance for hierarchical temporal analysis and temporal-dimension reconstruction.
- Evaluates MIDAPN across 13 multivariate and 12 multimodal datasets, comparing against 16 SOTA TSF models, 14 time series foundation models, and fused pretrained language models.

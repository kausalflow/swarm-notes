# Transformer-based Diffusion models for Hydrological Time Series Probabilistic Imputation and Forecasting

**Authors**: Ferdinand Bhavsar, Lionel Benoît, Maxime Savatier, Edith Gabriel
**Date**: 2026-07-23
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2607.21200](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21200)

## Summary

This paper investigates transformer-based diffusion models for probabilistic imputation and forecasting of complex hydrometeorological time series under sparse observation settings. Evaluated on over 15 years of water quantity and quality data across three headwater catchments in France, the framework effectively handles variable missing data and outperforms baseline approaches in capturing temporal dynamics.

## Key Contributions

- Investigates the application of transformer-based diffusion models for probabilistic imputation and forecasting of hydrological time series across sparse observation sites.
- Applies the proposed framework to joint modeling of water quantity and quality data spanning over 15 years across three adjacent headwater catchments in North-East France.
- Demonstrates through quantitative evaluation that diffusion models efficiently sample realistic time series distributions for handling variable missing data in both forecasting and imputation tasks.

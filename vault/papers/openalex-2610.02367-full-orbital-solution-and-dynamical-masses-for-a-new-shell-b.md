# Full orbital solution and dynamical masses for a new shell-Be + sdOB binary AN Col

**Authors**: R. Klement, Th. Rivinius, A. C. Carciofi, A. Chaturvedi, Douglas R. Gies, A. C. Fonseca Silva, Pâmela R. Querido
**Date**: 2026-10-09
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.02367](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02367)

## Summary

This paper presents the full three-dimensional orbital solution, dynamical masses, and dynamical parallax for AN Col, a newly discovered shell-Be + sdOB binary. By combining near-infrared interferometry from GRAVITY with double-lined spectroscopy, the authors separate disk-wide density oscillations to reveal an 87.7-day orbit and determine stellar masses of 8.39 M_sun for the Be star and 0.81 M_sun for the stripped sdOB companion. AN Col serves as a rare benchmark system providing critical constraints on post-mass-transfer binary evolution and mass transfer efficiency in Be star formation.

## Key Contributions

- Derived the first full three-dimensional orbital solution and parallax-independent dynamical masses for the Be + sdOB binary AN Col by combining near-infrared interferometry time series from GRAVITY and double-lined spectroscopy.
- Characterized a disk-wide density oscillation cycle with a ~5.1-year period to separate spectroscopic variations from orbital signals in AN Col.
- Established AN Col as only the second Be + sdOB binary with a complete orbital solution, providing key constraints for binary mass transfer and stellar evolution models.

# Frequency-Based Reservoir computing

**Authors**: Arthur S Powanwe
**Date**: 2026-07-27
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2607.24420](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24420)

## Summary

The paper introduces a frequency-based reservoir computing framework inspired by brain oscillatory dynamics and nonlinear oscillator theory, replacing traditional random recurrent networks with independent oscillatory units that selectively process input frequencies. This approach provides a theoretical understanding of reservoir internal behavior, matches or exceeds random reservoir performance, enables optimization for short-term prediction, and successfully models complex spatiotemporal dynamics.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a frequency-based reservoir computing framework inspired by brain oscillatory dynamics and forced nonlinear oscillator theory.
- Models the reservoir as an ensemble of independent oscillatory units that selectively amplify and store specific input frequency components.
- Demonstrates competitive or superior forecasting performance compared to equivalent random reservoirs while enabling systematic optimization for short-term prediction.
- Shows capability in predicting complex spatiotemporal dynamics without random network connectivity.

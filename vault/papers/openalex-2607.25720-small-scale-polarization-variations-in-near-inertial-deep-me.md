# Small-scale polarization variations in near-inertial deep Mediterranean horizontal waterflow

**Authors**: Hans van Haren
**Date**: 2026-07-28
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2607.25720](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25720)

## Summary

This paper reports deep-sea mooring observations of horizontal waterflow differences in the 2500-m deep Mediterranean Sea, where weak vertical stratification and slow waterflow are dominated by inertial internal waves and sub-mesoscale eddies. The observations reveal that horizontal waterflow differences increase when polarization switches sign, transitioning between common anticyclonic polarization under near-homogeneous conditions and uncommon cyclonic polarization under stratified conditions. This small-scale alternation aligns with non-traditional inertio-gravity wave theory in the presence of strong turbulent convection causing local reduced stratification.

## Key Contributions

- Reported deep-sea observations of horizontal waterflow differences showing relative vorticity reaching the inertial frequency f in the 2500-m deep Mediterranean Sea.
- Identified an alternation between common anticyclonic and uncommon cyclonic polarization of inertial motions over 50-m vertical distances under varying stratification conditions.
- Demonstrated that polarization switches align with non-traditional inertio-gravity wave theory when relatively strong turbulent convection causes local reduced stratification.

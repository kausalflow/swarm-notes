# Communicating Uncertainty in AI-Supported Nurse Scheduling: Expert Co-Design and Evaluation of an Explainable AI Prototype

**Authors**: Alice Rollwagen, Emily Schiller, Anton Hummel, Kathrin Ebertsch, Andreas Riener, Jan-Philipp Steghöfer
**Date**: 2026-10-13
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.18679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18679)

## Summary

This paper investigates human-AI collaboration in nurse scheduling by developing and evaluating an explainable AI prototype through a mixed-methods user study with domain experts. The authors find that uncertainty communication in AI forecasts is often misinterpreted and lowers user confidence, while explanations are well-received but do not significantly impact advice acceptance or decision confidence. The study culminates in eight practical design implications for transparent healthcare decision support tools.

## Key Contributions

- Developed an iterative prototype decision support system for AI-supported nurse scheduling using a human-centered co-design approach.
- Evaluated the impact of uncertainty communication and AI advice explanations on healthcare professionals through a mixed-methods user study combining thematic analysis, questionnaires, and eye tracking.
- Demonstrated that AI forecast uncertainty is frequently misinterpreted and decreases decision confidence, whereas explanations are viewed positively but do not significantly alter advice acceptance or confidence.
- Derived eight concrete design implications for transparent and effective AI-supported decision-making tools in healthcare operations.

## Limitations

Limited to expert user evaluation of a specific prototype; generalizability to other complex scheduling domains requires further study.

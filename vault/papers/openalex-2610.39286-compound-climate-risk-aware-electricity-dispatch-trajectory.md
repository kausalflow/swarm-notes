# Compound Climate Risk-Aware Electricity Dispatch: Trajectory-Based Modeling for Power System Resilience

**Authors**: Feiyu Cai, Jng Qiu, Chenxi Zhang, Yi Yang, Yuechuan Tao, Xin Lu
**Date**: 2026-10-29
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.39286](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39286)

## Summary

This paper introduces a compound climate risk-aware electricity dispatch framework to address temporally compounding hazards like tropical cyclones, blackouts, and heatwaves that threaten power system resilience. By modeling risk through entire feeder connection trajectories parameterized by uncertain restoration times and subsequent hazards, the method captures sustained operational flexibility requirements that conventional Value-at-Risk formulations miss. Analytical moment-generating functions and exponential-cone reformulations make the risk constraints computationally tractable, avoiding explicit joint-state enumeration. Case studies using real-world time-series power outage data from Puerto Rico, including Hurricane Fiona (2022), demonstrate the framework's effectiveness.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a compound climate risk-aware electricity dispatch framework using a trajectory-based compound risk model for power system resilience.
- Develops analytical moment-generating functions and equivalent exponential-cone reformulations to provide a compact representation of risk constraints without joint-state enumeration.
- Demonstrates the effectiveness of the method using real-world time-series power outage data from Puerto Rico, including data from Hurricane Fiona in 2022.

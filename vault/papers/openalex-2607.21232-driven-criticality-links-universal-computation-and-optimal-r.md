# Driven criticality links universal computation and optimal representations

**Authors**: Adrián Roig, Miguel A. Muñoz, Guillermo B. Morales
**Date**: 2026-07-23
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2607.21232](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21232)

## Summary

This paper investigates the relationship between near-critical dynamics and enhanced computation using reservoir computing frameworks. The authors extend fixed-reservoir universality to discrete-time input-driven reservoirs and link neighborhood separation to dynamics through a finite-resolution neighborhood separability index and an input-conditioned maximal Lyapunov exponent. They find that neighborhood separability, chaotic time-series forecasting, and optimal representation geometry coincide within a narrow window of marginal driven stability where covariance spectra exhibit power-law scaling.

## Key Contributions

- Extends fixed-reservoir universality results to discrete-time input-driven reservoirs
- Introduces a finite-resolution neighborhood separability index and an input-conditioned maximal Lyapunov exponent to connect geometry and dynamics
- Demonstrates that neighborhood separability, chaotic time-series prediction, and smooth high-dimensional representation geometry are optimized within a narrow window of marginal driven stability
- Shows that the covariance spectrum approaches power-law scaling in the optimal regime corresponding to near-optimal smooth representations

# Near-infrared periodicity in the SPICY catalog. True young stellar objects versus contaminants

**Authors**: C. Ordenes-Huanca, A. Bayo, M. Zoccali, V. Guzmán, A. Rojas-Arriagada
**Date**: 2026-10-06
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.37779](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37779)

## Summary

This paper investigates contamination in the SPICY young stellar object (YSO) catalog by utilizing near-infrared time-series analysis to distinguish between true YSOs and dusty asymptotic giant branch stars. By cross-matching SPICY with the VIRAC2 catalog, the authors construct light curves for over 58,000 sources, isolating 742 long-period variable (LPV) contaminants and 307 high-confidence periodic YSOs based on period and amplitude thresholds. Their findings confirm distinct physical origins and spatial distributions for the two populations, enhancing the reliability of spatially unbiased YSO searches.

## Key Contributions

- Assessed contamination in the SPICY catalog by distinguishing true young stellar objects (YSOs) from dusty asymptotic giant branch stars using near-infrared time-series analysis.
- Cross-matched SPICY and VIRAC2 catalogs to build near-IR light curves for 58,737 common sources, identifying 742 long-period variable (LPV) contaminants and 307 high-confidence periodic YSO candidates.
- Demonstrated that near- and mid-IR amplitudes are correlated only among LPVs, and showed that spatial distributions align with expected populations (LPVs in the bulge, YSOs at low galactic latitudes).

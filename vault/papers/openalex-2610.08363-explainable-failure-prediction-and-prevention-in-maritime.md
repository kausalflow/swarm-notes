# Explainable Failure Prediction and Prevention in Maritime

**Authors**: Dionisis Kalogeropoulos, Georgia Sovatzidi, Panagiotis G. Kalozoumis, Δημήτρης Κ. Ιακωβίδης
**Date**: 2026-10-06
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.08363](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08363)

## Summary

This chapter reviews AI technologies for failure prediction and prevention in maritime systems, proposing a closed-loop conceptual architecture for trustworthy decision-making. The framework integrates time-series forecasting, anomaly detection, risk assessment, and explainable AI to support autonomous or human-in-the-loop corrective actions. Furthermore, the authors survey existing maritime studies and outline open challenges such as data scarcity, model robustness, and operational deployment.

## Key Contributions

- Reviews key AI technologies for explainable failure prediction and prevention in maritime systems.
- Presents a closed-loop conceptual architecture integrating data acquisition, time-series forecasting, anomaly detection, risk assessment, decision-making, and explainable AI.
- Evaluates relevant maritime studies, highlighting current challenges such as uncertainty, robustness, explainability, and dataset scarcity.

## Limitations

Identifies key challenges including limited availability of maritime datasets, model generalization, and operational deployment hurdles.

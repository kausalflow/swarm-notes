# EpiGuard: An Autonomous Infectious-Disease Early Warning Agent Platform

**Authors**: Tonglong Wei, Zheng Yuan, Lingshuai Lin, Yuhui Yao, Xing Xie, Na Zou
**Date**: 2026-10-14
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.19396](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19396)

## Summary

EpiGuard is an autonomous, continually self-improving agentic platform designed for early warning and risk assessment of over 50 infectious diseases across diverse geographic regions. The platform tackles fragmented evidence, uneven data availability, and delayed reporting by dynamically adjusting its analysis across three evidence regimes and preserving successful workflows as reusable standard operating procedures. Evaluated on official surveillance data from the China CDC and U.S. CDC, EpiGuard outperforms existing forecasting baselines by up to 14.04%.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces EpiGuard, an autonomous, continually self-improving agentic platform for infectious-disease early warning across more than 50 diseases.
- Implements a dynamic analytical strategy operating across three evidence availability regimes to handle sparse, abundant, or absent historical data.
- Demonstrates superior performance over state-of-the-art forecasting systems, improving performance by 9.68% and 14.04% on official China CDC and U.S. CDC surveillance datasets.

## Archivist Review

No novel concepts meet the strict long-term reusability criteria; EpiGuard is an application framework for infectious-disease forecasting rather than a core foundational ML mechanism.

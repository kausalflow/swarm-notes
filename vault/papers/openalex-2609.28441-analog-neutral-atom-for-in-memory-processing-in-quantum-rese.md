# Analog neutral-atom for in-memory processing in quantum reservoir computing

**Authors**: Luca de Nigro, Gian Luca Giorgi, Enrico Prati, Roberta Zambrini
**Date**: 2026-09-23
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.28441](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28441)

## Summary

This paper investigates analog neutral-atom systems for in-memory processing within quantum reservoir computing (QRC), addressing the challenge of realizing intrinsic temporal memory without classical buffering. The authors simulate and identify specific non-unitary dynamical regimes—particularly controlled dissipation and operation at the edge of quantum chaos—required to satisfy the echo state property, fading memory, and separability. By implementing this framework, the approach successfully solves complex non-linear time-series tasks like the Mackey-Glass benchmark with as few as five atoms, offering a robust blueprint for near-term quantum devices.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates that controlled dissipation is strictly necessary to induce the echo state property, fading memory, and separability in neutral atom array quantum reservoir computing.
- Shows that computational performance is maximized at the edge of quantum chaos using neutral-atom quantum reservoirs.
- Successfully solves the complex non-linear Mackey-Glass time series task using as few as N=5 neutral atoms.

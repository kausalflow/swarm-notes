# Hyperchaos in a Magnetic Nanodisk Driven by Ferromagnetic Resonance

**Authors**: A Kolli, Hugo Merbouche, Salvatore Perna, Claudio Serpico, Damien Rontani, Grégoire de Loubens, Hugo Merbouche, Salvatore Perna, Claudio Serpico, Damien Rontani, Grégoire de Loubens
**Date**: 2026-07-30
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2607.27884](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27884)

## Summary

This paper investigates the nonlinear ferromagnetic resonance dynamics of an out-of-plane magnetized nanodisk using micromagnetic simulations and time-series analysis. The authors discover that the nanoscale system can exhibit robust hyperchaotic behavior characterized by up to three positive Lyapunov exponents across vast regions of the control parameter space. A mode projection technique further reveals that this dynamical complexity arises from the number of interacting quantized spin-wave modes, highlighting magnon-spintronic nanodevices as promising physical entropy sources.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrated through micromagnetic simulations that an out-of-plane magnetized nanodisk driven by ferromagnetic resonance exhibits hyperchaotic dynamics with up to three positive Lyapunov exponents.
- Mapped topological transitions from stable periodic orbits to strange attractors across the control parameter space.
- Linked the emergence of dynamical complexity and hyperchaos to the participation of multiple quantized spin-wave modes using a mode projection technique.

## Limitations

Specific to micromagnetic simulations of nanoscale magnetic disks; experimental verification in physical hardware remains to be fully explored in detail.

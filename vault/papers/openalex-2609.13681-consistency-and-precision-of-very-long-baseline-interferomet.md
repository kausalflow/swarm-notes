# Consistency and precision of very long baseline interferometry source coordinate time series

**Authors**: Xiaxuan Zhang, Niu Liu, S. Lambert, K. Le Bail, Hana Krásná, Oleg Titov, María Karbon, Tobias Nilsson, Zi Zhu, Jiacheng Liu, Ibnu Nurul Huda, Jun Yang, Xiaopeng Cheng, Bong Won Sohn, Fengchun Shu, Xuan He, Zhenwei Wang, Zhi-Yun Zhang, Hua-Feng Yu, Jun Yao, Dading Zhang
**Date**: 2026-09-18
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.13681](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13681)

## Summary

This paper investigates the consistency and precision of extragalactic radio source coordinate time series derived from very long baseline interferometry (VLBI) observations across different analysis centers. By analyzing eight solutions covering 496 common sources, the authors evaluate inter-solution differences, realistic errors using the N-cornered-hat method, and the impact of processing strategies and analysis configurations. The results show that solutions generally remain consistent, with precision heavily influenced by source declination and observation count, providing a robust pathway to estimate realistic stochastic errors beyond formal errors.

## Key Contributions

- Constructed a common sample of 496 extragalactic radio sources from eight VLBI coordinate time-series solutions across seven analysis centers.
- Evaluated external consistency and realistic errors using pairwise positional offsets, correlation analysis, and the N-cornered-hat (NCH) method.
- Demonstrated that solutions using similar processing strategies, software packages, or source constraints exhibit higher consistency and precision.

## Limitations

Future work will explore broader sets of analysis configurations and deeper investigations into systematic errors across VLBI software packages.

# Qualitative analysis, chaotic structure and exact solution of the nonlinear seventh-order Caudrey-Dodd-Gibbon-KP equation

**Authors**: S.S. Samanta, S. Sahoo, Vijil Kumar, Rajib Mia
**Date**: 2026-08-05
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.04846](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04846)

## Summary

This paper investigates the qualitative properties, chaotic structures, and exact traveling wave solutions of the (2+1)-dimensional seventh-order Caudrey-Dodd-Gibbon-KP (sCDG-KP) equation. By applying a suitable wave transformation, the governing nonlinear partial differential equation is reduced to an ordinary differential equation, yielding bright and anti-kink soliton solutions via the G'/G+G+A method. Furthermore, the authors perform extensive bifurcation, phase portrait, attractor, sensitivity, and recurrence analyses to characterize the complex chaotic behavior of the system.

## Key Contributions

- Investigates traveling wave solutions and dynamic characteristics of the (2+1)-dimensional seventh-order Caudrey-Dodd-Gibbon-KP (sCDG-KP) equation.
- Derives exact bright and anti-kink soliton solutions using the advanced G'/G+G+A expansion method via wave transformations.
- Performs comprehensive bifurcation, phase portrait, sensitivity, and chaotic attractor analysis on the reduced dynamical system.

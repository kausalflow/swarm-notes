## Summary

This white paper outlines the preliminary architecture, performance goals, and scientific mission of a proposed 3.5-meter segmented-mirror robotic space telescope designed for precision astrophysics and rapid-response transient astronomy across the 0.2-1.5 micron wavelength range. The observatory features a Cassegrain optical layout achieving diffraction-limited performance and a Strehl ratio above 0.8 at 633 nm, supported by a payload comprising a Wide-field Camera, a spectroscopic instrument, and an optional Exoplanet Imaging Coronagraph. Planned science investigations range from multi-messenger gravitational-wave counterparts and supernovae cosmology to direct exoplanet imaging and atmospheric characterization.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a preliminary architecture and mission overview for a 3.5-meter segmented-mirror robotic space telescope operating in the 0.2-1.5 micron wavelength range.
- Details an optical design utilizing a Cassegrain configuration optimized for diffraction-limited performance with a Strehl ratio greater than 0.8 at 633 nm.
- Outlines core scientific missions including gravitational-wave counterparts, rapid transients, Type Ia supernovae, direct exoplanet imaging, and atmospheric spectroscopy.

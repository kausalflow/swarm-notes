# One Probability, Two Roles: The Separation of Coherence and Frequency in Adaptive Regimes

**Authors**: Yonggang Lu
**Date**: 2026-09-03
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.04115](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04115)

## Summary

The paper formally examines the dual roles of probability in modern data analysis and AI-mediated settings: internal coherence for sequential forecasting versus empirical frequency-based reliability under a governing law. It introduces the Role Separation Principle to distinguish these roles and develops a unified stabilization framework across various regimes, operationalized through an audit checklist.

## Key Contributions

- Synthesizes prequential forecasting, calibration, martingale validity, adaptive data analysis, conformal prediction, and AI reliability into a formal account of role separation.
- Establishes the Role Separation Principle distinguishing internal coherence from conditional-mean correctness and stability conditions.
- Develops a unified stabilization framework spanning filtration-based, shift-based, policy-environment, and design-based regimes with an operational audit checklist.

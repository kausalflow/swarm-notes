# Non-invasive Seizure Detection Using Wearable Wrist-worn Accelerometry and Deep Learning

**Authors**: Nilushika Udayangani Hewa Dehigahawattage, Kishor Nandakishor, Marimuthu Palaniswami
**Date**: 2026-10-05
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.05919](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05919)

## Summary

This paper presents a non-invasive wearable seizure detection system utilizing a single wrist-worn accelerometer paired with a time-series ResNet architecture. Evaluated on 5576 hours of recording from 79 patients monitored via video-electroencephalography, the system achieves a 95.65% seizure detection sensitivity with a low false alarm rate of 0.15 per 24 hours. The approach demonstrates strong potential for reliable, long-term ambulatory seizure monitoring outside hospital settings.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed a non-invasive wearable seizure detection system using a single wrist-worn accelerometer and a deep time-series ResNet architecture.
- Achieved a seizure detection sensitivity of 95.65% and a low false alarm rate of 0.15/24 hours across 5576 hours of recording from 79 patients.
- Demonstrated an AUC-ROC of 0.98 and AUC-PRC of 0.67 for convulsive seizures across 20 evaluated patients.

## Limitations

Validation currently limited to specific patient cohorts; future work includes validating on larger datasets and expanding to broader seizure types.

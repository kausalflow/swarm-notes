# Multistep and Multivariate Time Series Explainability with GradientSHAP: A Wind Power Case Study

**Authors**: Robbie Southam, Fan Zhong, Ke Li
**Date**: 2026-10-29
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.40330](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40330)

## Summary

This paper introduces a carefully adapted GradientSHAP framework to provide local and global interpretability for multivariate, multistep wind power forecasting, a critical task for day-ahead grid management. By combining SHAP values with novel visualizations and domain expertise, the study uncovers complex relationships between environmental factors, site conditions, and temporal dynamics. The findings demonstrate that GradientSHAP can effectively lift model opacity, though its complexity requires further streamlining for routine operational deployment.

## Key Contributions

- Applies GradientSHAP for the first time to multivariate, multistep wind power forecasting to address model opacity.
- Develops novel visualizations for SHAP values to handle high dimensionality and deliver local and global explanations.
- Uncovers intricate relationships between environmental factors, site-specific conditions, and temporal dynamics for day-ahead grid management.

## Limitations

GradientSHAP is not an out-of-the-box solution for this setting and requires thoughtful handling and domain expertise, and its complexity poses challenges for practical implementation.

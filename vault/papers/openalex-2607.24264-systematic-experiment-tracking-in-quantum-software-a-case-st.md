# Systematic Experiment Tracking in Quantum Software: A Case Study of Reservoir Computing with Error Mitigation

**Authors**: Otso Kinanen, Valter Uotila, Vlad Stirbu
**Date**: 2026-07-27
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2607.24264](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24264)

## Summary

This paper investigates systematic experiment tracking in quantum software development, focusing on the specific monitoring and error-tracking demands introduced by noisy quantum hardware and evolving stacks. The authors present a case study applying quantum reservoir computing to chaotic time series data prediction with error mitigation, detailing the software development process and tracking practices. Finally, these insights are generalized into a broader framework for quantum development.

## Key Contributions

- Identifies unique demands and challenges of experiment tracking in quantum software development due to experimental hardware volatility and evolving software stacks.
- Presents a detailed case study of quantum reservoir computing for chaotic time series data prediction incorporating error mitigation.
- Generalizes experiment tracking methodologies and practices into a broader quantum software development process.

## Archivist Review

No new concepts proposed; the paper is a methodology and case study on experiment tracking for quantum reservoir computing rather than introducing a standalone algorithmic primitive or architecture.

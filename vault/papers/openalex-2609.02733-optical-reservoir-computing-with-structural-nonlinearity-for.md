# Optical Reservoir Computing with Structural Nonlinearity for Forecasting Chaotic Time Series

**Authors**: Leandro R. Venâncio, J. Dong, Mickaël Mounaix, Jacopo Bertolotti
**Date**: 2026-09-02
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.02733](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02733)

## Summary

This paper introduces structural nonlinearity into an optical reservoir computing architecture using entirely linear optical components, achieving quadratic input mapping via a digital micromirror device and scattering media. Evaluated on the chaotic Mackey-Glass time series forecasting task, the proposed approach improves short-term prediction accuracy and better preserves long-term dynamics compared to conventional optical reservoirs. This demonstrates a low-power, energy-efficient route to optical computing without requiring strong light-matter interactions.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces structural nonlinearity into optical reservoir computing using only linear components and a digital micromirror device for quadratic mapping.
- Demonstrates that structural nonlinearity improves short-term prediction accuracy on chaotic time series.
- Preserves long-term dynamics of the chaotic Mackey-Glass time series better than conventional optical reservoir architectures.

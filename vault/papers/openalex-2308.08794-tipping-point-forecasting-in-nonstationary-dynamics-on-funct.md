# Tipping point forecasting in nonstationary dynamics on function spaces

**Authors**: Miguel Liu-Schiaffini, Clare E. Singer, Nikola Kovachki, Sze Chai Leung, H. Jane Bae, Kamyar Azizzadenesheli, Anima Anandkumar
**Date**: 2026-10-15
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2308.08794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08794)

## Summary

This paper proposes a recurrent neural operator (RNO) framework combined with a conformal prediction approach to forecast abrupt tipping points in nonstationary dynamical systems on function spaces. Trained exclusively on pre-tipping dynamics, the model monitors deviations from physics constraints such as conserved quantities and partial differential equations to provide rigorous uncertainty bounds. The methodology is successfully validated on ordinary and partial differential equations like Lorenz-63 and Kuramoto-Sivashinsky, as well as complex climate and fluid dynamics applications including stratocumulus cloud cover and airfoil stall transitions.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a recurrent neural operator (RNO) framework trained solely on pre-tipping dynamics to model nonstationary dynamical systems on function spaces.
- Introduces a conformal prediction framework leveraging physics constraints (conserved quantities and PDEs) to forecast abrupt tipping points with rigorous uncertainty quantification.
- Demonstrates zero-shot generalization to forecasting multiple future tipping points under varying Reynolds numbers for airfoil wake and stall transitions.

# Multi-Track Time-Series Burst-Overlap Interferometry for Resolving Horizontal Deformation in Earthquake-Cycle Studies

**Authors**: Xing Li, Zhuang Gao, Han Chen, Zhangfeng Ma, Yangkang Chen, Alexandros Savvaidis
**Date**: 2026-09-17
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.20564](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20564)

## Summary

Interferometric Synthetic Aperture Radar (InSAR) struggles with north-south crustal motion due to near-polar orbits. To overcome this, the authors propose the Multi-track Time-Series Burst-Overlap Interferometry (MTSB) framework, delivering absolute ITRF-referenced deformation fields across interseismic, coseismic, and postseismic earthquake phases. By combining phase linking, time-series deformation/misregistration separation, and block-wise spectral analysis, MTSB achieves centimeter-level and millimeter-per-year agreement with GNSS data.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces the Multi-track Time-Series Burst-Overlap Interferometry (MTSB) framework to resolve along-track horizontal deformation and north-south motion in InSAR.
- Combines optimized phase linking, a unified time-series framework for separating deformation and residual misregistration, and block-wise spectral analysis.
- Achieves centimeter-level agreement with GNSS for coseismic and postseismic displacements and millimeter-per-year agreement for interseismic velocities.

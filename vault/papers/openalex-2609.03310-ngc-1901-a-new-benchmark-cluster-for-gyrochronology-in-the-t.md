# NGC 1901: A new benchmark cluster for gyrochronology in the TESS Southern continuous viewing zone

**Authors**: M. Bernizzoni, Timothy R. Bedding, Prasad Mani, Luke G. Bouma, Courtney L. Crawford, Jason Lee Curtis, Benjamin T. Montet, Samir Nepal
**Date**: 2026-09-03
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.03310](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03310)

## Summary

The authors utilize Gaia DR3 astrometry and TESS time-series photometry to establish the neglected open cluster NGC 1901 as a new benchmark for stellar gyrochronology. By measuring rotation periods for 32 high-confidence members, they derive a gyrochronal age of 830 +/- 50 Myr, filling the gap between Praesepe and NGC 6811. Their findings reveal spin-down stalling in late K dwarfs and demonstrate that temperature-period relations mitigate sub-solar metallicity effects for Sun-like stars.

## Key Contributions

- Refined membership list and measured rotation periods for 32 high-confidence rotating stars in the open cluster NGC 1901 using Gaia DR3 astrometry and TESS time-series photometry.
- Calculated a gyrochronal age of 830 +/- 50 Myr for NGC 1901 using the gyro-interp empirical model, placing it in the temporal gap between Praesepe (670 Myr) and NGC 6811 (1 Gyr).
- Demonstrated that temperature-period relations mitigate metallicity effects for Sun-like stars at a sub-solar metallicity of [Fe/H] approx -0.11.
- Observed significant spin-down stalling in late K dwarfs, supporting core-envelope recoupling models and establishing NGC 1901 as a new calibration benchmark for gyrochronology.

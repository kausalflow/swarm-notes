# Automated Detection of Double-Lined Spectroscopic Binaries in High-Resolution Spectra and a Probabilistic Analysis of Stellar Multiplicity

**Authors**: William B. Salazar, Steven Giacalone, Andrew W. Howard
**Date**: 2026-09-09
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.09734](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09734)

## Summary

This paper introduces an automated algorithm for detecting double-lined spectroscopic binaries (SB2s) in high-resolution spectra to vet Sun-like stars for stellar companions in preparation for missions like the Habitable Worlds Observatory. Using the MOLUSC framework to simulate stellar populations, the authors evaluate the detection efficiency of the SB2 algorithm both independently and in conjunction with high-resolution adaptive optics and time-series radial velocity surveys. The combined multi-method approach successfully reduces the likelihood of undetected stellar companions around typical nearby stars to under 1%.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed an automated algorithm for detecting double-lined spectroscopic binaries (SB2s) in high-resolution spectra
- Utilized MOLUSC to simulate realistic stellar populations and quantify detection capabilities for nearby stars and future missions
- Demonstrated that combining SB2 detection, high-resolution adaptive optics, and time-series radial velocities reduces the probability of undetected stellar companions to less than 1%

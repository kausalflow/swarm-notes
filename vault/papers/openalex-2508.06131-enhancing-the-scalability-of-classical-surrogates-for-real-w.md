# Enhancing the scalability of classical surrogates for real-world quantum machine learning applications

**Authors**: Philip Anton Hernicht, Alona Sakhnenko, Corey O’Meara, G. Cortiana, J. Lorenz
**Date**: 2026-07-30
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2508.06131](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06131)

## Summary

This paper addresses the prohibitive computational bottlenecks of deploying quantum machine learning models by proposing an efficient pipeline for generating lightweight classical surrogates. The method reduces redundancies in prior approaches, scaling polynomially rather than exponentially in resource requirements. The authors validate their approach on a real-world energy demand forecasting task using both simulations and quantum hardware, showing high accuracy and drastically lower resource usage.

## Key Contributions

- Identifies prohibitive high computational demands in existing classical surrogate generation methods for quantum machine learning models.
- Proposes an efficient alternative pipeline that generates classical surrogates at scale with polynomial resource scaling instead of exponential.
- Demonstrates the practical effectiveness of the method on a real-world energy demand forecasting problem using both simulations and real quantum hardware.

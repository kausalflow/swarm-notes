# Superextensive learning in quantum reservoirs at the onset of information scrambling

**Authors**: Jonas Freiheit, Francesco Campaioli
**Date**: 2026-08-26
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.25511](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25511)

## Summary

The authors investigate quantum reservoir computing using 2D Ising networks up to N=20 spins for time-series forecasting, demonstrating that prediction precision and memory capacity exhibit superextensive power-law scaling specifically at the onset of information scrambling.

## Key Contributions

- Numerically simulates 2D Ising networks of up to N=20 spins as quantum reservoirs for time-series forecasting.
- Locates the onset of information scrambling using out-of-time-order correlators (OTOCs), showing prediction precision peaks at this boundary.
- Establishes that prediction precision and total memory capacity grow superextensively as a power law (α > 1) with reservoir size at the onset of scrambling.
- Demonstrates that scrambling enhances nonlinear memory components while reducing linear capacity, requiring a collective relaxation channel for forgetting.

## Limitations

Evaluated on system sizes up to N=20 spins due to numerical simulation constraints of quantum many-body systems.

# Physics-Guided Flow Matching: A Generative AI Framework for Global Total Electron Content Forecasting

**Authors**: Si-Tong Yang, Feng Ye, Xian Lu, Dong Lin, Benjamin C. Martinez
**Date**: 2026-10-27
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.37159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37159)

## Summary

This paper proposes a physics-guided flow matching framework for day-ahead global total electron content (TEC) forecasting by incorporating ionospheric partial differential equations into the generative transformation process. The model adaptively combines physical dynamics with data-driven patterns across spatial locations. Evaluated on one year of TIEGCM simulation data, the framework outperforms statistical, deep learning, and unguided generative benchmarks across all lead times and latitudes.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed a physics-guided flow matching generative AI framework for day-ahead global total electron content (TEC) forecasting.
- Incorporated ionospheric partial differential equations (PDEs) to guide the transformation between distributions during flow matching.
- Demonstrated a 39.2% reduction in RMSE compared to ConvLSTM and a 23.8% improvement over the unguided generative framework on TIEGCM simulation data.

# A Jupiter-like radio aurora in the L3.5 dwarf LSPM J0036+1821

**Authors**: Diego Martín-Carrero, Juan B. Climent, Paulo A. Miles-Páez, Joan Balmon, J. C. Guirado, Melodie M. Kao, Cian O'Toole, John Sebastian Pineda, Johanna M. Vos
**Date**: 2026-10-23
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.09854](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09854)

## Summary

This paper investigates the magnetic activity, rotation, and atmospheric variability of the L3.5 dwarf LSPM J0036+1821 through a multiwavelength monitoring campaign combining VLA, VLBI, and TESS data from 2019 to 2023. The authors derive precise rotation periods from both radio and optical data and successfully model the persistent, highly circularly polarized radio emission using a Jupiter-like hybrid auroral framework. Their findings demonstrate that the radio emission is driven by electron-cyclotron maser instability in a structured magnetosphere analogous to magnetized Solar System planets.

## Key Contributions

- Characterized the magnetic activity, rotation, and atmospheric variability of the L3.5 dwarf LSPM J0036+1821 using a multiwavelength analysis combining VLA, VLBI, and TESS data spanning 2019--2023.
- Derived precise rotation periods of 3.07941 hours from radio data and 3.07908 hours from optical TESS photometry.
- Successfully modeled radio light curves using a hybrid Jupiter-like auroral framework featuring main-oval and active field line components driven by electron-cyclotron maser instability.

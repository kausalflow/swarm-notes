# Beyond pre-whitening: Detection of gravity modes and their period spacings in slowly pulsating B stars using the multitaper F test

**Authors**: Aarya A. Patil, C. Aerts, N. Wang, J. Van Beeck, M. G. Pedersen
**Date**: 2026-08-25
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2512.10019](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10019)

## Summary

This paper proposes a statistically robust, data-driven approach based on the multitaper F test and the multitaper non-uniform fast Fourier transform to extract gravity modes and period spacing patterns in slowly pulsating B stars. Addressing the bias and inconsistency issues of traditional pre-whitening and Lomb-Scargle periodograms, the method scales efficiently to large 4-year Kepler light curves. Results show that the multitaper F test offers an objective alternative that recovers known modes, detects new ones, and identifies multiple period spacing patterns with minimal subjective intervention.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed the multitaper F test combined with the multitaper non-uniform fast Fourier transform as a statistically robust, data-driven alternative to traditional pre-whitening for frequency extraction of gravity modes in slowly pulsating B stars.
- Demonstrated efficient scaling on 4-year Kepler light curves, recovering most known gravity modes while revealing new ones and detecting multiple period spacing patterns.
- Provided an objective, bias-reducing framework for stellar oscillation analysis that eliminates subjective stopping criteria inherent in iterative pre-whitening.

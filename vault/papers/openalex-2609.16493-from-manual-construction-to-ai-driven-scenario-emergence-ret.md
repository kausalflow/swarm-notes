# From Manual Construction to AI-Driven Scenario Emergence: Rethinking Catastrophe Risk Modeling

**Authors**: Hang Gao
**Date**: 2026-09-15
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.16493](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16493)

## Summary

Traditional catastrophe risk models rely on manual construction methods that struggle to keep pace with intensifying climate extremes. This paper introduces the TAISE framework, which leverages AI weather forecasting models through self-iterative generation to produce continuous global atmospheric fields and emergent extreme weather events. Experiments show an order-of-magnitude reduction in computational cost while capturing crucial temporal continuity and cross-regional correlations.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes the TAISE framework, which repurposes AI weather forecasting models to generate extreme weather sequences via self-iterative generation.
- Achieves an order-of-magnitude reduction in computational cost compared to conventional manual CAT risk construction methods.
- Captures vital temporal continuity and cross-regional correlations that are typically absent in snapshot-based approaches.

## Summary

Timestral is a family of four probabilistic time series foundation models ranging from 5M to 209M parameters that establish a new state of the art on major benchmarks including GIFT-Eval, TIME, and fev-bench. The architecture leverages Decoupled Horizon Forecasting (DHF) to separate prediction tokens from causally encoded context, native known-future covariate support, and a two-stage pre-training/post-training pipeline using synthetic and real data.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces Timestral, a family of four time series foundation models ranging from 5M to 209M parameters.
- Sets a new state-of-the-art across GIFT-Eval, TIME, and fev-bench benchmarks, with Timestral-209M and Timestral-123M taking the top two places on every metric.
- Proposes Decoupled Horizon Forecasting (DHF) to separate prediction tokens from causally encoded context and support known-future covariates within a causal architecture.
- Employs a two-stage training pipeline leveraging pure large-scale synthetic pre-training followed by mixed real-synthetic post-training.

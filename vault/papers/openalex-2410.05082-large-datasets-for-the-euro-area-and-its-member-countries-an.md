# Large Datasets for the Euro Area and Its Member Countries and the Dynamic Effects of the Common Monetary Policy

**Authors**: Matteo Barigozzi, Claudio Lissona, Lorenzo Tonni
**Date**: 2026-07-29
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2410.05082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05082)

## Summary

The authors introduce EA-MD-QD, a publicly available, continually updated dataset of 1136 macroeconomic time series covering the euro area and its ten largest member countries. Using this dataset, they investigate country-specific impulse responses to a euro area-wide monetary policy shock and uncover notable cross-country heterogeneity between core and peripheral economies. Their findings suggest that factors such as homeownership and saving behavior drive the observed divergences in price and real activity responses.

## Key Contributions

- Introduced EA-MD-QD, a publicly available dataset comprising 1136 macroeconomic time series for the euro area and ten member countries, updated monthly since January 2024.
- Evaluated country-specific impulse responses to an EA-wide monetary policy shock, revealing moderate yet significant cross-country heterogeneity.
- Identified structural differences in price, interest rate, and real activity responses between core and peripheral countries, highlighting homeownership and saving behavior as potential drivers.

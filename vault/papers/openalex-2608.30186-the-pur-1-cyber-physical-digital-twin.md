# The PUR-1 Cyber-Physical Digital Twin

**Authors**: Vasileios Theos, Jonah Lau, Konstantinos Gkouliaras, Zachery Dahm, Konstantinos Vasili, Noah Fillgrove, William Richards, True Miller, Brian Jowers, Stylianos Chatzidakis
**Date**: 2026-08-31
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.30186](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30186)

## Summary

The paper presents the Purdue University Reactor One Digital Twin (PUR-1 DT), a cyber-physical digital twin integrating physics-based (neutronics, thermal-hydraulics, point kinetics) and AI-driven models for real-time decision support in nuclear facilities. PUR-1 DT maintains two-way synchronization with the physical reactor to deliver closed-loop explainable diagnostics, forecasting, and predictive control. Experimental benchmarking across full reactor operational cycles validates the system's real-time accuracy and low-latency synchronization capabilities.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces the Purdue University Reactor One Digital Twin (PUR-1 DT), integrating high-fidelity physics-based and AI-driven virtual models for real-time cyber-physical nuclear reactor control.
- Implements closed-loop explainable diagnostics, forecasting, predictive control, and two-way synchronization maintaining time constants faster than the reactor's operational cycle.
- Demonstrates real-time synchronized state estimation and short-term forecasting validated against experimental benchmarking across a full reactor operational cycle.

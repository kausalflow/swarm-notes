# A Missing Tool for Calculating Auto/Cross-correlation Function under Nonuniform Sampling Observations

**Authors**: Chen-Ran Hu, Yong-Feng Huang, Jin-Jun Geng, Orkash Amat, Ze-Cheng Zou, Chen Deng, Fan Xu, Xiao-Fei Dong, Chen Du, Nurimangul Nurmamat, Pei Wang, Lang Cui, Cheng-Ming Li
**Date**: 2026-09-08
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.08604](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08604)

## Summary

The paper introduces a rigorous method for computing nonuniform autocorrelation and cross-correlation functions (NUACF and NUCCF) for irregularly sampled time series without relying on interpolation or binning. By incorporating time-interval weights and misalignment penalties, the approach naturally evaluates correlation functions and provides a complete error budget accounting for flux uncertainties and sampling irregularity. Extensive simulations and real-world astrophysical applications demonstrate its superiority over traditional ad-hoc methods across diverse variability patterns.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a rigorous method for calculating nonuniform autocorrelation (NUACF) and cross-correlation (NUCCF) functions for irregularly sampled time series without interpolation.
- Incorporates time-interval weights and misalignment penalties to accurately evaluate correlation functions under nonuniform sampling.
- Provides a complete error budget accounting for both flux uncertainties and sampling irregularity via Monte Carlo simulations.
- Demonstrates superior performance across periodic and complex aperiodic variability patterns, with astrophysical applications to stellar light curves, AGN disc reverberation, and gravitationally lensed quasars.

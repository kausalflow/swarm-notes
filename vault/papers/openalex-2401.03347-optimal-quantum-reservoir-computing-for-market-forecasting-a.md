# Optimal quantum reservoir computing for market forecasting: An application to fight food price crises

**Authors**: L. Domingo, M. Grande, Gabriel G. Carlo, F. Borondo, J. Borondo
**Date**: 2026-08-07
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2401.03347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03347)

## Summary

This paper investigates Optimal Quantum Reservoir Computing (QRC) for financial and agri-commodity market forecasting to address food price crises. The authors propose circuit complexity evaluated via the majorization criterion as a practical forecasting performance indicator requiring only measurement statistics. Furthermore, they demonstrate that gate-based quantum reservoirs achieve strong predictive accuracy with fewer parameters than classical models and show resilience to realistic noise conditions.

## Key Contributions

- Investigates the design of quantum reservoirs and identifies circuit complexity, quantified via the majorization criterion, as a practical indicator of forecasting performance for near-term quantum devices.
- Demonstrates that quantum reservoirs built from Hadamard, controlled-NOT, and T gates achieve strong predictive accuracy while requiring fewer gates than the transverse-field Ising model.
- Shows that quantum reservoirs outperform deep learning and statistical baselines while achieving comparable or slightly superior performance to classical reservoir computing with fewer trainable parameters in data-scarce regimes.
- Explores robustness under realistic noise conditions, demonstrating that certain noise types like amplitude damping can enhance performance at low error rates.

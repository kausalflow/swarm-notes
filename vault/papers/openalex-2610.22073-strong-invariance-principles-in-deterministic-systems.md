# Strong invariance principles in deterministic systems

**Authors**: Zemer Kosloff, Dalibor Volný
**Date**: 2026-10-15
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.22073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22073)

## Summary

This paper investigates the probabilistic properties of deterministic systems, establishing strong invariance principles in aperiodic, probability-preserving dynamical systems. Specifically, the authors prove the existence of a function whose generated time series satisfies both the almost sure invariance principle with a rate asymptotically smaller than the square root of n and the almost sure weak invariance principle.

## Key Contributions

- Proof that every aperiodic, probability-preserving dynamical system contains a function whose associated time series satisfies the almost sure invariance principle with a rate asymptotically smaller than the square root of n.
- Demonstration that such time series also satisfy the almost sure weak invariance principle of Brosamler and Schatte.

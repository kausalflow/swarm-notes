# A Brief Review on the Statistical Properties of Fast Radio Bursts

**Authors**: Yu Sang, Hai-Nan Lin
**Date**: 2026-09-22
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.25810](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25810)

## Summary

This paper provides a comprehensive review of the statistical properties of Fast Radio Bursts (FRBs), synthesizing findings from current catalogs on dispersion measures, redshifts, energy functions, repetition rates, and host galaxies. It covers time-series analyses of repeating bursts—including waiting times, memory effects, and chaotic dynamics—as well as periodic activity and polarization properties. The review highlights robust empirical trends while carefully accounting for observational biases and selection effects to constrain the underlying physical mechanisms.

## Key Contributions

- Synthesizes statistical properties derived from current Fast Radio Burst (FRB) catalogs, encompassing dispersion measure, redshift, energy and luminosity functions, repetition rate, and host-galaxy demographics.
- Examines statistics of energy, fluence, flux, and waiting time for repeating FRBs, discussing time-series analyses that reveal memory effects and chaotic dynamics.
- Surveys the observational discovery and theoretical interpretation of periodic activity and polarization properties probing magneto-ionic environments.

## Limitations

Future work requires addressing observational biases, selection effects, and unresolved emission mechanisms for FRBs.

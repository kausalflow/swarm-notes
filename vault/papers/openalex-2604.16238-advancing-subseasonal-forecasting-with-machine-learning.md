# Advancing subseasonal forecasting with machine learning

**Authors**: Hannah Guan, Soukayna Mouatadid, Paulo Orenstein, Judah L. Cohen, Haiyu Dong, Zekun Ni, Jeremy D. Berman, Genevieve Flaspohler, Alex Lu, Jakob Schlör, Joshua Talib, Jonathan A. Weyn, Lester Mackey
**Date**: 2026-10-14
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2604.16238](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16238)

## Summary

The paper introduces probabilistic bias correction, a machine learning framework designed to improve subseasonal weather forecasting (2 to 6 weeks ahead) by learning to correct historical probabilistic forecasts. Applied to leading dynamical and AI models from ECMWF, the approach successfully doubles the subseasonal skill of the AI Forecasting System and outperforms competing models from international forecasting centers and global teams in the 2025 ECMWF real-time forecasting competition. These substantial skill gains enhance the prediction of extreme weather events, supporting critical applications in agriculture, energy management, and disaster preparedness.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces probabilistic bias correction, a machine learning framework that learns to correct historical probabilistic forecasts to mitigate compounding errors and systematic biases at subseasonal timescales (2–6 weeks ahead).
- Doubles the modest subseasonal skill of the ECMWF AI Forecasting System and improves the skill of the operationally debiased dynamical model for over 90% of pressure, temperature, and precipitation targets.
- Achieves first place in the 2025 ECMWF real-time forecasting competition, outperforming dynamical models from six operational centres, multimodel ensembles, and 34 international team systems across all weather variables and lead times.

# The Role of Deep Mesoscale Eddies in Ensemble Forecast Performance

**Authors**: Justin Cooke, Kathleen Donohue, Clark Rowley, Prasad G. Thoppil, D. Randolph Watts
**Date**: 2026-09-02
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2511.09747](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.09747)

## Summary

This paper investigates the role of deep mesoscale eddies and full water column circulation on ensemble forecast performance in the Gulf of Mexico during the Loop Current Eddy Thor separation event. By contrasting best and worst ensemble members across two 92-day forecasts, the authors show that initial conditions in the deep ocean significantly influence surface field evolution. The findings highlight the necessity of assimilating deep ocean observations to constrain initial fields and enhance forecast accuracy.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrated the importance of initial deep ocean features and full water column circulation on ensemble forecast evolution in the Gulf of Mexico during the Loop Current Eddy Thor separation event.
- Identified and contrasted best and worst ensemble members across two 92-day forecasts to show how deep ocean features and initial field alignment impact predictions.
- Motivated the assimilation of deep ocean observations to better constrain deep initial fields and improve both surface and sub-surface predictions.

# Human population dynamics as a Bayesian inverse transport problem

**Authors**: Chong Qi
**Date**: 2026-10-20
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2607.13171](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13171)

## Summary

This paper introduces a unified Bayesian inverse transport framework that embeds Bayesian Neural Networks within exact partial differential equations in age-time space to model non-equilibrium transport processes while satisfying mass conservation laws. Evaluated on human cohort advection across China, Japan, and South Korea, the method enables consistent uncertainty propagation and missing-data reconstruction from sparse observations. This approach establishes a generalizable foundation for observing and forecasting boundary dynamics across biological, physical, and engineered systems.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a unified Bayesian inverse transport framework embedding Bayesian Neural Networks within exact partial differential equations in age-time space to preserve mass conservation.
- Demonstrates consistent uncertainty propagation and missing-data reconstruction on human cohort advection datasets across China, Japan, and South Korea.
- Establishes a principled data-driven and physics-informed foundation for observing and forecasting non-equilibrium boundary dynamics.

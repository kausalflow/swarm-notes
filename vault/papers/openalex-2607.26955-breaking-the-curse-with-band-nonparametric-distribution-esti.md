# Breaking the Curse with BAND: Nonparametric Distribution Estimation in High Dimensions

**Authors**: Shuo-Chieh Huang, Chien-Ming Chi, Jau‐er Chen
**Date**: 2026-07-29
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2607.26955](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26955)

## Summary

This paper introduces Bayesian Network Distribution regression (BAND), a sparsity-aware conditional mean method designed to overcome the curse of dimensionality in multivariate distribution estimation. By leveraging sparse Bayesian networks, BAND handles mixed data types in high-dimensional time series, attaining polynomial total variation convergence rates where classical multivariate histogram estimators fail. Empirical evaluations confirm its strong performance on data sampling and confidence region forecasting compared to state-of-the-art benchmarks.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes Bayesian Network Distribution regression (BAND), a sparsity-aware conditional mean approach for high-dimensional nonparametric distribution estimation.
- Achieves polynomial total variation convergence rates in high dimensions where the feature dimension grows polynomially with sample size.
- Demonstrates competitive performance in data sampling and confidence region forecasting against state-of-the-art benchmarks.

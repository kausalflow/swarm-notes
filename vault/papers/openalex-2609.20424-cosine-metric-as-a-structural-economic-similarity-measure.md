# Cosine metric as a structural economic similarity measure

**Authors**: Marina Telezhkina, Dominik Wied
**Date**: 2026-09-17
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.20424](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20424)

## Summary

This paper introduces the cosine of the angle between linear model coefficient vectors as a geometric measure of structural economic similarity, capturing how similarly economic mechanisms respond to common shocks. The authors develop a corresponding inference procedure and apply it to New Jersey's wage mechanism during the 1992 minimum-wage reform, finding that the underlying mechanism linking worker characteristics to wages remained unchanged by the reform.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces the cosine of the angle between linear model coefficient vectors as a structural economic similarity measure
- Develops a statistical inference procedure for the cosine similarity measure of economic mechanisms
- Demonstrates the empirical applicability of the approach by analyzing New Jersey's wage mechanism around the 1992 minimum-wage reform

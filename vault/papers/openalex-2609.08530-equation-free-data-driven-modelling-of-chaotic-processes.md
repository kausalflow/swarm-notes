# Equation free data-driven modelling of chaotic processes

**Authors**: Aurora Poggi, Marco Martens, Hjalmar Brismar, Ozan Öktem, Liviana Palmisano
**Date**: 2026-09-08
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.08530](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08530)

## Summary

The paper introduces an equation-free, data-driven modeling framework for constructing predictive models of non-cyclic physical processes from time-series observations. By defining a discrete evolution rule from observations, the approach captures recurrent dynamics and models them as probabilistic Markov chains across multiple geometric scales. Furthermore, hyperbolicity criteria are leveraged to ensure that these Markov models yield consistent statistical descriptions of chaotic processes, as demonstrated on a biological Cell Process imaging dataset.

## Key Contributions

- Introduced an equation-free, data-driven modeling method for non-cyclic physical processes directly from time-series observations.
- Constructed discrete evolution rules capturing essential dynamics whose recurrent behavior is modeled as probabilistic Markov chains across multiple geometric scales.
- Employed hyperbolicity criteria to establish conditions under which the resulting Markov models provide consistent statistical descriptions of chaotic processes.
- Illustrated the framework's effectiveness through the analysis of biological imaging data from a Cell Process dataset.

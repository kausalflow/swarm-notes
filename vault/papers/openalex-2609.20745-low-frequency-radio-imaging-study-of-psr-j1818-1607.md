# Low Frequency Radio Imaging Study of PSR J1818-1607

**Authors**: A. Yadav, M. P. Surnis, B. C. Joshi, M. Bagchi, None
**Date**: 2026-09-17
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.20745](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20745)

## Summary

This paper presents a low-frequency radio imaging and timing study of the magnetar PSR J1818-1607 using observations from the upgraded Giant Metrewave Radio Telescope (uGMRT) at Band 3 and Band 4. The authors identify the continuum source associated with the magnetar and detect significant flux density variations alongside spectral index changes, revealing two potential radio flaring episodes. Additionally, they examine multiple archival radio imaging surveys, reporting non-detections of the continuum source and diffuse emission in those datasets.

## Key Contributions

- Conducted low-frequency radio observations of magnetar PSR J1818-1607 using the upgraded Giant Metrewave Radio Telescope (uGMRT) across Band 3 (300-500 MHz) and Band 4 (550-750 MHz).
- Identified the continuum source associated with the magnetar and documented variations in flux density and spectral index over time.
- Discovered two potential radio flaring episodes with distinct spectral properties from the flux density time series analysis.
- Analyzed archival radio imaging surveys, reporting non-detections of the continuum source and no direct indication of associated diffuse emission.

## Summary

We present a detailed abundance tomography analysis of SN 2024pxl, an intermediate-luminosity Type Iax supernova observed over its first 100 days using synthetic radiative-transfer spectra generated with TARDIS. Our findings show that while pure deflagration models reproduce the observed spectral evolution well, modifications to the inner ejecta density and outer chemical profiles are necessary. This analysis bridges the extremes of the Type Iax subclass, supporting the hypothesis that all SNe Iax share a common progenitor and explosion origin.

## Key Contributions

- Performed abundance tomography analysis of the intermediate-luminosity Type Iax supernova SN 2024pxl using TARDIS synthetic spectra over its first 100 days.
- Demonstrated that pure deflagration models fit the observed spectral evolution well when modified in the inner ejecta density and outer chemical profiles.
- Provided evidence that intermediate-luminosity Type Iax SNe form a continuous bridge across the subclass, supporting a unified progenitor and explosion origin for all SNe Iax.

# Earth and space observations meet complex algebras: from complex to octonions for multivariate autoregressive time series analysis

**Authors**: S. Eyheramendy, Felipe Elorrieta, Wilfredo Palma, Javier Lopatin
**Date**: 2026-09-16
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.18794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18794)

## Summary

This paper introduces a discrete-time framework for analyzing irregularly observed multivariate time series using hypercomplex autoregressive processes. By embedding multiple variables and observation gaps into a hypercomplex algebraic structure, the model is formulated as a state-space system that enables parameter estimation and recursive prediction via Kalman filtering. Evaluations on remote sensing and astronomical datasets show that the approach successfully captures complex cross-variable interactions and dynamics under nonuniform sampling intervals.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a discrete-time framework based on hypercomplex autoregressive processes to handle irregularly observed multivariate time series without requiring continuous-time assumptions.
- Embeds multivariate observations and irregular observation gaps into a hypercomplex algebraic structure represented as a state-space system.
- Enables robust parameter estimation, recursive estimation, and forecasting using Kalman filtering techniques for non-uniformly spaced data.
- Demonstrates effectiveness on remote sensing and astronomical datasets characterized by variable observation gaps.

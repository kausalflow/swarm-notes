# An extended red wing of the 5797 Å diffuse interstellar band in the Red Rectangle nebula

**Authors**: A. Ebenbichler, A. N. Witt, Hans Van Winckel, N. Przybilla, P. Moraga Baez, J. Cami, E. Peeters, A. Candian, I. Argyriou, L. B. F. M. Waters
**Date**: 2026-10-26
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.34978](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34978)

## Summary

This paper reports the detection of an extended tail towards the red (ETR) in the 5797 Å diffuse interstellar band (DIB) along the sight line of the Red Rectangle nebula, marking only the second known sight line globally to exhibit this phenomenon. Using archival GIRAFFE/FLAMES integral field spectroscopy, the authors analyse spatial variations and rule out instrumental or stellar contamination. They observe that the 5780 Å DIB does not share this ETR feature and record an unprecedentedly high equivalent width ratio EW(5797)/EW(5780) of 1.79, providing crucial new observational constraints for identifying DIB carriers.

## Key Contributions

- Reported the first detection of an extended tail towards the red (ETR) for the 5797 Å diffuse interstellar band (DIB) outside of the Herschel 36 sight line, specifically in the Red Rectangle nebula.
- Demonstrated that while the 5797 Å DIB exhibits an ETR in the Red Rectangle nebula, the nearby 5780 Å DIB does not.
- Found an exceptionally high equivalent width ratio of EW(5797)/EW(5780) = 1.79, exceeding any previously reported literature value.

# Accurately modeling long-term storage with minimum representative hours in large-scale renewable energy systems

**Authors**: Jacob Mannhardt, Lukas Kunz, Giovanni Sansavini, Jacob Mannhardt, Lukas Kunz, Giovanni Sansavini
**Date**: 2026-10-13
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2512.00892](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00892)

## Summary

Energy system optimization frequently relies on time series aggregation to achieve computational tractability, though traditional approaches struggle with accurate long-term storage modeling under representative hours (RH). This paper introduces a novel storage representation method that bridges the accuracy of RH time series aggregation with the computational efficiency of representative days. Benchmarked on a net-zero European energy system model, the proposed approach reduces solving time by over 95% while maintaining objective value accuracy, particularly under strong aggregations of 100 to 500 representative hours per year.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a novel storage representation method based on representative hours (RH) that combines high accuracy with high computational efficiency in energy system optimization.
- Reduces solving time by over 95% for the same objective value compared to established representative days (RD) and RH methods on a net-zero European energy system model.
- Demonstrates particular effectiveness under strong aggregations of 100 to 500 representative hours per year for large-scale and sector-coupled transition pathway models.

# A Functional Central Limit Theorem for Locally Stationary Time Series in Banach Spaces

**Authors**: Florian Heinrichs, Luis-Alberto Rodríguez
**Date**: 2026-09-22
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.25791](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25791)

## Summary

This paper establishes a functional central limit theorem (FCLT) for locally stationary time series taking values in separable Banach spaces, such as $C([0,1])$ and $L^p([0,1])$, without imposing restrictive type-2 or cotype assumptions. Under physical dependence and bracketing entropy conditions, the partial sum process converges weakly to a $B$-valued Gaussian process characterized via an integral of local long-run covariances and a cylindrical Brownian motion representation. As an application, the authors construct a self-normalized CUSUM test for mean changes in functional data, supported by Monte Carlo simulations and empirical analyses of EEG and temperature curves.

## Key Contributions

- Establishes a functional central limit theorem for locally stationary time series in separable Banach spaces without requiring type-2 or cotype assumptions.
- Derives a stochastic integral representation of the limiting Gaussian process with respect to a cylindrical Brownian motion.
- Develops a self-normalized CUSUM procedure for change-point detection in the mean of Banach-valued observations with a pivotal asymptotic null distribution.

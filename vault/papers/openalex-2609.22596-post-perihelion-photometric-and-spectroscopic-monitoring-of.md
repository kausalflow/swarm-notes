# Post-perihelion Photometric and Spectroscopic Monitoring of the Interstellar Comet 3I/ATLAS

**Authors**: Goldy Ahuja, Shashikiran Ganesh, K. Aravind, Emmanuel Jehin, Bhavya Ailawadhi, Vikrant K. Agnihotri, B. Arvind, Pallavi Saraf, Alka Singh, Deekshya Roy Sarkar, Sunil Chandra, Prithish Halder, Devendra Sahu, T. Sivarani, Anil Bhardwaj
**Date**: 2026-09-23
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.22596](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22596)

## Summary

This paper presents post-perihelion photometric and spectroscopic monitoring of the third interstellar object, comet 3I/ATLAS, using various Indian telescopes. The spectroscopic analysis reveals molecular emission signatures of CN, C2, and C3, indicating a compositional shift from carbon-depleted pre-perihelion to carbon-typical post-perihelion behavior. Broadband photometric measurements show that the object's colors are redder than typical solar system long-period comets, matching ultrared-material rich bodies, while its nuclear radius is estimated to be between 0.45 and 0.65 km.

## Key Contributions

- Conducted post-perihelion photometric and spectroscopic monitoring of the third interstellar object, comet 3I/ATLAS, using multiple Indian telescopes.
- Identified molecular emission signatures (CN, C2, C3) and revealed a compositional transition from carbon-depleted pre-perihelion to carbon-typical post-perihelion.
- Determined broadband colors showing the object is redder than typical long-period comets and similar to ultrared-material rich objects in the solar system.
- Derived a nuclear radius estimate between 0.45 and 0.65 km based on non-gravitational forces.

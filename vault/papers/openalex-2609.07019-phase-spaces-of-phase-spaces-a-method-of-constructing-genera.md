# Phase spaces of phase spaces: A method of constructing generalizable and topologically informed bases from time series demonstrated by reconstruction of the geometry of the macroeconomy

**Authors**: Maximilian Topel
**Date**: 2026-09-07
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.07019](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07019)

## Summary

The paper proposes a framework called the phase space of phase spaces (PSoPS) that combines Takens' Delay Embedding Theorem, dimensionality reduction, and optimal transport to extract an interpretable, low-dimensional, and dynamically informed basis from arbitrary time series. Applied to a macroeconomic corpus of 281,536 series, PSoPS reveals a connected object of effective dimension 7.3 into which canonical laws embed as low-dimensional sub-attractors, recovering monetary transmission purely from relational geometry.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a framework (PSoPS) combining Takens' Delay Embedding Theorem, dimensionality reduction, and optimal transport to extract an interpretable, low-dimensional basis from arbitrary time series.
- Reconstructs the relational geometry of the macroeconomy using 281,536 time series, demonstrating an effective dimension of 7.3 +/- 0.2 where canonical economic laws embed as low-dimensional sub-attractors.
- Recovers directed monetary transmission and macroeconomic coupling purely from geometric barycenters and transport plans without a priori structural economic models.

## Limitations

Demonstrated primarily on macroeconomic data; future extensions needed for other scientific domains sharing attractor geometries.

# A Uniform Census of Variable Stars in M101 Based on HST/ACS Photometry

**Authors**: Jiyu Wang, Xiaodian Chen, Pinjian Chen, Jianxing Zhang, Shu Wang, Licai Deng
**Date**: 2026-10-09
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.05825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05825)

## Summary

This paper presents a uniform census of variable stars in the M101 galaxy using HST/ACS F555W and F814W time-series photometry. Through rigorous Lomb-Scargle period searches, Fourier light-curve modeling, and mode-aware Cepheid refinement, the authors identify 1417 variable-star candidates, including 1180 secure classical Cepheids (420 newly discovered). The released catalog and light curves provide a valuable resource for studies of stellar variability and cosmic distance scales.

## Key Contributions

- Presented a homogeneous Hubble Space Telescope/Advanced Camera for Surveys time-series census of variable stars in two M101 disk fields using uniform multiband reduction.
- Identified 1417 variable-star candidates, comprising 1102 fundamental-mode and 78 secure first-overtone classical Cepheids, of which 420 are newly discovered relative to previous catalogs.
- Released a public catalog and source-level two-band light curves to facilitate investigations into Cepheid physics and distance-scale applications.

# Transverse impulse response of finite and infinite Rayleigh beams

**Authors**: Saranya Biswas, Anindya Chatterjee
**Date**: 2026-10-14
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.19986](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19986)

## Summary

This paper analytically studies the transverse impulse response of undamped finite and infinite Rayleigh beams under a concentrated impulse. The authors derive short-time asymptotics for finite beams via modal expansion and exact spectral integrals involving modified Bessel functions for infinite beams using Laplace transforms. They analyze finite length consequences, including exponential components in power series, smooth precursors tied to maximum group velocity, and slope discontinuities from high-frequency group velocity limits.

## Key Contributions

- Derived a short-time asymptotic series for the struck point of a simply supported finite Rayleigh beam under a concentrated transverse impulse using modal expansion.
- Obtained the infinite beam impulse response as a spectral integral and evaluated it exactly using Laplace transforms in terms of modified Bessel functions.
- Demonstrated three distinct consequences of finite beam length: exponentially decaying length components in power series coefficients, a smooth precursor deviation governed by the greatest group velocity, and a slope discontinuity corresponding to the high-frequency limit of group velocity.

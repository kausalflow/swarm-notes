# Multi-Source Dynamic Graph Learning for Compound-Flood Forecasting in Managed Coastal Systems

**Authors**: Liangjun You, Min Wu, Orlando Woods, Dongsheng Luo
**Date**: 2026-08-03
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.01775](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01775)

## Summary

Compound flooding in managed coastal systems is difficult to forecast because single-site records fail to capture high-water dynamics distributed across heterogeneous gages, yet unconstrained cross-site fusion degrades local temporal forecast stability. To address this, the authors propose an anchored forecasting framework that uses state- and lead-dependent bounded residual corrections to incorporate cross-site information without destabilizing local forecasts. A multi-source regime representation adaptively calibrates inter-site relationships and correction scales, improving the prediction of sustained high-water plateaus while maintaining high accuracy during routine conditions.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes an anchored forecasting framework for compound-flood forecasting that uses state- and lead-dependent bounded residual corrections to integrate cross-site signals.
- Introduces a multi-source regime representation built from hydrometeorological and operational observations to adaptively calibrate inter-site relationships and correction scales.
- Improves the prediction reliability of sustained high-water plateaus in managed coastal systems while maintaining accuracy during routine hydrological conditions.

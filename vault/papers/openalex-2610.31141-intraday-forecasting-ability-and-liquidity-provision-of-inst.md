# Intraday Forecasting Ability and Liquidity Provision of Institutional Traders

**Authors**: Xinlei Hao, Jiajun Guo, Jussi Keppo, Xianhua Peng
**Date**: 2026-10-22
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.31141](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31141)

## Summary

This paper examines the relationship between forecasting ability, liquidity provision, and intraday proprietary profitability among institutional traders using high-frequency exchange data. The authors uncover persistent forecasting skills, showing that flow-following strategies yield exceptionally high annualized Sharpe ratios. Furthermore, matching buy and sell executions reveals that both improved forecasting proxies and heightened liquidity-provision intensity significantly increase realized profit and loss (P&L) per position.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates persistent intraday forecasting abilities among institutional traders using high-frequency stock exchange data, with flow-following strategies yielding annualized Sharpe ratios between 16 and 63.
- Establishes that a one-standard-deviation increase in forecasting ability corresponds to a 1.02 to 1.19 currency unit increase in realized P&L per matched position.
- Quantifies the profitability of liquidity provision within matched positions, showing P&L increases by 1.28 to 1.31 units for one passive execution and an additional 3.79 to 3.82 units for two passive executions relative to none.

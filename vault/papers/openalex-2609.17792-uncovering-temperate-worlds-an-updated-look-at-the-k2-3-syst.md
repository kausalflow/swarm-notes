## Summary

This paper presents an updated analysis of the K2-3 planetary system using 8 years of combined radial velocity data, including new ESPRESSO-GTO observations alongside archival HARPS, HARPS-N, and HIRES measurements. By applying a line-by-line spectroscopic analysis coupled with a multi-dimensional Gaussian process framework, the authors effectively disentangle stellar activity from planetary Keplerian signals. The resulting refined mass measurements for the three sub-Neptune planets improve detection significance—notably doubling it for K2-3b—and offer new insights into their internal compositions and atmospheric characterization potential with JWST.

## Key Contributions

- Combined 8-year ESPRESSO-GTO, HARPS, HARPS-N, and HIRES radial velocity dataset to update masses for the K2-3 planetary system.
- Employed a multi-dimensional Gaussian process framework combined with line-by-line spectroscopic analysis to separate stellar rotation from Keplerian signals.
- Derived precise planetary masses: Mb = 6.61 +/- 0.47 Me, Mc = 2.23 +/- 0.68 Me, and Md = 2.12 +/- 1.27 Me, doubling the detection significance for K2-3b.
- Demonstrated via forward atmospheric modeling that JWST can distinguish between H/He-rich and water-world atmospheric scenarios for the system.

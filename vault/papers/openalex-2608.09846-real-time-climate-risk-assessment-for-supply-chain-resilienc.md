# Real-Time Climate Risk Assessment for Supply Chain Resilience: A Data-Driven Nowcasting Framework for Colombian Agriculture

**Authors**: Hernan J. Silva-Sosa
**Date**: 2026-08-10
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.09846](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09846)

## Summary

This paper presents a methodological framework for real-time climate risk assessment using data-driven nowcasting techniques to enhance supply chain resilience in Colombian agriculture. The approach integrates short-term climate forecasting with supply chain risk modeling to build an early warning system architecture using official meteorological and agricultural time series. Results from experiments on historical and synthetic data show that precipitation nowcasts can be translated into actionable risk indicators for supply chain logistics.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a methodological framework for real-time climate risk assessment combining data-driven nowcasting with supply chain risk modeling for Colombian agriculture.
- Establishes a conceptual early warning system architecture integrating historical meteorological observations and agricultural time series without satellite imagery.
- Demonstrates through prototype experiments that short-term precipitation nowcasts translate into actionable risk indicators supporting inventory, sourcing, and transport decisions.

## Limitations

Evaluated using synthetic and historical data prototypes rather than large-scale live operational deployments.

# Central limit theory for serial tail dependence estimators in heavy-tailed long memory linear time series

**Authors**: Ioan Scheffel, Marco Oesting, Gilles Stupfler
**Date**: 2026-08-11
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.10944](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10944)

## Summary

This paper establishes a comprehensive central limit theory for serial tail dependence estimators in heavy-tailed linear time series exhibiting long memory. The authors develop two novel multivariate reduction principles for partial sums of heavy-tailed long memory processes subordinated over sliding windows and growing thresholds. These theorems accommodate infinite-variance innovations and hold uniformly in the threshold, thereby providing rigorous asymptotic guarantees for empirical extremograms based on sample quantiles. Additionally, the analysis uncovers distinct convergence rates and limit laws that differentiate long-range dependence from classical short-range settings.

## Key Contributions

- Proves multiple central limit theorems for serial tail dependence estimators in heavy-tailed long memory linear time series under mild assumptions including infinite variance innovations.
- Establishes two novel multivariate reduction principles for partial sums of heavy-tailed long memory linear series subordinated over sliding windows and growing thresholds.
- Derives a uniform reduction principle across varying thresholds to enable central limit theory for empirical extremograms using sample quantiles.
- Identifies markedly different convergence rates and limit laws compared to standard short-range dependence settings.

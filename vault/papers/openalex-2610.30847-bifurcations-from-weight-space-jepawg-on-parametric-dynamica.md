# Bifurcations from Weight Space: JEPAWG on Parametric Dynamical Systems

**Authors**: Tobias Göbel, Miranda C. N. Cheng
**Date**: 2026-10-22
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.30847](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30847)

## Summary

This paper evaluates weight-space analysis methods using model zoos generated by fine-tuning forecasting networks across parameter grids of four dynamical systems. The authors apply JEPAWG, a joint-embedding hypernetwork originally designed for lattice field theories, to analyse these populations of trained networks. Results demonstrate that JEPAWG not only matches direct hypernetwork forecasting performance but also enables interpretable detection of complex physical phenomena such as Hopf bifurcations, period-doubling cascades, and global bifurcations directly from the weight space.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates that fine-tuning forecasting networks on parametric dynamical systems creates model zoos that validate weight-space methods against analytical bifurcations.
- Applies JEPAWG to four dynamical systems, showing it achieves competitive forecasting performance despite being supervised on parameter-weight correspondence rather than direct forecasts.
- Shows that the norm of the parameter-derivative of decoded weights from JEPAWG successfully locates Hopf points and curves, tracks period-doubling cascades to estimate the Feigenbaum constant, and detects global bifurcations invisible to linearisation.

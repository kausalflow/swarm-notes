# Graph Representation via Elements of Discrete Morse and Cobordism Theories

**Authors**: Jennifer Rozenblit, Chenguang Yang, Yuxin Liu, Yuzhou Chen, Yulia Gel
**Date**: 2026-10-01
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.01937](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01937)

## Summary

This paper advocates for the integration of low-dimensional topology tools, specifically discrete Morse theory and cobordism theory, into machine learning frameworks. The authors introduce MG-Diff, a pipeline that incorporates these topological tools to improve graph diffusion models. Furthermore, they establish theoretical stability guarantees under small perturbations and demonstrate utility in spatio-temporal graph forecasting and graph regeneration.

## Key Contributions

- Introduced MG-Diff, a novel pipeline integrating concepts from cobordism theory and discrete Morse theory into graph diffusion models.
- Derived theoretical guarantees and sufficient conditions proving that Morse-theoretic induced diffusion guidance is stable under small perturbations given a positive decision-gap.
- Illustrated the utility of discrete Morse theory for spatio-temporal graph forecasting and graph regeneration tasks.

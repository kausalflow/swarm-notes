# The natural components of an autoregressive time series on Banach space

**Authors**: Phil Howlett, Brendan K. Beare
**Date**: 2026-09-16
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.17960](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17960)

## Summary

This paper investigates autoregressive time series defined on a Banach space, demonstrating that they can be canonically decomposed into a finite direct product of independent natural components. These components are isolated on distinct subspaces via spectral projections of the characteristic linear pencil. Depending on whether the corresponding spectral points lie outside, inside, or on the unit circle, each component evolves as a forward, backward, or outward flow determined by upstream boundary conditions in the distant past, distant future, or at a specific initial time.

## Key Contributions

- Proves that an autoregressive time series on a Banach space can be canonically decomposed into a finite direct product of self-contained natural components using spectral projections of the characteristic linear pencil.
- Establishes that each natural component corresponds to a forward, backward, or outward flow governed by boundary conditions in the distant past, distant future, or initial time depending on the location of the spectral point relative to the unit circle.
- Extends the structural and spectral analysis of autoregressive processes from finite-dimensional Euclidean spaces to infinite-dimensional Banach spaces under mild analytic conditions on the resolvent.

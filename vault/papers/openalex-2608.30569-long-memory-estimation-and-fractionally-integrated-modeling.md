# Long-Memory Estimation and Fractionally Integrated Modeling of White Maize Prices in Togo

**Authors**: Manganaw N'Daam, Edoh Katchekpele, Tchilabalo Abozou Kpanzou
**Date**: 2026-08-31
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.30569](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30569)

## Summary

This study investigates long-memory temporal persistence in logarithmic monthly white maize prices across six major markets in Togo from 2001 to 2022 using various long-memory estimators such as Local Whittle and wavelet log-regression. The authors compare SARIMA, ARFIMA, and SARFIMA models via the Bayesian Information Criterion and residual diagnostics. Their empirical findings confirm long-range dependence across all markets, while revealing that fractionally integrated models do not uniformly outperform standard models, highlighting the necessity of data-driven model selection.

## Key Contributions

- Investigates long-memory persistence in logarithmic monthly white maize prices across six major markets in Togo from January 2001 to June 2022.
- Employs a battery of long-memory estimators including Geweke-Porter-Hudak, Local Whittle, Exact Local Whittle, and wavelet log-regression.
- Compares SARIMA, ARFIMA, and SARFIMA models using Bayesian Information Criterion and residual diagnostics, demonstrating that long-memory presence does not automatically guarantee superior empirical fit for fractionally integrated models.

## Limitations

Limited to white maize prices in six markets in Togo; does not explore multivariate forecasting or machine learning time-series architectures.

## Archivist Review

No novel reusable machine learning concepts or datasets proposed; the paper investigates agricultural commodity price persistence using established econometric estimators (GPH, Local Whittle, Exact Local Whittle, wavelet log-regression) and standard models (SARIMA, ARFIMA, SARFIMA).

# Granular Framework for Construction Material Price Forecasting: Econometric and Machine-Learning Approaches

**Authors**: Boge Lyu, Qianye Yin, Iris D. Tommelein, Hanyang Liu, Karnamohit Ranka, Karthik Yeluripati, Junzhe Shi
**Date**: 2026-11-03
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2512.09360](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09360)

## Summary

This study proposes a granular forecasting framework for construction material prices using the Construction Specifications Institute (CSI) MasterFormat at the six-digit Section level. By integrating explanatory variables such as raw material prices, commodity indexes, and macroeconomic indicators, the authors evaluate five time-series models including LSTM, ARIMA, VECM, Transformer, and Chronos-Bolt. Results reveal that incorporating explanatory variables substantially enhances predictive accuracy, with the LSTM model achieving superior performance across residential material categories.

## Key Contributions

- Develops a forecasting framework leveraging the Construction Specifications Institute (CSI) MasterFormat for granular six-digit Section level predictions.
- Evaluates five diverse time-series models (LSTM, ARIMA, VECM, vanilla Transformer, and Chronos-Bolt) under baseline and explanatory-variable-extended configurations.
- Demonstrates that incorporating explanatory variables (raw material prices, commodity indexes, macroeconomic indicators) significantly improves predictive performance across all models.
- Shows that the LSTM model achieves the highest accuracy with RMSE values as low as 4.19 and MAPE of 1.60 on Division 06, outperforming traditional statistical models like ARIMA by up to 59%.

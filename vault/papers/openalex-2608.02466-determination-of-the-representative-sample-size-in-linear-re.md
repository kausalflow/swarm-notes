# Determination of the Representative Sample Size in Linear Regression

**Authors**: Anatoly Rayev, Gregory Keslin, Joseph Keslin
**Date**: 2026-08-03
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.02466](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02466)

## Summary

This paper addresses the common issue where regression analysis and forecasting accuracy degrade significantly when applied to new, homogeneous samples due to discrepancies between postulated and real models caused by series redundancy and sample noise. To mitigate this, the authors propose a principled procedure for selecting the appropriate terms of an approximating series (including nonlinear transformations of independent variables) and determining the representative sample size required for robust generalization.

## Key Contributions

- Addresses the accuracy discrepancy between training samples and out-of-sample forecasting in linear regression models caused by postulated vs. real model mismatches.
- Analyzes how series approximation redundancy and sample noise degrade out-of-sample forecasting and regression accuracy.
- Proposes a systematic procedure for selecting the optimal approximating series terms and determining the representative sample size.

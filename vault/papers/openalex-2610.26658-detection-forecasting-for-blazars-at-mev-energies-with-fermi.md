# Detection Forecasting for Blazars at MeV Energies with Fermi-LAT Swift-BAT Cross Match

**Authors**: Timothy Wessling-Resnick, Naomi Tsuji, T. Aramaki, P. Coppi, M. Errando, Yoshiyuki Inoue, Reshmi Mukherjee, Svanik Tandon, Jiancheng Zeng
**Date**: 2026-10-19
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.26658](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26658)

## Summary

This paper forecasts the detection capabilities of upcoming MeV gamma-ray instruments (such as COSI, GRAMS, newASTROGAM, and AMEGO-X) for observing blazars by leveraging cross-matched Fermi-LAT and Swift-BAT catalogs combined with spectral energy distribution modeling. The study demonstrates that instruments with sensitivities between 10^-11 and 10^-12 erg/cm^2/s can detect dozens to thousands of Flat-Spectrum Radio Quasars and BL Lacs out to a redshift of z approx 6. Furthermore, the findings indicate that these observations can shed light on neutrino-emitting candidate blazars and resolve lepto-hadronic degeneracies.

## Key Contributions

- Forecasts the observational capabilities of future MeV gamma-ray instruments (COSI, GRAMS, newASTROGAM, AMEGO-X) for detecting blazars using Fermi-LAT and Swift-BAT cross-matched catalogs.
- Estimates that an instrument with a sensitivity limit of 10^-11 erg/cm^2/s could detect 61 of 76 SED-modeled blazars, while 10^-12 erg/cm^2/s achieves full detection of all 76.
- Projects total blazar population detections up to hundreds or a thousand FSRQs and BLLs, with potential detectability out to redshift z approx 6.

# SD3: The First Operationally-Proven, Fully Heterogeneous-Accelerated Global Kilometer-Scale Model with Inline Physics Architecture

**Authors**: Yuan Liang, Xiaomin Hu, Xi Chen, Jingsheng Zhu, Li Yang, Leisheng Li, Qian Song, Xu Wang, Hao Wang, Zhenzhen Wang, Huiyuan Li
**Date**: 2026-10-29
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.39334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39334)

## Summary

The paper introduces Super Dynamics on the Cube (SD3), an operationally-proven global kilometer-scale model featuring an Inline Physics architecture implemented in native CUDA C that tightly couples its non-hydrostatic cubed-sphere dynamical core with fast physical processes. Evaluated through five years of continuous operational forecasting, SD3 achieves 205.7 simulated days per day at a global 1.5 km resolution on 24,576 GPUs. This heterogeneous-accelerated paradigm establishes a scalable blueprint for next-generation weather and climate prediction and Digital Twin Earth applications.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces Super Dynamics on the Cube (SD3), the first operationally-proven, fully heterogeneous-accelerated global kilometer-scale weather and climate model.
- Develops an Inline Physics architecture implemented via native CUDA C that deeply couples the non-hydrostatic cubed-sphere dynamical core with fast physical processes.
- Achieves an unprecedented throughput of 205.7 simulated days per day at a global 1.5 km resolution using 24,576 GPUs across continuous operational forecasting.

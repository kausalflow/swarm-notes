# Explore the ideology of deep learning in ENSO forecasts

**Authors**: Y L Gan, Yipeng Chen, Ning Li, Xingguo Liu, Junyu Dong, Xianyao Chen
**Date**: 2026-10-20
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2601.02050](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02050)

## Summary

This paper introduces a mathematically grounded interpretability framework using bounded variation functions to explain deep learning-based El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO) forecasts. By rescuing saturated neurons, the approach improves expressive capacity while confirming that model predictions align with physical oceanographic principles. Furthermore, the analysis investigates the Spring Predictability Barrier (SPB), attributing its performance drop to suboptimal variable selection and pointing toward expanded multi-basin variable integration as a solution.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a mathematically grounded interpretability framework based on bounded variation functions for deep learning-based ENSO forecasting.
- Rescues dead neurons from activation function saturation zones to enhance model expressive capacity.
- Reveals that ENSO predictability is dominated by the tropical Pacific with contributions from the Indian and Atlantic Oceans, aligning with physical principles.
- Probes the Spring Predictability Barrier (SPB) and identifies suboptimal variable selection as a primary driver of performance decline during spring.

## Limitations

The reliance on specific activation functions and bounded variation analysis may require tuning across different neural architectures.

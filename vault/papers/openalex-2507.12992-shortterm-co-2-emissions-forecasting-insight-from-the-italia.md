# Short‐Term CO 2 Emissions Forecasting: Insight From the Italian Electricity Market

**Authors**: Pierdomenico Duttilo, Francesco Lisi
**Date**: 2026-07-29
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2507.12992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12992)

## Summary

This study investigates short-term forecasting of carbon emissions from electricity generation in the Italian power market using hourly data from 2021 to 2023. The authors evaluate four major model classes—linear parametric, functional parametric, nonparametric/nonlinear (including GAM and TBATS), and semi-functional K-nearest neighbors—alongside various forecast combination methods. Results indicate that generalized additive models excel during daytime while functional parametric models lead in early morning, and simple average or selection-based combinations consistently outperform individual models.

## Key Contributions

- Evaluates and compares linear, functional, nonparametric (GAM, TBATS), and semi-functional K-nearest neighbors models for short-term CO2 emissions forecasting using hourly Italian electricity market data from 2021 to 2023.
- Demonstrates that generalized additive models (GAM) perform best during daytime hours, while functional parametric models excel in the early morning.
- Shows that simple averaging and selection-based forecast combination strategies consistently outperform all individual forecasting models.
- Highlights the importance of zonal specificities and temporal variations in low-carbon emissions across Italian electricity market zones.

# A hybrid proper orthogonal decomposition and diffusion framework for reduced-order forecasting of turbulent flow dynamics

**Authors**: Rodrigo Abadía-Heredia, Xiangrui Zou, Manuel López‐Martín, Petros Koumoutsakos, Soledad Le Clainche
**Date**: 2026-08-05
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.04728](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04728)

## Summary

This paper presents a hybrid reduced-order generative forecasting framework that combines proper orthogonal decomposition (POD) with Generative Learning of Effective Dynamics (G-LED) to model turbulent flow dynamics. By performing temporal prediction in a physics-based modal space and using diffusion-based reconstruction, the approach achieves substantial computational speedups over full-field methods while preserving dominant wake organization. Evaluations on experimental measurements of a circular cylinder turbulent wake demonstrate that global and localized POD-G-LED effectively balance predictive fidelity and efficiency.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a hybrid reduced-order generative forecasting framework combining proper orthogonal decomposition (POD) with Generative Learning of Effective Dynamics (G-LED) for turbulent flow dynamics.
- Compares three configurations (full-field G-LED, global POD-G-LED, and localized POD-G-LED) on experimental measurements of a circular cylinder turbulent wake.
- Demonstrates that global and localized POD-G-LED significantly reduce training and inference computational costs compared to full-field G-LED while preserving dominant wake organization and coherent energetic structures.

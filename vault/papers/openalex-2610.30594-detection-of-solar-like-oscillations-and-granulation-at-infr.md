# Detection of Solar-like Oscillations and Granulation at Infrared Wavelengths using JWST

**Authors**: Angharad Weeks, Daniel Huber, T. R. Bedding, Jared E.J. Splinter, H. Kjeldsen, Yaguang Li, Courtney Crawford, Daniel Hey, David Lafreniére, Louis-Philippe Coulombe, Nicolas Cowan
**Date**: 2026-10-22
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.30594](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30594)

## Summary

The paper presents the first detection of stellar oscillations and granulation at infrared wavelengths using JWST time-series observations of the F6V star WASP-121. By analyzing these infrared observations, the authors find that the amplitudes of oscillations and granulation decrease with wavelength in a manner that deviates from simple blackbody scaling. This discovery enables stringent tests of 3D convection models and refines the modeling of stellar noise in exoplanet transit observations.

## Key Contributions

- First detection of stellar oscillations and granulation at infrared wavelengths using JWST time-series observations of the F6V main-sequence star WASP-121.
- Demonstrated that oscillation and granulation amplitudes decrease with wavelength but deviate from simple blackbody scaling.
- Enabled detailed empirical tests of 3D stellar convection models and improved combined modeling of stellar and exoplanet signals in JWST passbands.

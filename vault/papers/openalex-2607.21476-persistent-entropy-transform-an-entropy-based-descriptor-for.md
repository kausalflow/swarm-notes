# Persistent Entropy Transform: An entropy-based descriptor for topological data analysis

**Authors**: Víctor Toscano-Durán, Miguel A. Gutiérrez Naranjo, Rocio Gonzalez-Diaz
**Date**: 2026-07-23
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2607.21476](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21476)

## Summary

The paper introduces the Persistent Entropy Transform (PET), a novel directional topological descriptor that compresses directional topological transforms using persistent entropy to combine geometric expressiveness with computational tractability. The authors prove key theoretical properties including translation invariance, uniform scale invariance, and orthogonal equivariance. Empirical evaluations on synthetic shapes demonstrate directional sensitivity and robustness, while experiments on the TwoLead-ECG and MIT-BIH time-series datasets establish PET as an effective compact feature vector.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces the Persistent Entropy Transform (PET), an entropy-based directional topological descriptor that bridges scalar summaries and directional transforms.
- Proves theoretical properties of PET including translation invariance, positive scale invariance, and orthogonal equivariance.
- Demonstrates the effectiveness of PET embeddings as compact feature vectors on real time-series benchmark datasets (TwoLead-ECG and MIT-BIH).

## Limitations

Evaluated primarily as a proof of concept on synthetic shapes and two time-series benchmarks; broader scale evaluations remain for future work.

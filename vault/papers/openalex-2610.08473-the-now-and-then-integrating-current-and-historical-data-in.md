# The Now and Then: Integrating Current and Historical Data in Small Multiple Time Series Visualization

**Authors**: Sydney K Purdue, Enrico Bertini, Melanie Tory
**Date**: 2026-10-06
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.08473](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08473)

## Summary

Small multiple time series visualizations are widely used for real-time monitoring in high-impact domains like healthcare and manufacturing, requiring users to quickly appraise current values alongside historical trends. This paper introduces a comprehensive design space for glanceable time series small multiples that emphasizes current values within historical data. Through two empirical studies, the authors evaluate these designs on rapid appraisal tasks, finding that spatially integrated representations—such as size encodings for non-threshold tasks and color thresholds for threshold tasks—significantly improve response times and monitoring efficiency.

## Key Contributions

- Presents a novel design space for glanceable time series small multiple displays that visually emphasize current values in historical time series.
- Evaluates the design space through two online empirical studies testing against non-threshold and threshold rapid appraisal tasks.
- Demonstrates that spatially integrated designs outperform separated designs, with size encodings on current values providing a 28% response time improvement for non-threshold tasks.
- Shows that color threshold encodings significantly outperform shaded band encodings for threshold-based monitoring tasks.

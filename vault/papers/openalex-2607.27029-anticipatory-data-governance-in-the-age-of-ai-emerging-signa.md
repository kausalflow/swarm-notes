# Anticipatory Data Governance in the Age of AI: Emerging Signals in Data Access, Reuse, and Sovereignty

**Authors**: Adam Zable, Stefaan Verhulst
**Date**: 2026-07-29
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2607.27029](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27029)

## Summary

This paper reports findings from a participatory foresight study examining emerging signals in data access, reuse, and sovereignty in the age of AI. Through expert forecasting studios and signal-scanning methodology, the authors identify seven convergent structural developments, such as AI-mediated data ecosystems and the fragmentation of governance across jurisdictions. The study highlights reinforcing feedback loops among these shifts and outlines an agenda for anticipatory data governance.

## Key Contributions

- Conducted a structured participatory foresight study comprising two expert forecasting studios with nineteen senior practitioners to examine emerging data governance developments.
- Identified seven convergent signals of structural shifts in data access, reuse, and sovereignty, including the strain on open-data paradigms and the AI-mediated nature of modern data ecosystems.
- Mapped reinforcing feedback loops coupling these signals to highlight how governance interventions propagate risks and opportunities across institutional and jurisdictional boundaries.

## Limitations

The contribution is diagnostic rather than predictive, offering an evidence-informed framework for reasoning about structural shifts rather than specific forecasts of technological outcomes.

**Authors**: Sena Ozgunay, Louise Travé-Massuyès, Jean-Michel Loubes, Raul Sena Ferreira
**Date**: 2026-08-10
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.09246](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09246)

## Summary

Industrial processes generate multivariate time series from interacting sensors, where detecting and diagnosing anomalies is critical for reliability. Existing GNN methods typically attribute faults directly to individual deviating sensors, ignoring that anomalies often stem from disruptions in inter-sensor influences. To address this, the authors propose an explainable GNN-based anomaly detection framework for component-level anomaly diagnosis that treats anomalous measurements as symptoms of altered system dynamics. Experiments show the method successfully identifies and prioritizes true faulty components with interpretable insights.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes an explainable GNN-based anomaly detection and diagnosis framework shifting focus from sensor-level deviations to component-level system dynamics.
- Hypothesizes that anomalous measurements are symptoms of altered inter-sensor influences rather than isolated sensor failures.

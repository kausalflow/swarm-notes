# Data-Driven Reduced-Complexity Modeling of Fluid Flows: A Community Challenge

**Authors**: Oliver T. Schmidt, Aaron Towne, Adrián Lozano-Duran, Scott T. M. Dawson, Ricardo Vinuesa
**Date**: 2026-10-14
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2601.06183](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06183)

## Summary

This paper introduces a community challenge designed to systematically evaluate and compare data-driven methods for compression, forecasting, and sensing of complex aerospace flows. Structured across three complementary tracks, the challenge provides standardized success metrics, evaluation tools, and baseline implementations to ensure fair comparisons on diverse flow datasets. By employing blind tests on withheld data and explicitly encouraging the analysis of limitations, the initiative aims to build a comprehensive picture of current algorithmic capabilities and shortcomings in fluid flow modeling.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a community challenge framework for comparing data-driven reduced-complexity modeling methods across compression, forecasting, and sensing tracks for complex aerospace flows.
- Provides standardized success metrics, evaluation tools, and baseline implementations (including classical and machine-learning baselines) for fair comparison.
- Implements blind tests on withheld data to rigorously assess generalization and explicitly encourages negative results and limitations analysis.

## Limitations

Relies on community participation and the scope of provided aerospace flow datasets and tracks.

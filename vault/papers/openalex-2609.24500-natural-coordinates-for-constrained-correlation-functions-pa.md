# Natural coordinates for constrained correlation functions: Partial autocorrelations and the geometry of positive power spectra

**Authors**: Thomas Erben
**Date**: 2026-09-21
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.24500](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24500)

## Summary

This paper establishes a formal equivalence between the affine variables governing constrained two-point correlation functions and partial autocorrelation coefficients of positive Toeplitz correlation matrices. By framing the geometry of positive power spectra through partial autocorrelations, the authors introduce $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ recursions for admissible intervals and provide a statistical interpretation for quasi-Gaussian likelihood transformations. Numerical experiments demonstrate the practical utility of these natural coordinates in one-dimensional settings.

## Key Contributions

- Identified the affine variable in constrained correlation function geometry with the partial autocorrelation coefficient of the associated positive Toeplitz correlation matrix.
- Established an O(N^2) recursion to compute admissible correlation intervals at arbitrary order using partial autocorrelation coordinates.
- Provided a classical statistical interpretation of the quasi-Gaussian likelihood transformation as Fisher's z-transformation of partial autocorrelations.

## Limitations

Higher-dimensional isotropic constraints require additional geometric input and lie outside the direct scope of this identification.

# Disrupted Companionship: A Risk Assessment Framework and Cross-Platform Quantitative Analysis of Psychosocial Responses to AI Companion Disruptions

**Authors**: Chau Do, Yunhao Yuan, Koustuv Saha, Renwen Zhang, Talayeh Aledavood
**Date**: 2026-09-15
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.16907](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16907)

## Summary

This paper investigates AI companion disruptions—platform-initiated changes that alter or terminate ongoing user relationships with AI companions—by compiling 30 disruption events, establishing a taxonomy of disruption types and a four-dimensional risk-assessment framework, and analyzing longitudinal Reddit data via a hierarchical Bayesian interrupted time-series model. The findings reveal that disruption onsets immediately trigger surges in anxiety, stress, suicidal expression, and grief activation, particularly when relational discontinuity and transition-support deficits are high.

## Key Contributions

- Compiled 30 disruption events across major AI companion platforms and developed a taxonomy of six disruption types alongside three broad disruption reasons.
- Proposed a prospective risk-assessment framework comprising four dimensions: relational discontinuity, population vulnerability, communication deficit, and transition-support deficit.
- Estimated community-level psychosocial responses using a longitudinal Reddit dataset and a hierarchical Bayesian interrupted time-series model with predictive controls, finding immediate increases in anxiety, stress, suicidal expression, and grief activation following disruption onset.

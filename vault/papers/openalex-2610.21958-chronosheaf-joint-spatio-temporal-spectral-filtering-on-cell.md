# ChronoSheaf: Joint Spatio-Temporal Spectral Filtering on Cellular Sheaves

**Authors**: Abeer Mostafa, Zahra Ahmadi
**Date**: 2026-10-15
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.21958](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21958)

## Summary

ChronoSheaf extends cellular sheaf neural networks to the spatio-temporal domain by introducing a joint spectral sheaf Laplacian, Transitional Restriction Maps (TRM) for asymmetric temporal influence, and a Dual Spectral Filter (DSF) to expand receptive fields without enlarging underlying sheaf structures. This design effectively captures signal evolution over time while mitigating oversmoothing, outperforming existing state-of-the-art baselines on spatio-temporal forecasting benchmarks.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a spectral sheaf Laplacian defined jointly over space and time to regulate signal propagation across both dimensions.
- Introduces Transitional Restriction Maps (TRM) to decompose restriction maps into spatial and temporal frames for asymmetric temporal influence.
- Develops a Dual Spectral Filter (DSF) using a bounded-degree polynomial filter with an adaptively estimated spectral radius to expand the effective receptive field.
- Demonstrates state-of-the-art performance on diverse spatio-temporal forecasting benchmarks.

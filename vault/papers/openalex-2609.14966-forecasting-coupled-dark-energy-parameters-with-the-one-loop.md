# Forecasting Coupled Dark Energy Parameters with the One-Loop Galaxy Power Spectrum

**Authors**: Bilal Tüdes, Luca Amendola
**Date**: 2026-09-14
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.14966](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14966)

## Summary

This paper forecasts constraints on coupled dark energy models using DESI and Euclid data combined with the one-loop galaxy power spectrum. The authors investigate model distinguishability from a zero-coupling baseline under varying maximum wavenumbers, potential slope parameters, and priors. The findings indicate that including mildly non-linear scales improves coupling constraints by a factor of five, and conclude that only time-varying couplings are detectable using late-time datasets.

## Key Contributions

- Forecasts parameter constraints on coupled dark energy models using DESI and Euclid data with the one-loop galaxy power spectrum.
- Demonstrates that the inclusion of mildly non-linear scales up to k_max = 0.3 h/Mpc improves coupling constraints by roughly a factor of five.
- Shows that only time-varying coupled dark energy models can be detected using late-time cosmological datasets.

## Limitations

Evaluated using late-time datasets which restricts detectability to time-varying coupling models.

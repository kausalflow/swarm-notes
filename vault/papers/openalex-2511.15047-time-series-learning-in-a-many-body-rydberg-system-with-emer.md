# Time series learning in a many-body Rydberg system with emergent collective amplification

**Authors**: Zongkai Liu, Qiming Ren, Chris Nill, Albert Cabot, Wei Xia, Yanjie Tong, Huizhen Wang, Wenguang Yang, Junyao Xie, Mingyong Jing, Hao Zhang, Liantuan Xiao, Suotang Jia, Igor Lesanovsky, Linjie Zhang
**Date**: 2026-08-24
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2511.15047](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15047)

## Summary

This paper explores time series forecasting using an interacting many-body Rydberg atomic vapour as a physical computing medium. By modulating the laser Rabi frequency near a non-equilibrium phase transition, the system leverages emergent collective amplification to enhance its learning and prediction capabilities. Evaluations on the Lorenz time series and temperature data demonstrate improved forecasting accuracy compared to standard regimes, highlighting the potential of non-equilibrium many-body quantum systems for data processing.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates time series prediction using an interacting Rydberg atomic vapour driven by a modulated laser field.
- Shows that proximity to a non-equilibrium phase transition enhances collective amplification and improves forecasting accuracy.
- Validates the approach using the Lorenz time series and real-world temperature data.

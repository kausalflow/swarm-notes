## Summary

We present Intern-S2-Preview, a series of scientific agentic foundation models designed for multimodal scientific understanding, reasoning, tool interaction, and long-horizon tasks. Trained via multimodal pre-training followed by a unified post-training pipeline of supervised fine-tuning, multi-task RL, agentic RL, and on-policy distillation, the flagship Intern-S2-Preview-397B incorporates time series modeling and an independent Memory Decoder for rapid specialization. Evaluations demonstrate strong performance across scientific, multimodal, and agentic benchmarks, including specialized gains on biology and time series forecasting tasks.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces Intern-S2-Preview-397B, a scientific agentic foundation model capable of multimodal understanding, numerical forecasting, and long-horizon tool use.
- Employs a unified post-training pipeline featuring multi-task RL, black- and white-box agentic RL, on-policy distillation, and techniques like partial rollout with off-policy correction and online speculative decoding.
- Proposes Memory Decoder (Intern-MemDec-4B) as a memory-augmented extension path for rapid scientific specialization without modifying the frozen 397B backbone.
- Achieves competitive or leading performance across multimodal, scientific, and agentic benchmarks, including an improvement on Biology-Instructions via the memory decoder.

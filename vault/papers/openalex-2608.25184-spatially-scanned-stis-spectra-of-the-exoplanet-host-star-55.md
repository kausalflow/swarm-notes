# Spatially Scanned STIS Spectra of the Exoplanet Host Star 55 Cnc

**Authors**: D. E. Welty, J. D. Lothringer, D. K. Sing, A. M. Jones, A. Riley, C. R. Proffitt
**Date**: 2026-08-25
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.25184](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25184)

## Summary

This paper analyzes optical and near-infrared spectra of the exoplanet host star 55 Cnc obtained via spatial scanning mode with the Space Telescope Imaging Spectrograph (STIS) and grating G750L. Using custom reduction procedures for cosmic ray removal and fringe correction, the authors achieve a white-light flux precision of 30-40 ppm, comparable to the best HST time series photometry. The findings indicate that STIS spatial scanning provides high-quality spectra for bright exoplanet hosts, successfully capturing the transit depth of 55 Cnc e alongside an unexpected increase in transit radius between 0.55 and 1.0 micron.

## Key Contributions

- Analyzed optical/near-IR spectra of exoplanet host star 55 Cnc obtained using STIS spatial scanning mode with grating G750L
- Demonstrated scatter of approximately 30-40 ppm for total white-light fluxes using custom cosmic ray and fringe removal procedures
- Retrieved transit depth of the super-Earth 55 Cnc e consistent with previous values and identified an unexpected increase in transit radius between 0.55 and 1.0 micron

## Limitations

Limited dataset size with slight orbit-to-orbit offsets and larger scatter in narrower wavelength bins

# Interval-valued circular time series: A cylindrical score-driven approach with an application to wind direction

**Authors**: Thasmika Mohan, Najmeh Nakhaei Rad, Sphiwe Skhosana, Yaser Samadi
**Date**: 2026-11-02
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2611.02874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02874)

## Summary

This paper proposes a cylindrical score-driven framework for interval-valued circular time series by representing intervals via a circular centre and a bounded linear width. By developing dynamic conditional score recursions for the centre and range processes, the approach jointly captures circular-linear dependence within a likelihood-based setting using newly introduced cylindrical distributions. Empirical evaluation on wind direction data from South African and Brazilian wind farms demonstrates the method's effectiveness for directional dispersion monitoring.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a cylindrical score-driven framework for interval-valued circular time series using a centre-range representation.
- Develops dynamic conditional score recursions for joint modelling of circular centre and bounded linear range processes.
- Introduces various cylindrical distributions to appropriately accommodate bounded range values within a likelihood-based framework.
- Demonstrates practical utility and performance on interval-valued wind direction datasets from South African and Brazilian wind farms.

# Phase Space Reorganization and Travelling Wave Emergence Driven by Non-Kerr Effects in Nonparaxial Optical Media

**Authors**: Naresh Saha, Nirmoy Kumar Das, Ashoke Das, Arnob Ray
**Date**: 2026-08-13
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.12856](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12856)

## Summary

This paper investigates the nonlinear Helmholtz equation incorporating non-Kerr nonlinearities like self steepening and self frequency shift in nonparaxial optical media. By applying a travelling wave transformation, the governing equation is reduced to a Hamiltonian dynamical system, enabling equilibrium classification, phase space analysis, and exact wave solution derivation. Under periodic forcing, the system transitions from regular dynamics to quasiperiodic and chaotic behavior, verified through largest Lyapunov exponents and Poincaré sections.

## Key Contributions

- Reduced the nonlinear Helmholtz equation with non-Kerr effects into a Hamiltonian dynamical system via travelling wave transformation.
- Demonstrated that self steepening directly modifies reduced dynamics while self frequency shift governs the compatibility condition for real travelling wave reduction.
- Established the emergence of quasiperiodic and chaotic responses under external forcing using bifurcation analysis, largest Lyapunov exponents, and Poincaré sections.

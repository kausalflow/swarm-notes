# A Physics-Constrained Implicit Profile Network for Continuous Reconstruction of Tropical Cyclone Near-Surface Wind Profiles

**Authors**: Jian Ma, Yilin Yang, Robert Rogers, Jun A. Zhang, Jie Tang
**Date**: 2026-10-08
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.11405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11405)

## Summary

This paper proposes a physics-constrained implicit profile network to continuously reconstruct tropical cyclone near-surface wind profiles from sparse, irregular aircraft dropsondes. The model effectively integrates physical principles with machine learning to preserve surface wind observations while capturing realistic vertical wind speed and direction variations. Evaluated on more than twenty years of NOAA hurricane data, the approach accurately reproduces vertical wind structures across diverse storm intensities and extends satellite surface wind data into three-dimensional near-surface fields.

## Key Contributions

- Developed a physics-constrained implicit profile network to reconstruct continuous tropical cyclone near-surface wind profiles from sparse dropsonde observations
- Preserves observed surface winds while generating realistic vertical changes in wind speed and direction
- Evaluated using over two decades of NOAA hurricane observations across a wide range of storm intensities

# BLG723.31.34651 as an eclipsing Roche lobe-filling sdOB+WD binary

**Authors**: Alekzander Kosakowski, Charles E. Derbyshire, Sydney Jetton, Brad N. Barlow
**Date**: 2026-08-24
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.23459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23459)

## Summary

The paper presents an observational analysis of the binary star system BLG723.31.34651, identified as the fourth reported eclipsing member of Roche lobe-filling hot subdwarfs. Utilizing time series spectroscopy and multi-band photometry from the SOAR telescope, the authors find periodic variability at P = 64 min and deep eclipses caused by an opaque accretion disc. Light curve modeling and spectroscopic analysis reveal phase-dependent variations in atmospheric parameters due to tidal deformation and disc flux contributions.

## Key Contributions

- Presented a detailed observational analysis of BLG723.31.34651 using time series spectroscopy and multi-band photometric observations from the Goodman High Throughput Spectrograph on the 4.1-m SOAR telescope.
- Identified periodic photometric variability at P = 64 min from the tidally-deformed hot subdwarf donor and deep eclipses by an opaque accretion disc.
- Provided two light curve model solutions agreeing to within 2sigma, indicating a hot subdwarf donor, white dwarf accretor, and irradiated opaque accretion disc.
- Identified statistically significant phase-dependent variations in effective temperature and surface gravity via spectroscopic analysis.

## Limitations

Future work would benefit from higher-resolution spectroscopy and refined modeling to fully account for unmodeled spectroscopic flux contributions from the accretion disc and tidal deformation parameters.

# Bridging Artificial Intelligence and Power Systems Education Using a Hands-On Executable Framework

**Authors**: Junjie Yin, Buxin She, Xinyu Feng, Fangxing Li
**Date**: 2026-08-03
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2608.02599](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02599)

## Summary

This paper introduces an engineering-grounded AI (EGAI) framework and an open, executable module library designed to bridge artificial intelligence and power systems education. Built on Jupyter notebooks following a progressive difficulty ladder, the framework covers foundational neural networks, CNN-based power-flow surrogates, optimization, deep reinforcement learning, and physics-informed neural networks. Deployed via an IEEE online course and PES webinar series, the approach successfully lowers entry barriers for interdisciplinary learners in power systems.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces engineering-grounded AI (EGAI) to align AI workflows with power-system domain rules rather than generic black-box modeling.
- Releases an open, executable module library with a progressive difficulty ladder mapping AI concepts to representative power tasks.
- Covers foundational DNN templates, CNN power-flow surrogates, DNN-assisted optimization, DRL for battery storage, and PINNs for the swing equation via Jupyter notebooks.

## Limitations

Focuses primarily on educational deployment and introductory-to-intermediate tasks, leaving large-scale transmission-grid operational scaling for future work.

# Geometry-Agnostic Data-Driven Discovery of Extreme Scenarios

**Authors**: Angeline Hu, Ava K. Tse, Kaitlyn S. Yasumura, Luciana Sarabia, Malcolm Yeung, Kavin K. Prasanna, Kai Wong, William Hammond, Ioannis G. Keverkidis, Shengkai Li, Trung V. Phan
**Date**: 2026-10-20
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.28487](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28487)

## Summary

The paper introduces a two-stage data-driven neural framework for discovering extreme scenarios and coherent structures without prior knowledge of governing equations. The first stage infers effective dynamical constraints in a geometry-agnostic form directly from observational data, while the second stage uses physics-informed neural-search to uncover rare, high-impact structures. Demonstrations across representative systems illustrate the identification of homoclinic pulses, Townes solitons, and instantons.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a two-stage data-driven neural framework for discovering dynamically admissible extreme scenarios without prior knowledge of governing equations.
- Learns effective dynamical constraints directly from observational data using a geometry-agnostic form independent of fixed grids, coordinate systems, dimensionality, and intrinsic curvature.
- Employs a physics-informed neural-search in the second stage to uncover rare, high-impact coherent structures not present in the original observations.
- Demonstrates applicability across diverse systems and structures including homoclinic pulses, Townes solitons, and instantons on varying manifolds.

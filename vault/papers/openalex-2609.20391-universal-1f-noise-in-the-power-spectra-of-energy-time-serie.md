# Universal 1/f Noise in the Power Spectra of Energy Time-series in Solvated DNA Dynamics

**Authors**: Harsh Sahu, Deepika Sardana, Pramod Kumar, Ajay Kumar Chand, Sobhan Sen, Sanjay Puri
**Date**: 2026-09-17
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2609.20391](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20391)

## Summary

This paper investigates the energy fluctuations of solvated DNA across three distinct conformations (G-quadruplex, Hairpin, and Duplex) using molecular dynamics simulations. The authors discover that the time-series of interaction energies—covering DNA self-energies, DNA-water, and DNA-ion interactions—universally exhibit 1/f noise with a decay exponent of approximately 0.90. Furthermore, the results indicate that DNA topology and structural rigidity modulate the dynamic coupling with hydration shells and mobile counter-ions, causing pronounced low-frequency modes in flexible structures that are absent in rigid duplexes.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates via molecular dynamics simulations that energy fluctuation time-series of solvated DNA across different conformations consistently exhibit 1/f noise with a universal decay exponent β ≈ 0.90.
- Reveals that DNA topology and structural rigidity (comparing G-quadruplex, Hairpin, and Duplex) modulate dynamic coupling with surrounding hydration shells and counter-ions.
- Shows that flexible DNA structures display pronounced low-frequency modes in self-energy spectra that are absent in rigid duplex structures.

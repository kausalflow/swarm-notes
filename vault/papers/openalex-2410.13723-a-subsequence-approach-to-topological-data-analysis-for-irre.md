# A Subsequence approach to topological data analysis for irregularly-spaced time series

**Authors**: Sixtus Dakurah, Jessi Cisewski-Kehe
**Date**: 2026-10-22
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2410.13723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13723)

## Summary

This paper introduces a novel subsequence embedding method to enable topological data analysis (TDA) on irregularly-spaced time-series data. Grounded in time-delay embedding principles, the proposed approach preserves the underlying state-space topology while minimizing spurious homological features. The authors establish theoretical stability and convergence properties under noise and irregular sampling, supported by numerical experiments and real-data applications.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a novel subsequence embedding method for applying topological data analysis to irregularly-spaced time series.
- Establishes theoretical stability results and convergence properties under noise and varying levels of observation irregularity.
- Demonstrates that the proposed method preserves original state space topology while reducing spurious homological features.
- Illustrates performance through numerical studies and real-world data applications.

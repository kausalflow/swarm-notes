# EPRV asteroseismology of the K dwarfs GJ 570 A and GJ 783 A with ESPRESSO. Empirical mode-amplitude scaling in tension with 3D convection simulations

**Authors**: T. L. Campante, Y. Zhou, Y. Li, H. Kjeldsen, R. R. Costa, A. W. Neitzel, Jorge H. C. Martins, A. M. Silva, V. Adibekyan, J. Gomes da Silva, J. R. Larsen, S. G. Sousa, T.R. Bedding, D. Bossini, W. J. Chaplin, E. Corsaro, M. S. Cunha, M. Deal, D. Huber, M. N. Lund, M. S. Lundkvist, T. S. Metcalfe, M.J.P.F.G. Monteiro, N. C. Santos
**Date**: 2026-10-21
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.20985](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20985)

## Summary

This paper investigates solar-like oscillations in mid-K dwarfs using high-cadence ESPRESSO radial-velocity measurements to perform Doppler asteroseismology on GJ 570 A and GJ 783 A. The authors reveal a steep transition in mode-amplitude scaling with stellar parameters near the GK-dwarf boundary that conflicts with predictions from 3D radiative-hydrodynamical convection simulations. This discrepancy exposes missing physics in convection-pulsation coupling models for low-mass stars.

## Key Contributions

- Utilized high-cadence ESPRESSO radial-velocity time series to perform Doppler asteroseismology on the mid-K dwarfs GJ 570 A and GJ 783 A.
- Extracted peak mode amplitudes, identifying 6 modes for GJ 570 A and 24 modes for GJ 783 A.
- Demonstrated a transition from (L/M)^0.7 to a steeper (L/M)^1.5 mode-amplitude scaling near the GK-dwarf boundary, revealing significant tension with 3D radiative-hydrodynamical convection simulations predicting (L/M)^0.9 scaling.

## Limitations

The discrepancy points to missing physics in convection-pulsation coupling implementations and limitations in current modeling assumptions, requiring further theoretical work.

### 1. Dynamics, ODEs, and Continuous-Time Modeling
Traditional discrete-time sequence models often falter when handling irregular sampling, non-stationary behaviors, or chaotic error propagation. Recent works offer elegant solutions bridging physical systems with deep learning:
* **Continuous-Time Integration & Attention:** To handle asynchronous exogenous variables without rigid temporal alignment, **CTIA-ODE** embeds a *Continuous-Time Inter-Variable Attention* mechanism directly into an ODE solver, paired with a dual-stabilization strategy (Linear Residual Augmentation and Outlier-Robust Hybrid Loss) to anchor long-term trajectories.
* **Refining Neural Operators:** Recognizing that deterministic PDE surrogates trained via Mean Squared Error (MSE) tend to smooth out high-frequency details, **ReAl** introduces a model-agnostic plug-in combining residual conditional flow matching with trajectory-level reward alignment. This restores fine-scale structures while preserving long-horizon rollout stability.
* **Tipping Point & Chaos Forecasting:** Dealing with abrupt state transitions, a Recurrent Neural Operator framework combined with conformal physics-informed prediction enables zero-shot forecasting of abrupt tipping points in infinite-dimensional function spaces (**2308.08794**). Meanwhile, for chaotic systems, partial multistep prediction strategies (omitting intermediate steps) successfully extend the positive-$R^2$ horizon and recover underlying topological scroll dynamics even when precise geometric localization fails (**2610.20093**).

### 2. Bayesian Inference, Uncertainty, and Structural Discovery
Capturing epistemic uncertainty and recovering true interaction graphs remains central to physical and biological time-series applications:
* **Stochastic System Identifiability:** Fully Bayesian ensemble-based inference frameworks are proving essential for recovering complex interaction structures. By combining an Onsager-Machlup quasi-potential SDE likelihood with a non-parametric Minimum Description Length prior, researchers show that propagating structural uncertainty via ensemble posteriors drastically improves perturbation prediction robustness over point estimates (**2610.19744**).
* **Graph-Enhanced Clinical EHRs:** In healthcare applications, graph-enhanced frameworks utilizing longitudinal electronic health records (MIMIC-IV) and branching motifs help discover disease trajectories and forecast temporal endpoints (**2610.20502**, **2610.20512**). However, a recurring nuance is that while these motifs provide structured long-term timing support, they require careful semantic prioritization to avoid degrading short-term hazard assessments.

### 3. Foundation Models, Agentic Deployment, and Retrieval
As time-series and specialized foundation models proliferate, the community is heavily focused on making them practically adaptable without costly manual re-engineering:
* **Self-Improving Agents:** Frameworks like **TIMERSI** wrap frozen time-series foundation models in coding-agent loops, dynamically translating task descriptions and error feedback into executable pipeline improvements. Similarly, **CoPE** co-evolves agentic policy acting and forecasting via multi-step action targets to improve reinforcement learning sample efficiency.
* **Retrieval over Retraining:** To mitigate OOD performance drops in anomaly detection and model selection, retrieval-based frameworks like **RAMSAD** query historical performance profiles via similarity search, matching full-ensemble performance at a fraction of compute costs (**2610.20243**).

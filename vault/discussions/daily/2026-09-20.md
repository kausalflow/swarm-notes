### 2. Generalization, Normalization, and Representation in Time Series
Handling domain shift and heterogeneous sampling rates remains a central bottleneck in applying deep learning to real-world signals.
* **Predictive Maintenance:** **FreqCondNorm** (2609.20535) addresses this head-on by introducing a FiLM-style frequency-conditioned normalization layer for Transformer foundation models. By unifying heterogeneous signals spanning five orders of magnitude (1 Hz to ~100 kHz) through masked auto-encoding and contrastive pretraining, it achieves exceptional zero-shot transfer across disparate industrial machinery datasets (though RUL tasks still face objective mismatches).
* **Neuroimaging:** In fMRI, sequence timing effects in segmented 3D acquisitions have often distorted spatio-temporal properties. A formal framework modeling these acquisitions as spatio-temporal filters reveals how high-frequency BOLD signals are disproportionately attenuated, providing a crucial lens for mitigating physiological noise in volumetric neuroimaging (2609.20105).

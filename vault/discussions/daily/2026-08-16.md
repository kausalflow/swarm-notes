### 1. Architectural & Training Paradigms for Time Series Foundation Models
The conversation around time-series foundation models is moving past simple sequence modeling to address data distribution control.

### 2. Statistical Rigor and the Mechanics of Time
As models grow more complex, theoretical guarantees and statistical identifiability are experiencing a resurgence to prevent spurious pattern matching.
* **The Arrow of Time (`2608.13431`)**: Establishes a formal mathematical framework for measuring directional structure in multivariate time series using a *lagged circulation matrix*. Crucially, the author proves that contemporaneous covariance is blind to time direction, providing a rigorous alternative (complete with bias-removed estimators and delete-block jackknives) to correlation networks and naive Granger causality.
* **Nonstationary Change Point Detection (`2608.13352`)**: Introduces **BARBS** (bootstrap-assisted robust binary segmentation), solving Type I error control under complex temporal dependencies via a novel Gaussian multiplier bootstrap for CUSUM statistics. 

### 3. Agentic Automation and High-Stakes Decision Making
Autonomous systems are increasingly operating in volatile, adversarial environments (e.g., finance and clinical interventions) where simulation safety and temporal leakage control are paramount.

By treating measured-grid intervention paths as predictive targets, PML successfully forecasts high-dose semantic outcomes using only low-dose causal responses.
